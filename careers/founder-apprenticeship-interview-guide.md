## Interview Structure

### Total Time: 60-90 minutes

| Phase | Duration | Focus |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Opening & Rapport | 5 min | Warm-up, set context |
| Vibe Coding Deep-Dive | 20 min | Technical capability |
| Marketing Instinct | 15 min | Strategic thinking |
| Speed & Execution | 10 min | Working style |
| Culture & Growth Fit | 15 min | Mindset alignment |
| Their Questions | 10 min | Curiosity signal |
| Close | 5 min | Next steps |

---

## Phase 1: Opening (5 min)

**Set the tone:**
> "This isn't a traditional interview. I want to understand how you think, how you build, and whether we'd thrive working together at high velocity."

**Opener question:**
- "Before we dive in—what made you stop scrolling and actually apply for this?"

*Listen for: Genuine connection to the opportunity, not generic job-seeking.*

---

## Phase 2: Vibe Coding Deep-Dive (20 min)

### Core Questions

**1. Show me your process**
> "Walk me through the last thing you built with AI tools—from idea to shipped. I want to see your screen if possible, or describe it in detail."

*Listen for:*
- Speed of iteration
- Tool fluency (Claude, Cursor, Replit, v0, etc.)
- Problem-solving when stuck
- Quality bar they set

**2. Tool mastery**
> "What's your AI tool stack right now? And what did you stop using—why?"

*Listen for:*
- Experimentation mindset
- Staying current
- Opinions and preferences (not just following trends)

**3. Limits of AI**
> "Tell me about a time AI couldn't solve what you needed. What did you do?"

*Listen for:*
- Resourcefulness
- Willingness to learn
- Not over-reliant on AI

**4. Live challenge (optional but powerful)**
> "Here's a simple product idea: [give them one]. How would you approach building a working prototype? Talk me through your first 2 hours."

Example prompts:
- "A landing page that captures emails for a new meditation app"
- "A simple tool that takes a URL and summarizes the page"
- "A waitlist page with a referral counter"

*Listen for:*
- Clarity of thinking
- Speed of decision-making
- Practical approach vs. over-engineering

## Phase 3: Marketing Instinct (15 min)

### Core Questions

**1. Product positioning**
> "Look at this [show a Mindvalley product/page]. In 60 seconds, tell me what's working and what you'd change."

*Listen for:*
- Quick pattern recognition
- Specific, actionable feedback
- Understanding of audience psychology

**2. Launch thinking**
> "You just built a new product with me. We have 48 hours to get our first 100 users. What do you do?"

*Listen for:*
- Scrappy vs. theoretical thinking
- Channel knowledge
- Speed orientation

**3. Messaging instinct**
> "Give me 3 different headlines for this product: [describe something simple]. Don't overthink—go."

*Listen for:*
- Creative fluency
- Understanding of hooks
- Willingness to riff quickly

**4. Past marketing wins**
> "What's the best marketing result you've ever achieved? What made it work?"

*Listen for:*
- Metrics awareness
- Attribution understanding
- Pride in craft

## Phase 4: Speed & Execution (10 min)

### Core Questions

**1. Pace check**
> "In your current/last role, how long did it typically take to go from idea to shipped product?"

*Listen for: Days/weeks is good. Months is a red flag.*

**2. Perfectionism test**
> "Tell me about something you shipped that you weren't 100% happy with. Why did you ship it anyway?"

*Listen for:*
- "Done is better than perfect" mindset
- Iteration over perfection
- Pragmatic decision-making

**3. Autonomy**
> "Describe a time you had to figure something out completely on your own with zero guidance."

*Listen for:*
- Self-direction
- Resourcefulness
- Comfort with ambiguity

**4. Feedback response**
> "Tell me about harsh feedback you received. What happened?"

*Listen for:*
- Ego management
- Growth orientation
- No defensiveness

## Phase 5: Culture & Growth Fit (15 min)

### Core Questions

**1. Growth orientation**
> "What are you actively trying to get better at right now—outside of work?"

*Listen for:*
- Self-improvement mindset
- Specific practices
- Alignment with Mindvalley values

**2. Intensity tolerance**
> "This role is intense. Bi-weekly in-person sessions, rapid execution between. How do you sustain high output?"

*Listen for:*
- Self-awareness about energy
- Sustainable practices
- Not naive about demands

**3. Mentorship fit**
> "What do you want from a mentor? What kind of feedback style works for you?"

*Listen for:*
- Coachability signals
- Direct feedback appetite
- Growth hunger

**4. Ambition check**
> "Where do you see yourself in 3 years? Be honest, not polished."

*Listen for:*
- Founder/CEO aspirations
- Aligned ambition
- Not just looking for a job

**5. Values alignment**
> "What does personal growth mean to you? How do you practice it?"

*Listen for:*
- Genuine connection to transformation
- Not just performative answers
- Depth of reflection

## Phase 6: Their Questions (10 min)

**Pay attention to what they ask.** Great candidates ask about:
- The work itself
- Your working style
- Growth trajectory
- Current projects/ventures
- What success looks like

**Red flag questions:**
- Only about compensation/benefits
- Work-life balance concerns (signals wrong fit)
- Reporting structure/hierarchy
- Nothing at all

---

## Phase 7: Close (5 min)

**If strong candidate:**
> "I enjoyed this. Here's what happens next: [explain process]. You'll hear from me within [timeframe]."

**If unsure:**
> "Thanks for your time. We're speaking with a few candidates and will be in touch within [timeframe]."

**If clear no:**
> "I appreciate you taking the time. I don't think this is the right fit for what we're building, but I wish you the best."

## Red Flags Checklist

### Immediate Disqualifiers
- [ ] No Loom video submitted
- [ ] Can't show AI-built work
- [ ] Not based in London
- [ ] Needs significant structure/hand-holding
- [ ] Defensive about feedback
- [ ] Talks about ideas but no shipped products

### Yellow Flags (Probe Deeper)
- [ ] Only theoretical marketing knowledge
- [ ] Projects took months, not days
- [ ] Over-reliance on teams
- [ ] Perfectionism signals
- [ ] Vague about tools used
- [ ] No questions about the work itself

## Quick Reference: What You're Really Looking For

### The Ideal Candidate:
1. **Ships fast** - Days, not weeks
2. **AI-native** - Tools are second nature
3. **Marketing brain** - Knows what resonates
4. **Hungry** - Wants transformation, not a job
5. **Coachable** - Welcomes direct feedback
6. **Self-directed** - Figures it out
7. **Growth-oriented** - Practices what Mindvalley teaches

### The One Question That Matters:
> "Can this person sit with me for 2 hours, understand the vision, and return with something real before the momentum fades?"

If yes → hire them.
If no → keep looking.
