The Three Problems Sparks Solve

🚪

Single Program Mentality

Members buy one Quest, complete it (or don't), and never explore the other 109 programs. They don't see the library as valuable.

Spark Solution:

Monthly Sparks encourage trying new programs. "I have 500 Sparks — let me try Silva Mind Control."

🧘

Hidden Meditation Library

1,000+ meditations sit unused. Most members don't know it exists or see value in a separate $99 subscription.

Spark Solution:

Meditation included in all tiers. Daily meditation earns Sparks — creating a habit loop.

🏔️

Mastery Price Barrier

$5,000 for Mastery is intimidating. Summit attendees get excited but can't commit to such a large purchase.

Spark Solution:

Accumulate Sparks toward Mastery over time. "I'm 60% there" creates commitment.
