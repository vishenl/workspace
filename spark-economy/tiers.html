Why Each Tier Works

Explorer ($49)

Target: Single-program buyers, price-sensitive newcomers

  • Lower than single program cost ($199-499)
  • Meditation Library inclusion drives daily engagement
  • Sparks accumulate toward first program purchase

Seeker ($99)

Target: Engaged learners, multi-program curious

  • 2 Pillars = $10,000+ in content value
  • 1.25x earning multiplier accelerates progress
  • Most members will graduate here from Explorer

Transformer ($149)

Target: Serious practitioners, Mastery-aspirational

  • 4 Pillars = most transformation needs covered
  • 1.5x multiplier + 20% Mastery discount
  • Path to Mastery becomes ~8 months

Visionary ($249)

Target: Power users, transformation enthusiasts

  • All-access unlimited content
  • 2x earning = fastest path to premium
  • 30% Mastery discount = $3,500 vs $5,000

Luminary ($499)

Target: VIPs, high-net-worth, ambassadors

  • Maximum earning potential (2.5x) — reaches Mastery in ~4 months
  • 40% off Mastery/Certifications — Mastery becomes $3,000
  • Exclusive access to Vishen, special events
  • White-glove concierge experience
