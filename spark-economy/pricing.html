Credit Pricing Model

Complete Spark pricing for all Mindvalley products with psychology and price anchoring.

Pricing Philosophy

Core Principles

  • Value Anchoring: Show "retail value" vs. Spark cost to emphasize savings
  • Psychological Decoupling: Sparks feel like "play money" — easier to spend
  • Accessibility Ladder: Price premium products to be achievable through engagement
  • Demand-Based Variation: Popular items cost more Sparks (like ClassPass)

Conversion Baseline

100 Sparks = $10

But members acquire Sparks at ~$8-9 effective cost through subscriptions

= 10-20% perceived savings on every redemption

Accessibility Analysis

At Transformer tier (1,800 Sparks/month + 1.5x earning):

  • Monthly subscription Sparks: 1,800
  • Monthly earned Sparks (active user): ~1,200 × 1.5 = 1,800
  • Total monthly: ~3,600 Sparks
  • Time to Mastery (28,000): ~8 months

This makes Mastery feel achievable through commitment, not just wealth.
