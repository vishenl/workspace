Why Retention Improves

1. Sunk Cost

Accumulated Sparks create switching cost — canceling feels like "wasting" investment.

2. Loss Aversion

Canceling = losing Sparks. Loss is 2x more painful than equivalent gain.

3. Progress Investment

"I'm 70% to Mastery" creates commitment to see it through.

4. Daily Habit

Meditation earning creates daily touchpoints with the platform.

5. Status

Tier status becomes part of identity — losing it feels like a "breakup".

6. Community

Deeper engagement with other members through challenges and leaderboards.

Cashflow Impact

Positive Cashflow Drivers

1. Annual Prepayment

Annual subscribers pay upfront for 12 months of Sparks, improving cash position.

2. Spark Pack Purchases

Cash received immediately, but redemption is deferred — positive working capital.

3. Breakage

Revenue recognized without redemption cost — pure margin improvement.

4. Reduced Churn

Lower customer acquisition cost burden as retention improves.

Risks & Mitigations

Risk Likelihood Impact Mitigation
Devaluation backlash Medium High Grandfather existing members; 90-day notice on changes; maintain core value
Gaming/fraud Medium Medium Rate limits; fraud detection; community reporting; IP tracking
Complexity confusion Medium Medium Simple tier names; excellent onboarding; in-app guidance

Breakage too high Low Medium Proactive reminders; generous rollover; easy redemption
Member rebellion Low High Soft launch; feedback loops; rapid iteration; pause option

Grandfathering Strategy

For current members:

  • Auto-convert to equivalent tier based on current subscription
  • Award "Founding Member" badge (permanent status symbol)
  • 3,000 bonus Sparks for transition
  • 6-month price lock guarantee
  • Opt-in migration (not forced)
