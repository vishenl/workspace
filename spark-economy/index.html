Executive Summary

The Core Idea

Sparks are a universal credit currency that psychologically decouples purchases from dollar pricing. Members earn Sparks through engagement (meditation, completions, referrals) and spend them on programs, events, Mastery, and certifications — creating a virtuous cycle of engagement and investment.

🎯

Problem: Single Program Mentality

Members buy one program and never explore the 110+ others available.

Solution: Sparks encourage discovery by making additional content feel "already paid for" within tiers.

🧘

Problem: Hidden Meditation Library

1,000+ meditations exist but members don't realize the value.

Solution: Daily meditation earns 10 Sparks, creating habit and surfacing the library.

💰

Problem: $5,000 Mastery Barrier

High-ticket Mastery programs feel out of reach for most members.

Solution: Earn Sparks over time — Mastery becomes achievable through commitment (~8-10 months).
