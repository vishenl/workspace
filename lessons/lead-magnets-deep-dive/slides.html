Part 1

Audience Research

Know your audience so well they think you're reading their mind

Why Research Matters

Skip this step and your lead magnet will feel generic.
Do it right and your copy practically writes itself.

The goal: When they read your landing page, they think "This was written for ME."

What You Need to Discover

  • Pain Points: What keeps them awake at night?
  • Desires: What transformation do they dream about?
  • Objections: Why haven't they solved this yet?
  • Language: What exact words do they use?
  • Failed Solutions: What have they already tried?

4 Research Methods

1

Direct Research

Talk to real customers. Even 5-10 conversations transform your understanding.

2

Review Mining

Amazon, Udemy, Reddit. Your competitors' customers already told you what they want.

3

AI-Assisted

Use AI to synthesize and expand. Works best when you feed it real data first.

4

Competitive Analysis

See what's working for others. Not to copy—to understand what resonates.

Customer Interview Questions

  • Before: "What was happening when you decided to seek help?"
  • Trigger: "What was the final straw?"
  • Failed attempts: "What had you already tried?"
  • The search: "How did you look for solutions?"
  • Hesitations: "What almost stopped you?"
  • In their words: "How would you describe this to a friend?"
Pro Tip

Record these conversations

The exact phrases they use become your copy.

Where to Mine Reviews

Amazon Reviews

Books in your niche. Read 3-star and 5-star reviews.

Course Platforms

Udemy, Skillshare. What do students praise and complain about?

Reddit & Forums

Look for "help me" and "finally figured out" posts.

YouTube Comments

Questions, frustrations, and "aha" moments.

Language Patterns to Find

Pain Language

"I was struggling with..."
"I couldn't figure out..."
"I was frustrated by..."

Desire Language

"I wanted to..."
"I wished I could..."
"My dream was..."

Objection Language

"I was worried that..."
"I almost didn't because..."

Transformation Language

"Now I..."
"Finally I..."
"This changed..."

Lead Magnet Planning

Choose the right type and create something irresistible

The Value Equation

People give you their email when:

Perceived Value > Perceived Cost

Your job: Maximize perceived value. Minimize perceived cost.

The 3 Psychological Triggers

1

Curiosity

"I need to know..."
Quizzes excel here

2

Quick Win

"I can solve this today"
Templates, checklists

3

Transformation

"This will change my life"
Guides, courses

The best lead magnets trigger all three. The minimum? At least one.

Landing Page Design

The anatomy of a high-converting opt-in page

Landing Page Anatomy

1

Headline

Clear, specific, outcome-focused. Not clever—clear.

2

Subheadline

Expands on headline. Adds credibility.

3

Visual

Mockup of lead magnet or transformation imagery

4

Bullet Points

3-5 specific benefits (not features)

5

Form + CTA

Part 4

The Elegant CTA

Build relationships, not just email lists

Pushy vs. Elegant

Don't Do This

  • "Get my FREE guide NOW!"
  • "Limited time! Act fast!"
  • Pop-ups in 2 seconds
  • Fake countdown timers

Do This

  • "Join 50,000+ who..."
  • "Get the guide that helped..."
  • Value-first, CTA at natural pauses
  • Authentic scarcity only

Trust-First CTA Formula

  1. Lead with value — Give something useful before asking
  2. Be specific — "Get the 5-step guide" beats "Download now"
  3. Add social proof — "Join 50,000+ entrepreneurs"
  4. Reduce risk — "No spam. Unsubscribe anytime."

CTA Button Copy

  • Quiz: "Discover Your [Result]" / "Take the 2-Minute Assessment"
  • eBook: "Get the Free Guide" / "Send Me the eBook"
  • Mini-Course: "Start Free Course" / "Begin Day 1"
  • Webinar: "Save My Seat" / "Register Free"

Avoid: "Submit" (homework), "Sign Up" (generic), "Click Here" (no value)
