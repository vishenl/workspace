Decision Helper: Which Stack?

Based on your budget and needs, here are recommended tool combinations.

💰 Budget Stack ($0-50/mo)
  • Quiz: Typeform Free or ScoreApp Free
  • Landing Page: Carrd ($9/yr) or ConvertKit Free
  • Email: ConvertKit Free or Mailchimp Free
  • eBook: Canva Free

Total: ~$10-30/mo

🚀 Growth Stack ($100-200/mo)
  • Quiz: Interact ($39/mo)
  • Landing Page: Leadpages ($37/mo)
  • Email: ConvertKit Creator ($29/mo)
  • eBook: Canva Pro ($13/mo)

Total: ~$118/mo

⚡ Pro Stack ($200-400/mo)
  • Quiz: Interact Pro ($89/mo)
  • Landing Page: Unbounce ($99/mo)
  • Email: ActiveCampaign ($79/mo)
  • eBook: Canva Pro ($13/mo)

Total: ~$280/mo

🛠️ DIY/Custom Stack
  • Quiz: Lovable + Supabase
  • Landing Page: Lovable
  • Email: ConvertKit or Beehiiv
  • eBook: Canva

Total: ~$30-60/mo + more control
