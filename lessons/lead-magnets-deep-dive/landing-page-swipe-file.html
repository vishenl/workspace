Headline Formulas

Proven formulas that work. Fill in the blanks for your niche.

1. The Outcome + Without Pain Formula
Get [DESIRED OUTCOME] Without [PAIN POINT/OBSTACLE]
Example: "Get a Flat Stomach Without Giving Up Your Favorite Foods"
Example: "Build a 6-Figure Business Without Quitting Your Day Job"
2. The X-Step System Formula
The [NUMBER]-Step System to [OUTCOME] in [TIMEFRAME]
Example: "The 5-Step System to Double Your Productivity in 30 Days"
Example: "The 3-Step System to Land Your Dream Clients This Month"
3. The Discovery Quiz Formula
What's Your [TOPIC] [TYPE]? Take the 2-Minute Quiz
Example: "What's Your Spiritual Archetype? Take the 2-Minute Quiz"
Example: "What's Your Productivity Style? Take the 2-Minute Assessment"
4. The Social Proof Formula
Join [NUMBER]+ [PEOPLE] Who Have [ACHIEVED RESULT]
Example: "Join 50,000+ Entrepreneurs Who Have Launched Profitable Side Businesses"
Example: "Join 135,000+ Students Who Have Transformed Their Stress Response"
5. The Secret/Hidden Formula
The [ADJECTIVE] [THING] That [OUTCOME] (And Why [EXPERTS] Don't Tell You)
Example: "The Simple Mindset Shift That Doubles Your Income (And Why Most Gurus Miss It)"
Example: "The Hidden Technique That Eliminates Anxiety in Minutes"
6. The Curiosity Gap Formula
Why [SURPRISING FACT]—And What to Do About It
Example: "Why 90% of Diets Fail in the First Month—And What to Do Instead"
Example: "Why Meditation Doesn't Work for Most People—And the Simple Fix"

Bullet Point Templates

Use 3-5 bullets to quickly communicate value. Each bullet = one benefit.

The "You'll Discover" Pattern
Swipe Copy
Inside this free guide, you'll discover:

• The [adjective] [technique] that [result] (page [X])
• Why [common belief] is keeping you stuck — and what to do instead
• The #1 mistake [target audience] make with [topic] (and how to avoid it)
• A simple [X]-minute exercise that [immediate benefit]
• How to [desired outcome] even if [objection]

Pro tip: Use specific numbers ("page 7", "in 3 minutes", "the 4-step process") to increase credibility. Vague claims feel like fluff.

CTA Button Copy

What goes on the button matters. Be specific about what happens when they click.

Lead Magnet Type Button Copy Options
eBook "Get the Free Guide" / "Download Now" / "Send Me the eBook"
Quiz "Take the Quiz" / "Discover Your Type" / "Start the Assessment"
Mini-Course "Start Free Course" / "Begin Day 1" / "Enroll Free"
Webinar "Save My Seat" / "Register Free" / "Join the Masterclass"
Checklist "Get the Checklist" / "Download Free" / "Send It To Me"
Template "Get the Template" / "Grab Your Copy" / "Download Template"

Avoid: "Submit" (feels like homework), "Sign Up" (too generic), "Click Here" (says nothing about value)
