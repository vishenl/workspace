Lead Magnet Planning Template

Your step-by-step worksheet to plan a high-converting lead magnet

← Back to Session Guide
1

Foundation: Know Your Audience

Before creating anything, get crystal clear on who you're serving.

Be specific: demographics, role, situation, challenges

The specific problem that keeps them up at night

Their dream outcome in their own words

Understanding their failed attempts helps you position your solution

Write down actual phrases they say when describing their problem

2

Choose Your Lead Magnet Type

Select the format that best matches your audience and expertise.

Consider: audience preference, your expertise, complexity of topic

3

Define Your Lead Magnet

Get specific about what you're offering and the value it provides.

Use the formula: "The [X]-Step System to [Outcome] Without [Pain Point]"

What will they be able to do/know/feel after consuming this?

What's ONE thing they can implement immediately for a result?

Keep it under 15 minutes for optimal completion rates

4

Connect to Your Offer

Your lead magnet should naturally lead to your paid product/service.

The product or service you want to sell after the lead magnet

The lead magnet should solve part of the problem; your offer solves the rest

What's the logical next step after consuming the lead magnet?

5

S.P.E.C.I.F.I.C. Checklist

Verify your lead magnet meets all the criteria for high conversion.

Goal: Check ALL boxes before you start creating. If you can't check one, refine your concept.

6

Content Outline

Draft the structure of your lead magnet.

7

Generate with AI

Use this prompt to create your lead magnet content.

Lead Magnet Creation Prompt
[Upload any data files about your business/methodology first] Create a [TYPE: eBook/Quiz/Mini-Course] for my business. AUDIENCE: [Paste your audience description from Section 1] LEAD MAGNET DETAILS: - Title: [Your title from Section 3] - Promise: [Your one-sentence promise] - Quick Win: [The quick win you defined] - Time to consume: [X] minutes OUTLINE: [Paste your outline from Section 6] PAID OFFER: The lead magnet should naturally lead to my [OFFER NAME], which [brief description]. REQUIREMENTS: - Make it actionable, not theoretical - Include specific examples - Use the language my audience uses: [paste phrases from Section 1] - End with a soft CTA for my paid offer - Keep total reading time under [X] minutes Generate the full content now.

Pro tip: After generating, use the "Copywriter Improvement Prompt" from the session guide to refine the copy using Eugene Schwartz, Cialdini, and other frameworks.
