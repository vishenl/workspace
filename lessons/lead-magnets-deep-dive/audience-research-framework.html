Audience Research Framework

Understand your audience deeply before creating anything

← Back to Session Guide
1

Why Research Matters

Skip this step and your lead magnet will feel generic. Do it right and your copy practically writes itself.

The goal: Know your audience so well that when they read your landing page, they think "This was written for ME."

What You Need to Discover:

  • Pain Points: What problems keep them awake at night?
  • Desires: What transformation do they dream about?
  • Objections: Why haven't they solved this problem yet?
  • Language: What exact words and phrases do they use?
  • Failed Solutions: What have they already tried that didn't work?
2

Method 1: Direct Research

Nothing beats talking to real people. Even 5-10 conversations can transform your understanding.

Customer Interview Questions:

  • Before working with you: "What was happening in your life when you decided to seek help with [topic]?"
  • The trigger: "What was the final straw that made you take action?"
  • Failed attempts: "What had you already tried before that didn't work?"
  • The search: "How did you look for solutions? What did you search for?"
  • Hesitations: "What almost stopped you from [signing up/buying]?"
  • The outcome: "How would you describe the transformation you experienced?"
  • In their words: "If you were recommending this to a friend, how would you describe it?"

Pro tip: Record these conversations (with permission). The exact phrases they use become your copy.

Method 2: Review Mining

Your competitors' customers have already told you what they want. You just need to find it.

Where to Find Reviews:

Amazon Reviews

Search for books in your niche. Read 3-star reviews (balanced feedback) and 5-star reviews (what they loved).

Course Platforms

Udemy, Skillshare, Coursera. See what students praise and complain about in similar courses.

Reddit & Forums

Find subreddits about your topic. Look for "help me" and "finally figured out" posts.

YouTube Comments

Popular videos in your niche. Comments reveal questions, frustrations, and "aha" moments.

What to Look For:

  • Pain language: "I was struggling with...", "I couldn't figure out...", "I was frustrated by..."
  • Desire language: "I wanted to...", "I wished I could...", "My dream was..."
  • Objection language: "I was worried that...", "I almost didn't because...", "I hesitated because..."
  • Transformation language: "Now I...", "Finally I...", "This changed..."

Method 3: AI-Assisted Research

Use AI to synthesize and expand your research. But remember: AI works best when you feed it real data first.

Deep Audience Analysis Prompt
I need to deeply understand my target audience for my lead magnet. MY BUSINESS: [Describe your business/offer] MY NICHE: [Your specific niche] MY IDEAL CUSTOMER: [Basic demographics and situation] Help me understand this audience by answering: 1. PAIN POINTS - What are the top 5 daily frustrations they experience? - What keeps them awake at 3am? - What do they complain about to friends? 2. DESIRES - What does their ideal life look like? - What transformation do they dream about? - What would they pay almost anything to achieve? 3. OBJECTIONS - Why haven't they solved this problem yet? - What have they tried that didn't work? - What are they afraid of? 4. LANGUAGE - What phrases do they use to describe their problem? - What words resonate with them? - What do they call themselves? 5. BUYING TRIGGERS - What would make them say "I need this NOW"? - What external events might trigger them to seek help? - What proof would convince them to trust me? Be specific. Give me real examples of thoughts that go through their head, written in first person as if they're speaking.
Validate Your Assumptions Prompt
I've made some assumptions about my target audience. Help me validate or challenge these. MY ASSUMPTIONS: - Pain point: [What you think their main problem is] - Desire: [What you think they want] - Objection: [What you think holds them back] - Failed solutions: [What you think they've already tried] Questions: 1. Are these assumptions accurate for this audience? 2. What am I missing or getting wrong? 3. What nuances should I consider? 4. Are there segments within this audience I should target differently? Audience: [Describe your audience] Niche: [Your niche]
5

Method 4: Competitive Analysis

See what's working for others in your space. Not to copy—to understand what resonates.

Analyze Competitor Lead Magnets:

  • Headline: What promise are they making?
  • Format: What type of lead magnet (quiz, eBook, webinar)?
  • Value proposition: What specific outcome do they promise?
  • Social proof: What credibility indicators do they use?
  • CTA: What language do they use on buttons?
