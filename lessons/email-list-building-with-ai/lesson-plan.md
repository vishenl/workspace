## LECTURE PORTION (60 Minutes)

### Module 1: Why Email > Social Media (10 minutes)

#### 1.1 The Problem with Social Media
- Meta decides who sees your content
- They can throttle or close your account anytime
- Advertising rates go up every single year
- You're building Mark Zuckerberg's asset, not yours

#### 1.2 The Power of Email
- You own your audience
- As long as you're not spamming, your message gets delivered
- Email ROI: **$36-42 for every $1 spent** (highest of any channel)
- When you have an email list, you can turn on the tap and bring in revenue instantly

#### 1.3 The List Building Equation
```
Traffic × Conversion Rate × Value Proposition = List Growth
```
- **Traffic:** Ads, social, SEO, podcasts (separate lesson)
- **Conversion Rate:** Your landing page optimization
- **Value Proposition:** What you offer in exchange for email

**Key insight:** 500 engaged subscribers beats 10,000 cold ones. If Gmail sees your list isn't active, emails go to spam.

### Module 2: Organizing Your Data (10 minutes)

#### 2.1 The 80% Rule
> "80% of all AI projects is just organizing data." — The Economist, 2020

Before you can build quizzes, landing pages, and email sequences with AI, you need organized data.

#### 2.2 Data Organization Best Practices
1. **Use TXT files** — AI doesn't care about formatting. PDF and Word are too heavy.
2. **Gather everything** — Product descriptions, methodologies, customer comments, interviews
3. **Interview yourself** — Ask ChatGPT to give you 25 questions about your expertise. Record answers on memo app, feed transcript back.

#### 2.3 What to Include in Your Data
- Your methodology/unique approach
- Customer testimonials and comments
- Product/service descriptions
- Your story and credentials
- Common questions you get asked
- Industry insights you've learned

### Module 3: Two Ways to Collect Emails (15 minutes)

#### 3.1 Quizzes (Recommended)
**Why quizzes are powerful:**
- Interactive and engaging
- Customer gets "aha" moment immediately—before even checking email
- Activates commitment and consistency
- Higher conversion than static lead magnets

**Ideal Quiz Structure (15 questions):**
- **10 problem questions** — Questions about their challenges, goals, current situation (what you'd ask as an expert)
- **5 demographic questions** — Age, location, how they heard about you, company name (for lead scoring)

#### 3.2 Free Downloads
- E-books and mini-guides
- Audio trainings
- Templates and checklists
- Delivered via email sequence

**The S.P.E.C.I.F.I.C. Framework:**
- **S**olves one specific problem
- **P**rovides immediate value
- **E**asy to consume (under 15 min)
- **C**onnects to your paid offering
- **I**nstantly deliverable
- **F**eels premium (high perceived value)
- **I**nspires action
- **C**lear outcome promised

#### 3.3 Live Demo: Build a Quiz in 20 Minutes

**Step 1: Upload Your Data to ChatGPT**
Upload all your TXT files with your methodology, customer insights, etc.

**Step 2: Brainstorm the Quiz**
```
I'm [describe yourself and your business]. My ideal audience is [describe them]
who struggle with [main problem].

Help me brainstorm a quiz that:
- Analyzes what's weak in their current approach
- Gives them a score or personalized result
- Can be completed in under 2 minutes
- Naturally leads to my product/service

I'm uploading my data files now. Use them to make this quiz specific to my methodology.
```

**Step 3: Get the Lovable Prompt**
```
I want a simple 15 question quiz. Give me the Lovable prompt to build this quiz.
This quiz should also collect email address and first name.
```

**Step 4: Build in Lovable**
Paste the prompt into Lovable and connect to Supabase for data storage.

### Module 4: The Auto Responder Sequence (15 minutes)

#### 4.1 My Original 9-Part Sequence (2005)
"Living Your Alpha Life" — A 9-lesson e-book on harnessing the power of the mind. **It took me 30 days to write. 1 million people signed up.**

How it worked:
- Each lesson was dripped via email over 2 weeks
- Short email → link to full lesson on website
- Day 3: Reminder email with discount offer for full program
- After sequence: Weekly newsletter with upsells

**With AI, what took 30 days can now be done in 1 hour.**

#### 4.2 The First 7 Days Are Everything
> The first 7 days are where you build trust. After 7 days, there's a big drop off. **Ask for the sale in the first week.**

**The 5-Email Welcome Framework:**

| Email | Timing | Purpose |
|-------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Immediately | Deliver + Connect |
| 2 | Day 1-2 | Your Story |
| 3 | Day 3-4 | Quick Win |
| 4 | Day 5-6 | Common Mistake |
| 5 | Day 7 | The Invitation (soft pitch) |

#### 4.3 AI Prompt for Email Sequence
```
[Upload your data files first]

I want to create an auto responder sequence for my website to educate people on [TOPIC].

We don't want this to be super salesy, but we want it to be really educational.
However, sales should be subtle, gentle, and in the PS line.

Give me an idea for an auto responder sequence that can be delivered in 7 lessons.
```

#### 4.4 Make AI Improve Itself
```
Evaluate this email from the perspective of world-class copywriters like
Eugene Schwartz, Gary Bensivenga, Joanna Wiebe, and Andre Chaperone.

Also bring in Cialdini's 6 triggers.

Then improve the email and explain to me why you're improving it
and the psychology you're using.
```

**Don't waste time reading copywriting books. Ask ChatGPT to apply the principles for you.**

### Module 5: Don't Be Vanilla (5 minutes)

#### 5.1 Authenticity Drives Revenue
When I started sharing real stories, real opinions, and being controversial in my emails, **revenue went up 44%**.

My lawyers said I'd kill the list. Instead, engagement skyrocketed.

#### 5.2 The Rules of Engagement
- Share real stories, real experiences, real opinions
- It's okay for some people to love you and some to hate you
- What you DON'T want is apathy—that's marketing death
- Controversy drives engagement (Gmail notices!)
- Discuss topics others won't dare to discuss

#### 5.3 Why This Works
- You either want people to love you or hate you
- Apathy is the opposite of marketing
- Your email list becomes a voice where you build authority
- When people engage (even to disagree), Gmail keeps delivering your emails

### Module 6: Tools & Metrics (5 minutes)

#### 6.1 What Email Tool Should You Use?
**Don't ask me. Ask ChatGPT:**
```
What email auto responder tool should I use?

I am in [LOCATION]. My list is primarily [DESCRIBE AUDIENCE].
I'm expecting to build a list of [SIZE] people.
I want something easy to use.
Budget: no more than $[X] a month.

Important feature: A/B testing for subject lines.
```

#### 6.2 Key Metrics to Track
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Opt-in Rate (cold traffic) | 25%+ |
| Welcome Sequence Open Rate | 50%+ |
| Click-through Rate | 2-5% |
| Unsubscribe Rate | <0.5% |

#### 6.3 On Segmentation
> "Even with 2.5 million people, we don't really segment. Don't worry about segmentation until you have 100,000 people."

- **Avoid:** Demographic segmentation (age, gender)—people are too complex for boxes
- **Better:** Value-based segmentation (spiritual seekers vs. exponential entrepreneurs)

## Q&A PORTION (30 Minutes)

### Common Questions to Prepare For

**"What email platform should I use?"**
→ Ask ChatGPT with your specific requirements (location, list size, budget, features)

**"How do I ensure people actually open emails?"**
→ Good subject lines. Use ChatGPT to generate multiple options. Use A/B testing.

**"Is there a difference for B2B vs B2C?"**
→ Yes, collect company name for B2B. Use open fields in your email provider.

**"What if I don't have data to upload?"**
→ Interview yourself. Have ChatGPT ask you 25 questions, answer via voice memo, transcribe, feed back.

**"How do I make the quiz valuable?"**
→ You don't know until you test. But with vibe coding, it takes 20 minutes. Just do it.

**"Should I segment my list?"**
→ Not until you have 100K+ people. Focus on value-based segments, not demographics.
