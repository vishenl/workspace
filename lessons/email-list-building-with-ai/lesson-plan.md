# Email List Building with AI Tools
## 90-Minute Lesson Plan

**Duration:** 90 minutes total (60 min lecture + 30 min Q&A)
**Audience:** Entrepreneurs, creators, and professionals who want to build an email list using AI tools
**Outcome:** Participants leave with a complete list-building strategy and AI-generated assets ready to deploy

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, participants will be able to:
1. Identify their ideal subscriber and craft a compelling value proposition
2. Use AI tools to create a high-converting lead magnet in under 15 minutes
3. Write opt-in page copy that converts using AI prompts
4. Generate a welcome email sequence using their expertise + AI
5. Understand the key metrics and strategies for growing their list

---

## Pre-Lesson Setup

**Participants should have:**
- Access to an AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, or similar)
- A clear idea of their niche/expertise area
- Optional: Laptop open and ready to follow along

## LECTURE PORTION (60 Minutes)

### Module 1: The Foundation (10 minutes)

#### 1.1 Why Email Lists Still Matter (3 min)
- You own your list (vs. social media algorithm dependency)
- Email ROI: $36-42 for every $1 spent (highest of any channel)
- Direct relationship with your audience
- The "1,000 true fans" concept applied to email

#### 1.2 The List Building Equation (3 min)
```
Traffic × Conversion Rate × Value Proposition = List Growth
```
- Most people focus on traffic first (wrong order)
- Start with VALUE (what's in it for them?)
- Then optimize conversion
- Then drive traffic

#### 1.3 Know Your Ideal Subscriber (4 min)
**Exercise prompt for participants to think about:**
- Who specifically do you want on your list?
- What problem are they desperately trying to solve?
- What transformation do they want?
- Where do they currently hang out online?

**Key insight:** Your list quality matters more than quantity. 500 engaged subscribers beats 10,000 cold ones.

### Module 2: Creating Your Lead Magnet with AI (20 minutes)

#### 2.1 What Makes a Great Lead Magnet (5 min)

**The S.P.E.C.I.F.I.C. Framework:**
- **S**olves one specific problem
- **P**rovides immediate value
- **E**asy to consume (under 15 min)
- **C**onnects to your paid offering
- **I**nstantly deliverable
- **F**eels premium (high perceived value)
- **I**nspires action
- **C**lear outcome promised

**Best Lead Magnet Types for AI Creation:**
1. Checklists / Cheat Sheets
2. Templates / Swipe Files
3. Mini-Guides (5-10 pages)
4. Resource Lists
5. Quizzes / Assessments
6. Video Training + Workbook

#### 2.2 Live Demo: Creating a Lead Magnet with AI (10 min)

**Step 1: The Discovery Prompt**
```
I'm an expert in [YOUR EXPERTISE]. My ideal audience is [DESCRIBE THEM]
who struggle with [MAIN PROBLEM].

Help me brainstorm 10 lead magnet ideas that:
- Solve a specific, urgent problem
- Can be consumed in under 15 minutes
- Showcase my expertise
- Naturally lead to my paid offering: [YOUR OFFER]

For each idea, rate it 1-10 on: urgency, ease of creation, perceived value.
```

**Step 2: The Creation Prompt**
```
Create a complete [LEAD MAGNET TYPE] on [TOPIC].

Structure it as:
- Compelling title with specific outcome
- Brief intro (why this matters)
- The main content (actionable, specific)
- Quick wins they can implement today
- Clear next step (leading to my offer)

My unique perspective/method is: [YOUR APPROACH]
Include these key insights from my experience: [YOUR KNOWLEDGE]
```

**Step 3: The Polish Prompt**
```
Review this lead magnet and:
1. Make the title more compelling (use specific numbers/outcomes)
2. Add a "Quick Win" callout box every 2-3 sections
3. Ensure every paragraph earns its place (cut fluff)
4. Add a persuasive call-to-action at the end
```

#### 2.3 Lead Magnet Mistakes to Avoid (5 min)
- Too broad (trying to solve everything)
- Too long (books are not lead magnets)
- No clear next step
- Generic content (could be anyone's)
- Doesn't connect to your paid offer
- Poor design (perceived value matters)

### Module 3: Opt-In Page Copy That Converts (15 minutes)

#### 3.1 Anatomy of a High-Converting Opt-In Page (5 min)

**Essential Elements:**
1. **Headline:** Specific outcome + timeframe (if possible)
2. **Subheadline:** Who it's for + what they'll get
3. **Bullet points:** 3-5 specific benefits (not features)
4. **Social proof:** Testimonials, numbers, logos (if available)
5. **Simple form:** Name + email (fewer fields = higher conversion)
6. **CTA button:** Action-oriented, specific ("Get Your Free Checklist")
7. **Trust elements:** Privacy note, unsubscribe mention

**Benchmark conversion rates:**
- Cold traffic: 20-35%
- Warm traffic: 35-50%
- Hot traffic (your audience): 50-70%

#### 3.2 AI Prompt for Opt-In Page Copy (5 min)

```
Write opt-in page copy for my lead magnet: [NAME OF LEAD MAGNET]

Target audience: [DESCRIBE THEM]
Main problem they face: [PROBLEM]
Transformation promised: [OUTCOME]

Include:
1. 3 headline options (use specific numbers/outcomes)
2. A subheadline for each
3. 5 benefit-driven bullet points (start with action verbs)
4. 3 CTA button text options
5. A brief "What you'll learn" section

Tone: [Professional/Casual/Urgent/Friendly]
My brand voice is: [DESCRIBE YOUR VOICE]
```

#### 3.3 The "Above the Fold" Test (5 min)
- Everything needed to convert should be visible without scrolling
- Mobile-first: 60%+ of opt-ins happen on mobile
- Load time matters: Every second of delay = 7% drop in conversions
- One clear action (don't confuse with multiple offers)

### Module 4: Welcome Sequence That Builds Trust (10 minutes)

#### 4.1 The First 5 Emails Framework (4 min)

**Email 1: Deliver + Connect (Send immediately)**
- Deliver the lead magnet
- Quick win they can implement now
- Set expectations for future emails
- Personal story/connection

**Email 2: Your Story (Day 1-2)**
- Why you care about this topic
- Your journey/credentials
- Relatability + authority

**Email 3: Quick Win (Day 3-4)**
- Actionable tip they can use today
- Build trust through value
- Subtle mention of your offer

**Email 4: Common Mistake (Day 5-6)**
- What most people get wrong
- Position yourself as the guide
- Agitate the problem (ethically)

**Email 5: The Invitation (Day 7)**
- Soft pitch for your paid offer
- Clear next step
- Remove friction (FAQ, guarantee)

#### 4.2 AI Prompt for Email Sequence (4 min)

```
Write a 5-email welcome sequence for new subscribers who downloaded
my lead magnet: [LEAD MAGNET NAME]

About my audience:
- Who they are: [DESCRIPTION]
- Their main struggle: [PROBLEM]
- What they want: [DESIRED OUTCOME]

About me:
- My background: [BRIEF BIO]
- My unique approach: [WHAT MAKES YOU DIFFERENT]
- My paid offer: [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE]

For each email provide:
- Subject line (+ 1 alternative)
- Preview text
- Full email body
- Clear CTA

Tone: [YOUR BRAND VOICE]
Email length: 200-400 words each
```

#### 4.3 Email Best Practices (2 min)
- Subject lines: Curiosity, specificity, or benefit
- Write like you're emailing ONE person
- One CTA per email
- P.S. lines get read (use them)
- Test send times (Tuesday-Thursday often best)

### Module 5: Growth Strategies & Next Steps (5 minutes)

#### 5.1 Quick Traffic Strategies
1. **Content upgrades:** Add opt-ins to existing content
2. **Social bio links:** Direct to opt-in (not homepage)
3. **Podcast guesting:** Always have a freebie to mention
4. **Collaborations:** Joint webinars, bundles, swaps
5. **Quizzes:** Higher engagement than static lead magnets

#### 5.2 Key Metrics to Track
- **Opt-in rate:** Aim for 25%+ (cold), 40%+ (warm)
- **Open rate:** Aim for 30%+ (welcome sequence should be 50%+)
- **Click rate:** Aim for 2-5%
- **Unsubscribe rate:** Under 0.5% is healthy
- **List growth rate:** Track weekly/monthly

#### 5.3 Your Action Plan
1. Today: Create your lead magnet using AI prompts
2. This week: Write and schedule your welcome sequence
3. Next week: Set up your opt-in page and test
4. Ongoing: Add one new traffic source per month

## Q&A PORTION (30 Minutes)

### Suggested Q&A Structure

**First 5 minutes:** Collect questions
- Have participants type questions in chat or raise hands
- Organize by theme (creation, tech, strategy, mindset)

**Next 20 minutes:** Answer questions
Likely topics to prepare for:
- "What email platform should I use?"
- "How often should I email my list?"
- "What if my niche is too small/competitive?"
- "How do I write if I'm not a good writer?" (AI!)
- "Should I buy a list?" (Never!)
- "How do I re-engage a dead list?"
- "What about GDPR/compliance?"

**Final 5 minutes:** Wrap-up
- Recap the key action items
- Share any additional resources
- Provide way to follow up/get support
