Optimizing Your Landing Page

  • Headline: Specific outcome + timeframe
  • Subheadline: Who it's for + what they get
  • Bullet points: 3-5 specific benefits
  • Simple form: Name + email only
  • CTA button: Action-oriented ("Get Your Free Guide")
  • Mobile first: 60% of conversions happen on mobile
