02 / Brand Identity

Who Is Vishen?

The authentic person behind the brand—not a persona, but the real story.

The Background

Malaysian-born. Raised Hindu in a Muslim country. Computer engineer by training. Failed Silicon Valley startup founder. Commission-only salesman who hit rock bottom. Discovered meditation out of desperation. Built Mindvalley from $0 to $100M+ guided by intuition and daily practice.

Father of two. Married to an Estonian woman from the Lutheran faith at a time when interracial marriages were still questioned. Put on a Muslim watchlist at JFK despite not being Muslim. Relocated to Malaysia. Used pain as fuel for mission.

Core belief: The human species is capable of extraordinary things. Our natural state is unity. The systems dividing us—education, politics, religion, business—need to evolve.

The Transformation

Before: School felt like a prison. Creativity suppressed. Microsoft career that felt wrong. Dot-com crash wiped everything out. Broke, desperate, burnt out.

The Moment: One night, desperate, Googled "how to find happiness." Found a meditation class. Within months, sales skyrocketed. Creativity exploded. Asked: "Why didn't school teach us THIS?"

After: Founded Mindvalley in 2004 selling meditation CDs. Built it into the world's largest personal transformation platform. Now look better, feel better, and get more done in my 40s than I did in my 20s.

03 / Brand Pillars

What I Stand For

The five non-negotiable principles that guide everything I create and say.

01

Consciousness Engineering

A systematic, science-backed approach to upgrading beliefs and habits. Your mind is like an operating system—it can be debugged, upgraded, and optimized. I teach people to become the engineers of their own consciousness.

Extraordinary Living

Rejecting mediocrity and societal "brules" (bullshit rules). Extraordinary isn't about achievement—it's about refusing to live a borrowed life. It's about questioning the playbook you were handed and writing your own.

Merging profit with purpose and transformation. Mindvalley proves that conscious capitalism works. We're profitable, self-funded, and singularly obsessed with elevating humanity—not reporting to investors.

04

Evidence-based approaches to human potential. I don't ask you to believe—I ask you to experiment. Every framework I teach can be tested, measured, and validated in your own life.

05

Before we colonize other planets, we must fix our own. I serve as a spokesperson for the Earth Flag and believe in "Earth identity"—one human family, sharing one home, capable of extraordinary co-creation.

04 / Voice & Tone

How I Sound

Guidelines for maintaining authentic communication across all platforms.

Direct & Confident

I speak with conviction. I don't hedge or apologize for my ideas. But confidence isn't arrogance—I'm always open to being wrong.

Provocative (Not Offensive)

I challenge assumptions and question the status quo. I'm willing to say unpopular things if they're true. But I never punch down or attack individuals.

Vulnerable & Human

I share my struggles, fears, and failures openly. Recent content about hair loss, aging, and visibility fears connects because it's real.

Data + Story

I blend scientific evidence with personal narrative. Lead with story, support with data. Make the complex accessible without dumbing it down.

05 / Signature Ideas

My Frameworks & Concepts

The intellectual property that defines my thought leadership.

Brules (Bullshit Rules)

The invisible rules we inherit from culture, family, and society that we follow blindly—even when they no longer serve us. College is the only path to success? Brule. You need to work 40 hours a week? Brule. Retirement at 65? Brule. My work is about helping people identify their brules and consciously choose which rules to follow.

When to Use This Concept

When challenging conventional wisdom. When discussing education, career, success, relationships. When helping someone see that their "limitation" is actually an inherited belief they can question.

The 6 Phase Meditation

A radical re-envisioning of meditation for modern life. Not about sitting in silence for hours—it's a 20-minute practice that combines gratitude, forgiveness, visualization, intention, blessing, and presence. Used by high performers and creatives worldwide.

When to Use This Concept

When discussing meditation, productivity, mental health, morning routines. When someone says "I can't meditate." Position as: "The meditation that makes meditation actually work for your real life."

Consciousness Engineering

A two-part framework for human transformation: upgrade your models of reality (beliefs about how the world works) and your systems for living (habits and practices). Like debugging and upgrading your personal operating system.

When to Use This Concept

When explaining personal growth to skeptics or technical audiences. When discussing the systematic approach to transformation. Best for B2B, speaking, and intellectual contexts.

The Borrowed Identity Problem (NEW)

Most people are living borrowed identities—versions of success, happiness, and purpose handed to them by family, culture, and social media. Borrowed dreams. Borrowed metrics. Borrowed timelines. The path to extraordinary living starts with asking: "Is this goal mine, or borrowed?"

When to Use This Concept

For mainstream cultural conversations. Social media content about identity, purpose, success. Mental health discussions. Highly relatable—use frequently for cultural breakthrough.

Bending Reality

The state where you're happy in the present while having a vision for the future that pulls you forward. When you operate from this state, "lucky" coincidences increase, opportunities appear, and life seems to bend in your favor. Not magic—it's pattern recognition meeting action.

When to Use This Concept

When discussing manifestation, goal-setting, or entrepreneurship. Best used with business/achievement audiences who might be skeptical of pure "spiritual" language.

07 / Origin Story

The Journey

Key moments that shaped who I am today—use these for storytelling.

Growing Up in Malaysia
School Felt Like Prison

Raised in Kuala Lumpur. School suppressed creativity and individuality instead of celebrating it. Planted a seed: one day, reinvent education to awaken the human spirit, not anesthetize it.

Early Career
Microsoft to Silicon Valley

Computer engineering degree. Joined Microsoft but quickly realized corporate life wasn't for me. Left to pursue a software startup in Silicon Valley. The timing was disastrous.

The Crash
Lost Everything

Dot-com bubble burst. Lost everything. Took a commission-only sales job to survive. Broke, desperate, burnt out. Rock bottom.

The Discovery
"How to Find Happiness"

One desperate night, Googled "how to find happiness." Found a meditation class. Within months: sales skyrocketed, creativity exploded. Asked myself: "Why the hell didn't school teach us this?"

2004
Mindvalley is Born

Registered a tiny website to sell meditation CDs. The simplest expression of a big dream: bring transformational education to everyone. Started as a small digital publisher.

2003
The Watchlist

At JFK airport, taken to a small room and told I'd been added to a Muslim watchlist—despite not being Muslim. Couldn't board a flight without 3-hour interviews. Had to report to government every 28 days. Relocated to Malaysia with Kristina.

2010
A-Fest Launches

Moved beyond online courses to create real-world experiences. A-Fest became one of the world's most unique gatherings for conscious entrepreneurs and changemakers.

2016
Mindvalley Quests + The Code

Launched Mindvalley Quests—revolutionary learning with 800% higher completion rates. Published "The Code of the Extraordinary Mind"—hit #1 on Amazon globally, became NYT Bestseller.

2020
The Buddha and the Badass

Second book showing that spirituality and business aren't opposites—they're partners. Wall Street Journal Bestseller.

Today
Mission: 1 Billion by 2038

Mindvalley App in every Apple Store globally. 20M+ students. Team from 50+ nations. 300 people working full-time on evolving human consciousness. Goal: elevate 1 billion souls by 2038.
