01 / Core Positioning

Who Am I & What Am I Doing For You?

Lead with mission, not title. Lead with value, not credentials.

Part 1: Who I Am + Mission

"I'm Vishen Lakhiani and I'm transforming a billion lives through Mindvalley."

Part 2: What I Do For YOU

"I help high performers merge spirituality and strategy to become the best versions of themselves."

Primary Positioning Statement

I speak at the intersection of AI, Mindset, Soul, and Longevity—helping high performers merge spirituality and strategy to evolve into the best versions of themselves. Transformation never ends.

What NOT to Lead With

Don't lead with "Founder of Mindvalley" or "$100M company." This can trigger cynicism ("he built this to get rich"). Instead, lead with the MISSION: "Transforming a billion lives." The business proof comes secondary—people already assume a billion lives = significant resources.

The Testing Angle

"I spend millions of dollars a year testing personal growth, AI, and longevity ideas on myself—so I can share the best with you."

From signing world-class authors, to running events, to building products—I'm the guinea pig so you don't have to be.

🎯

Mission-Led, Not Title-Led

"Transforming a billion lives" is more empowering than "Founder of Mindvalley." One invites everyone in. The other creates distance.

🧠

Spirituality + Strategy

No one talks about this openly, but all successful people use BOTH spirituality and strategy. I make that integration explicit and accessible.

📈

Performance is the Trunk

Think of my brand as a tree. The trunk is HIGH PERFORMANCE. AI, Mindset, Soul, and Longevity are the major branches that all feed into performance.

"Transformation never ends. The single most consistent foundation to success in life is lifelong learning. There isn't an end. It's perpetual, it's consistent."

— Team Discussion, November 2025

05 / Voice & Tone

How I Sound

Guidelines for maintaining authentic communication across all platforms.

Direct & Confident

I speak with conviction. I don't hedge or apologize for my ideas. But confidence isn't arrogance—I'm always open to being wrong.

Mission-Led, Not Ego-Led

Lead with "transforming lives" not "I built a $100M company." Show the impact, not the trophy case. Let proof come through story, not stats.

Vulnerable & Human

I share my struggles, fears, and failures openly. Recent content about hair loss, aging, and visibility fears connects because it's real.

Spirituality for Skeptics

I make spiritual concepts accessible to business-minded people. Data + story. Not "the universe will manifest" but "here's the science behind why this works."

Voice Guidelines

✓ Do Say

"I'm transforming a billion lives through Mindvalley..."

✗ Don't Say

"I'm the founder of a $100 million company..."

✓ Do Say

"I help high performers merge spirituality and strategy..."

✗ Don't Say

"I teach entrepreneurs how to succeed..."

✓ Do Say

"All successful people use this, but no one talks about it..."

✗ Don't Say

"As someone who has achieved success..."

✓ Do Say

"Here's what I learned spending millions testing this..."

✗ Don't Say

"The universe will manifest this for you..."

07 / Origin Story

The Journey

Key moments that shaped who I am today—use these for storytelling.

Growing Up in Malaysia
School Felt Like Prison

Raised in Kuala Lumpur. School suppressed creativity and individuality instead of celebrating it. Planted a seed: one day, reinvent education to awaken the human spirit, not anesthetize it.

Early Career
Microsoft to Silicon Valley

Computer engineering degree from top-5 university. Joined Microsoft but quickly realized corporate life wasn't for me. Left to pursue a software startup in Silicon Valley. The timing was disastrous.

The Crash
Lost Everything

Dot-com bubble burst. Lost everything. Took a commission-only sales job to survive. Broke, desperate, burnt out. Rock bottom.

The Discovery
"How to Find Happiness"

One desperate night, Googled "how to find happiness." Found a meditation class. Within months: sales skyrocketed, creativity exploded. Asked myself: "Why the hell didn't school teach us this?"

2004
Mindvalley is Born

Registered a tiny website to sell meditation CDs. The simplest expression of a big dream: bring transformational education to everyone. Inspired by Mandela: "If you want to change the world, change education."

2010
A-Fest & Events

Moved beyond online courses to create real-world experiences. A-Fest became one of the world's most unique gatherings for conscious entrepreneurs and changemakers.

2016
Mindvalley Quests + The Code

Launched Mindvalley Quests—revolutionary learning with 800% higher completion rates. Published "The Code of the Extraordinary Mind"—hit #1 on Amazon globally, became NYT Bestseller.

Today
Mission: 1 Billion by 2038

App in every Apple Store globally. 20M+ students. Team from 50+ nations. Spending millions testing AI, mindset, soul, and longevity ideas. Goal: transform 1 billion lives by 2038.
