Sales Psychology

The Core Framework: Calling, Not Closing

Traditional sales: Overcome objections → Push to close → Get the sale.

This sale: Validate the calling → Remove obstacles → Help them say yes to themselves.

⚠️
What NOT To Do
  • Don't be pushy. These are Mindvalley's beloved students. Vishen hangs out with them for dinner. They're family.
  • Don't re-pitch the webinar. They've seen it. Go deeper, not wider.
  • Don't treat price as the obstacle. They checked a box for $10K. If price comes up, dig for the real concern.
  • Don't rush. This is high-touch, not high-volume. Quality over quantity.

The 3 Psychological Levers

1. Identity Transformation (Primary)

They're not buying training. They're becoming a Certified Mindvalley Manifesting Coach.

Use language that speaks to who they'll BECOME:

  • "Imagine introducing yourself as a Certified Mindvalley Manifesting Coach..."
  • "When you're working with your first client and they have that breakthrough moment..."
  • "A year from now, you'll look back at this as the moment everything changed..."

2. Soul Calling (Emotional)

They feel CALLED to this. Honor that. Don't make it transactional.

  • "It sounds like this isn't just interest—it's a calling. Am I reading that right?"
  • "What would it mean to you to help others experience what you've experienced?"
  • "When you imagine NOT doing this... how does that feel?"

3. Exclusivity & Intimacy (Scarcity)

40 people max. Not recorded. Vishen and Regan want to MEET them. Use this.

  • "This isn't a recorded course—Vishen and Regan specifically wanted to meet each participant personally."
  • "They kept it to 40 people so everyone gets direct access. No hiding in the back."
  • "You can't buy this experience later—once this cohort is full, that's it."

  4. Remove obstacles to their YES

Complete Phone Script

💡
Script Philosophy
This is a guide, not a teleprompter. Internalize the flow and key phrases, then make it your own. Authenticity beats perfection.

Phase 1: Opening & Connection (2-3 min)

Goal: Warm connection. Establish you're a human, not a sales robot. Show you know who they are.

YOU: "Hi [NAME], this is [YOUR NAME] from Mindvalley. Is now still a good time?"

[Wait for confirmation]

YOU: "Perfect. First, I just want to say—I saw you completed Manifesting Mastery. That's a real commitment. Five months, 75 hours... how was that experience for you?"

[Let them share. Listen for transformation stories, vocabulary they use, emotional highlights. This is GOLD for the rest of the call.]

YOU: "Wow. That's powerful. [Reflect back something specific they said]. It sounds like this wasn't just learning—it was real transformation."

[Transition to discovery]

YOU: "So you expressed interest in the Sevilla certification... I'd love to understand what's drawing you to this. But first—I noticed you mentioned [REFERENCE THEIR FORM RESPONSE]. Tell me more about that."

Phase 2: Discovery & Understanding (8-12 min)

Goal: Understand their WHY. Uncover desires, concerns, and what this would mean for them. Let them sell themselves.

Discovery Questions (Use 4-6 of these)

Understanding Their Calling: YOU: "When you imagine yourself as a Certified Manifesting Coach... what does that look like for you?"

YOU: "What made you fill out that interest form? Was there a specific moment or feeling?"

YOU: "If you became certified, who would be the first person you'd want to help?"

Understanding Their Situation: YOU: "Are you already coaching or would this be a new path entirely?"

YOU: "What's your current work situation? How does coaching fit into your vision?"

Understanding Their Concerns: YOU: "What questions do you have that would help you decide if this is right for you?"

YOU: "Is there anything that would stop you from saying yes today?"

The Power Question: YOU: "If you imagine yourself a year from now... and you DID do this... where do you see yourself? And if you DIDN'T do this... how would you feel?"
🎯
Listen for These Signals
  • Calling language: "I feel drawn to this," "I can't stop thinking about it," "It feels like a sign"
  • Transformation stories: Their own manifestations, synchronicities, breakthroughs
  • Service desire: "I want to help others," "My friends keep asking me how I did it"
  • Concerns: Time, money, travel, "am I ready?"

Phase 3: Vision & Future Pacing (5-7 min)

Goal: Paint the picture. Help them FEEL the transformation. Connect their desires to this specific experience.

YOU: "Let me paint a picture of what this is actually going to be like..."

"January 17th. You land in Sevilla. It's sunny—like 60 degrees while everyone back home is freezing. You walk into this stunning historic hotel that's been hosting visionaries since 1929."

"You look around the room and there are 40 people—all of them Manifesting Mastery graduates just like you. People who GET it. No explaining. No skeptics. Just soul-aligned humans."

"And then Vishen walks in. Not on a screen—actually THERE. Same with Regan. And they WANT to meet you. This isn't a recorded course they're selling a million copies of. They deliberately kept it small because they want to know your name, hear your story, work with you directly."

"For three days, you're not just learning—you're BECOMING. The manifesting principles you practiced in MM? Now you're learning to transmit them. To hold space for others. To guide transformation."

"And in the evenings? Authentic flamenco shows. Tapas in the historic quarter. Conversations with your cohort that go until midnight. This isn't a seminar—it's a transformational vacation."

"Then you fly home and for two months, Ajit Nawalkha—who's trained over 50,000 coaches—teaches you the professional side. How to actually BUILD a practice. How to enroll clients. How to make this sustainable."

"And at the end? You're not just someone who took another course. You're a Certified Mindvalley Manifesting Coach. With credentials. With a system. With a community."

[Pause. Let them sit with it.]

YOU: "How does that feel? What part resonates most?"

Phase 4: Address Questions & Concerns (5-8 min)

Goal: Handle logistics, objections, and concerns. Make the path clear and safe.

YOU: "What questions do you have? I want to make sure you have everything you need to make the best decision for you."

[Answer their specific questions first. See Objection Handling section for common responses.]

[After addressing their questions:]

YOU: "I want to be upfront about the logistics so nothing feels hidden..."

Cover These Proactively: • "The investment is $9,899—but I'm able to offer you our Black Friday rate of $9,200. That's $700 off."
• "You can pay in full or break it into three payments of $3,300/month."
• "The certification fee doesn't include flights and hotel—but hotels in Sevilla in January are very affordable. Great Airbnbs for $150/night. The venue hotel is the Colón Gran Meliá and we have a small discount available for attendees."
• "Time commitment after Sevilla is about 2-3 hours per week for two months. Doable alongside a full-time job."
• "You'll need to confirm by January 1st—that's when we finalize the cohort."

Phase 5: The Invitation & Close (3-5 min)

Goal: Make it easy to say yes. This isn't pressure—it's an invitation to step into their calling.

YOU: "So [NAME], based on everything we've talked about... where are you at? Does this feel like your path?"

[Listen to their response. Three scenarios:]

If YES / Ready: YOU: "I'm so excited for you. Let's lock in your spot. I'll send you the enrollment link right now—you can complete it while we're on the call or in the next few hours. Which works better?"

If ALMOST / Needs a Nudge: YOU: "I hear a 'yes' in there but also some hesitation. What's the one thing that would make this a clear yes for you?"

[Address that specific thing]

YOU: "Here's what I know: You've invested 5 months in Manifesting Mastery. You feel called to help others. You're already imagining yourself as a certified coach. The only question is timing—and Sevilla is 6 weeks away."

"The universe brought you to this call for a reason. What do you think that reason is?"

If NOT NOW / Needs Time: YOU: "I totally respect that. This is a significant decision and you should feel 100% clear."

"Here's what I'd ask: Give yourself a real deadline to decide—not indefinitely. The cohort fills and then it's done. How about we reconnect on [specific date before Jan 1]? I'll hold your spot informationally until then."

"What would need to happen between now and [date] for you to feel ready?"

The Magic Question
If they're on the fence, ask: "When you imagine yourself in Sevilla in January, standing next to Vishen and Regan, about to step into this new chapter... what does that feel like?"

Let them answer. Their own words will sell them.

Objection Handling

Remember: These leads acknowledged a $10K investment. Real objections are usually about readiness, not price. Dig deeper.

"I need to think about it."
"I completely understand. This is a significant decision. Can I ask—what specifically do you need to think through? Is it the investment, the timing, the travel, or something else?"

Then: "What would need to be true for this to be a clear yes?"

If vague: "Here's what I've learned: 'I need to think about it' often means there's something specific we haven't addressed. What's the real hesitation? I promise I won't push—I just want to make sure you have what you need."
"It's a lot of money."
"It IS a significant investment. You're right. Let me ask—is it that you're not sure the value is there, or that the timing is challenging financially?"

If value concern: "What would make this worth it to you? What would you need to see happen in 12 months to say 'that was the best $9,200 I ever spent'?"

If timing concern: "We have a 3-payment option—$3,300/month. Does that make it more workable? And here's a way to think about ROI: At $250/session, you'd need just 4 clients at 10 sessions each to recoup the entire investment. Our graduates average 6-8 clients in the first 90 days."
"I don't know if I'm ready to coach."
"That's such an honest thing to say. And here's what I want you to know: That feeling of 'am I ready?' is exactly what the certification addresses."

"You've already done the inner work in Manifesting Mastery. You've experienced transformation. What Ajit's 2-month training does is give you the professional skills—how to hold space, how to ask the right questions, how to structure sessions. That's what turns your experience into expertise."

"Also—can I share something? The best coaches I've seen aren't the ones who felt 100% ready. They're the ones who said yes despite the uncertainty and grew INTO it. Sound familiar from your manifestation journey?"
"The travel is complicated."
"Where would you be flying from?"

Then address specifically:
"Sevilla in January is actually a hidden gem—beautiful weather, no crowds, and very affordable. Flights from [their location] are usually [estimate]. Hotels range from $100-200/night for 4-star, or you can get amazing Airbnbs for $150/night."

"The venue is the Hotel Colón Gran Meliá—a 5-star historic landmark. We have a small discount for attendees if you want to stay there."

"Would it help if I connected you with the logistics team to map out exactly what the travel would look like?"
"I need to talk to my spouse/partner."
"Absolutely. Big decisions should be made together. What do you think their main question or concern would be?"

[This helps you address the real objection]

"Would it be helpful if I sent you a summary of what we discussed? That way you can share the details accurately. And would a three-way call help? I'm happy to talk through any questions they have."

"When do you think you'll have that conversation? I want to make sure you have time to decide comfortably before the January 1st deadline."
"What if I can't build a coaching business?"
"That's exactly why Ajit's 2-month training is so valuable. This isn't just certification—it's a complete business-building system."

"He teaches: How to get your first clients. How to price. How to market without feeling salesy. He's trained 50,000+ coaches and knows exactly what works."

"Also, you'll have the Mindvalley certification—which immediately differentiates you from the thousands of uncertified coaches out there. When people see 'Mindvalley Certified,' that's instant credibility."

"What specifically worries you most about building a practice?"
"Can I attend a future cohort instead?"
"That's a fair question. Here's what I'd consider: This specific cohort is with Vishen and Regan in person. They specifically chose not to record this—they want to actually be present with each participant."

"I can't guarantee the format, teachers, or location for future cohorts. This is what's available and confirmed."

"What's making you want to wait? Is there something specific about January that doesn't work, or is it more about readiness?"
"Is the certification recognized?"
"Great question. The Mindvalley Certified Manifesting Coach credential is recognized internationally. While coaching certifications aren't regulated like medical licenses, the Mindvalley name carries significant weight—we've trained 20 million students globally."

"Ajit's coach training is also ICF-aligned, which is the gold standard in the coaching industry."

"More importantly: when potential clients see you're certified by Mindvalley and trained by Vishen, Regan, and Ajit—that's instant credibility that most coaches don't have."
