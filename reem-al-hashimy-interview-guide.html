Interview Questions — Enhanced

QUESTION 1

Content Creators & Sustainable Aid

Your Question: How can countries and governments work with content creators to make international aid more sustainable?

Background to Know:
  • She chairs Dubai Cares — philanthropic org focused on education in developing countries
  • The 1 Billion Followers Summit is a UAE government initiative combining creator economy with humanitarian themes
  • MrBeast's involvement with "Make Kindness Viral" theme suggests UAE is already pioneering this model
  • Bill Gates honored her as a "champion for child survival"
Suggested Approach: "Your Excellency, you chair Dubai Cares and now the UAE is hosting the world's largest creator summit with a 'Make Kindness Viral' theme. Is this a new model of humanitarian diplomacy? How do you see content creators becoming partners rather than just amplifiers of aid work?"
Potential Follow-ups:
  • How do you ensure creator involvement is sustainable vs. one-off viral moments?
  • What accountability structures should exist when creators promote aid causes?
  • Should governments offer creator visas or incentives for humanitarian content?
QUESTION 2

UAE's 40%+ Gaza Aid Contribution

Your Question: UAE have contributed more than 40% of global aid to Gaza according to United Nations. How come? & why?

Critical Facts:
  • Since 2023, UAE provided $828+ million in humanitarian aid to Gaza
  • This represents 44% of all international assistance to Gaza (UN data, Nov 2025)
  • UAE ranked 3rd globally for humanitarian aid in 2025 ($1.46 billion total)
  • UAE announced $550 million for UN's 2026 Global Humanitarian Overview
  • The UAE has maintained Abraham Accords with Israel while also condemning collective punishment of Gaza
Her Stated Position: She called the October 7 Hamas attacks "barbaric" and called for unconditional hostage release, BUT also said "Hamas attacks can never justify Israel's policy of collective punishment towards Gaza."
Suggested Approach: "The UN reports UAE has provided 44% of all international aid to Gaza — more than any other country. This is remarkable given the UAE also maintains relations with Israel. How do you navigate providing humanitarian leadership while holding this diplomatic position?"
Potential Follow-ups:
  • Is this level of aid sustainable long-term?
  • What operational challenges exist in delivering aid to Gaza?
  • How does the UAE decide where to prioritize — Gaza, Sudan, other crises?
QUESTION 3

US Aid Cuts & Future Sustainability

Your Question: USA Aid was stopped and created a global strategy — how you see sustainable opportunities in the future?

Background to Know:
  • US paused significant foreign aid under recent administration changes
  • This created a vacuum in global humanitarian funding
  • UAE is stepping into this space with major commitments ($550M for 2026)
  • She chairs UAE's National Committee on SDGs — so she's thinking long-term
Suggested Approach: "With shifts in US foreign aid policy creating uncertainty in global humanitarian funding, the UAE has stepped up significantly. Is this filling a temporary gap, or do you see a fundamental restructuring of who leads global aid? What role can private sector, creators, and Gulf states play in making aid less dependent on any single country's politics?"
Potential Follow-ups:
  • How can aid be structured to be less vulnerable to political changes?
  • What partnerships with other donors are you exploring?
  • Is there a model where creator-driven fundraising could become a reliable funding source?
QUESTION 4

Personal Leadership Journey

Your Question: A personal question: you managed Expo — the biggest in the world — as a woman, minister, mother...?

Background to Know:
  • She's been in government since age ~30 (appointed Minister of State in Feb 2008)
  • Led Expo 2020 bid starting in 2011 — managed a decade-long mega-project
  • Expo 2020 had to pivot due to COVID (delayed one year) — she navigated that
  • Created the Firdaus Orchestra (all-women) with A.R. Rahman during Expo
  • Comes from a prominent family in public service (father was also a minister)
Suggested Approach: "You were appointed Minister at 30, led a decade-long bid for the first World Expo in this region, navigated COVID delays, delivered 24 million visitors, and now continue as CEO of Expo City. As a woman in leadership in this region, and as a mother — what has this journey taught you about leadership under pressure?"
Potential Follow-ups:
  • What advice would you give young Emirati women entering public service?
  • How has being a mother influenced your approach to long-term projects like SDGs?
  • What was your hardest day during Expo, and how did you get through it?
