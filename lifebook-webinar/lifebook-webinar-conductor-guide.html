2. Fladlien Methodology Overview

The Jason Fladlien "One to Many" methodology is built on specific psychological principles. Master these and the webinar sells itself.

Stair-Step Pricing

Anchor to $15,000 (live experience) before revealing $500 online price. The contrast creates perceived value.

Core Offer First

Present the main offer in 3-5 minutes max. Don't over-explain. Then stack bonuses on top.

Compound Bonuses

After each bonus, state the running total: "So now you're getting X + Y + Z — over $X,XXX in value."

Save Best for Last

Lead with your second-best bonus. Save the most exciting one for the end to create a crescendo.

Reason Why

Always explain WHY you're offering something. "Why this guarantee? Because Jon believes..."

Objection Destruction

Directly address Time and Money objections. Don't dance around them — confront head-on.

Cost of Inaction

Make NOT buying more expensive than buying. "What's another year of unclear living costing you?"

26+ CTAs

Ask for the order repeatedly throughout the close. Every 2-3 minutes minimum.

The Fladlien Energy

Calm confidence, not hype. You're a doctor prescribing a solution, not a salesperson pushing a product. The teaching should be so valuable that the offer feels like a natural next step.

Phase 1: Teaching Content

64 Slides — Deliver transformational value before the offer

~80 minutes

Teaching Philosophy

The teaching phase is NOT a teaser. You're delivering genuine transformation. The goal is to give so much value that attendees think: "If this is what I get for free, imagine what the paid program offers."

Core Teaching Principle

Teach the WHAT and WHY completely. Let Lifebook be the HOW and the STRUCTURE. They leave with insight; Lifebook gives them the system to implement it.

The Visualization Exercise (Critical)

Visualization Guidance

This is the emotional peak of the teaching. Your voice should slow to about 60% of your normal speaking pace. Leave silence between prompts. Don't fill the space — let them imagine.

  • Morning prompts: Where, who, body, rhythm (4-5 min)
  • Day prompts: Work, people, money, contribution (4-5 min)
  • Evening prompts: Transition, relationships, relaxation (3-4 min)
  • Night prompts: Day ends, head on pillow, gratitude (2-3 min)

Common Mistake

Don't rush the visualization to "get to the sales." The visualization IS the sales. When people feel what an aligned life could be, they sell themselves on Lifebook.

Phase 2: Transition to Offer

The honest bridge between teaching and selling

2-3 minutes

The transition must be honest and direct. The Fladlien method never hides that an offer is coming — it frames it as the logical next step.

Transition Script "What you touched today in that visualization? That wasn't created from nothing. It was uncovered. It was already in you."

"The question is: do you have a system to bring it to life?"

"In a moment, I'm going to share how you can continue this work — how you can take what you've glimpsed today and build it into a complete life design."

"I'm going to make you an offer. And I want to be upfront about that. I genuinely believe this is the most important thing you could invest in — your own life."

"So let me show you what Lifebook is, what you get, and you can decide if it's right for you."

4. Live Case Studies Guide

Optimal Placement: After Price Reveal (Slide 25)

The audience just saw the price ($500) and is evaluating. Real people saying "yes, it was worth it" is 10x more powerful than you saying it. Case studies naturally address objections organically.

Case Study Structure (3-4 min each)

Question 1: Before State

"Where were you before Lifebook? What was the struggle or stuckness you were experiencing?"

Question 2: Decision Point

"What made you finally decide to join? Was there a moment or realization?"

Question 3: Transformation

"What changed? Give us specific, tangible results you've experienced."

Question 4: Advice

"What would you say to someone on the fence right now?"

Running the Case Studies

Transition Into Case Studies "Before I share the bonuses, I want you to hear from four people who were exactly where you are right now."

"They saw the price. They had doubts. They made the decision."

"Let me bring them on..."

[Bring in first guest]

"[Name], thanks for joining us. Tell us — where were you before Lifebook?"

Timing Control

Keep each person to 3-4 minutes max. If they're going long, gently interject: "That's amazing. Last question — what would you say to someone on the fence?" This naturally closes their segment.

Transition Between Guests

"Thank you, [Name]. Incredible story." [Remove from stage] "Let's hear from [Next Name]..." Keep momentum. No dead air.

Transition Out of Case Studies "Thank you to all four of our Lifebook members. Real people. Real transformations."

"Now, let me show you the bonuses that come with your enrollment..."

5. Objection Handling

Fladlien methodology confronts objections directly. Don't dance around them — destroy them.

The "I Don't Have Time" Objection

Time Objection Script "Some of you are thinking: 'I don't have time for a 6-week program.'"

"Let me be direct about this."

"You don't have time NOT to do this."

"How much time have you already wasted on goals that didn't matter? On relationships that weren't right? On work that didn't fulfill you?"

"Lifebook is 6 weeks. The cost of staying unclear is years."

"And here's the thing — it's about 2-3 hours per week. That's less time than you spend scrolling social media."

"The question isn't whether you have time. The question is whether you'll spend time designing your life or drifting through it."

The "I Don't Have Money" Objection

Money Objection Script "Some of you are thinking: '$500 is a lot right now.'"

"I understand. Let me offer a different perspective."

"$500 is less than most people spend on a weekend getaway. A new phone. A few nice dinners out."

"Those things give you hours of enjoyment. Lifebook gives you clarity for the rest of your life."

"And consider the cost of NOT having clarity:"
  • "Years in the wrong career — what's that cost?"
  • "Months with the wrong partner — what's that cost?"
  • "Decisions made randomly instead of intentionally — what's that cost?"

"The $500 isn't an expense. It's the most important investment you'll make — an investment in your own life."

The Guarantee Reinforcement

After handling objections, always reinforce the guarantee: "And remember — you have the Lifebook Breakthrough Guarantee. Complete the program, and if it doesn't transform how you see your life, you can request a refund anytime before March 1, 2026. You literally cannot lose."

6. Q&A Management

Q&A Philosophy

Q&A is not a break from selling — it's a continuation. Every answer should lead back to the offer.

The Fladlien Q&A Formula

  1. Answer the question briefly and directly
  2. Connect it to a benefit of Lifebook
  3. End with a CTA: "That's covered in detail in Module X — click the button to get access."

Common Questions & Responses

Question Response Framework
"How much time per week?" "2-3 hours per week for 6 weeks. Most people say it becomes the highlight of their week. Join now and see for yourself."
"Can I do this with my partner?" "Absolutely — many couples do Lifebook together. Jon and Missy created it as partners. Each person needs their own enrollment. Click now to secure your spot."
"What if I don't finish in 6 weeks?" "You have lifetime access. The 6 weeks is the guided experience, but the content is yours forever. Get started now."
"Is there live support?" "You'll have access to the Lifebook community and support team. You're never alone in this. Join the community now."
"What about the parenting category if I don't have kids?" "You adapt it to your situation — it could be mentorship, legacy, or skip it entirely. The framework is flexible. Get access and make it your own."

Q&A Time Limit

Don't let Q&A go longer than 15 minutes. After that, say: "I want to respect everyone's time. If you have more questions, join Lifebook and ask them inside the community where you'll get detailed answers. Click the button now."

Final Reminders

Your Mindset

You are not selling a product. You are offering a transformation that you genuinely believe will change their lives. Your calm confidence comes from knowing this is true. You don't need to hype — you need to serve.

The Fladlien Close Energy

In the final 10 minutes, you should be asking for the order every 2-3 minutes. Not aggressively — calmly and directly. "Click the button." "Join now." "Get access." "Don't wait." This is not pushy; this is clarity. You're showing them the door. Walking through it is their choice.
