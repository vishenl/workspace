SECTION 2: BORROWED GOALS & THE CULTURESCAPE

Slides 9-14 • Core Teaching: Most goals fail because they were never yours

12-15 minutes
9
Culturescape
Introduction
2 min

Script

"Let me introduce you to a concept I call the Culturescape."

"The culturescape is the invisible field of expectations telling you who to be, what to want, and what success looks like."

"It's running in the background of your mind — and you might not even know it's there."

Key Teaching

This concept is from Vishen's work. Present it as revealing hidden programming.

The word "invisible" is key — they can't fight what they can't see.

10
Culturescape
Examples
2.5 min

Script

"What does the culturescape look like? Here are some examples:"

  • "Instagram bodies — telling you what 'healthy' should look like"
  • "LinkedIn success — defining achievement by job titles and promotions"
  • "Family timelines — you should be married by 30, house by 35, kids by 37"
  • "Cultural definitions of wealth — the car you drive, the neighborhood you live in"

"None of these are inherently wrong. The problem is when they become your goals without you ever choosing them."

Engagement

Ask: "Can anyone relate to this? Have you ever chased a goal and then wondered, 'Wait, do I even want this?'"

Culturescape

"He said: 'Take what is useful, discard what is not, and add what is uniquely your own.'"

[PAUSE — Let the quote land]

Delivery Notes

This quote is foundational. Deliver it slowly and clearly.

The image of Bruce Lee adds authority to the principle.

12
Culturescape
Applied to Life Design

  • "Take what works for you from society — the parts that genuinely serve you"
  • "Discard what doesn't — the expectations that don't fit who you are"
  • "Add your own unique life design — the vision that only you can create"

"This is how you escape the culturescape. Not by rejecting everything, but by choosing consciously."

We're not saying reject society. We're saying: choose consciously instead of automatically.

29
Distinction #1
Baseline vs Peak
2 min

Script

"Distinction number one: Baseline versus Peak."

"Baseline is your sustainable default life — how you live on an average day."

"Peak is temporary intensity — those moments of exceptional effort or experience."

"Most people design for peaks. They think about their best days, their biggest achievements."

"But here's the truth:"

"A great life is built on baseline excellence, not constant peaks."

Key Teaching

This reframes success from "big moments" to "sustainable daily life."

It takes pressure off and makes the goal feel achievable.

30
Distinction #2
Direction vs Perfection
2 min

Script

"Distinction number two: Direction versus Perfection."

"Direction gives calm. When you know which way you're heading, anxiety decreases."

"Perfection creates paralysis. When you need everything figured out, you can't start."

"You don't need certainty. You need a direction your body agrees with."

Key Teaching

The phrase "a direction your body agrees with" is important.

It connects back to nervous system safety from Section 4.

31
Distinction #3
Correction vs Self-Judgment
2 min

Script

"Distinction number three: Correction versus Self-Judgment."

"Drift is normal. You will get off track. Everyone does."

"Correction is skill. The ability to notice and adjust."

"Judgment is noise. It doesn't help. It just slows you down."

"Mastery is how quickly you correct — not how perfectly you perform."

Key Teaching

This removes perfectionism pressure and normalizes course correction.

The line "Mastery is how quickly you correct" is quotable — emphasize it.
