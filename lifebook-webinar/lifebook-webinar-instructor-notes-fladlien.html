The 5 Fatal Flaws
8 min total

Flaw #1: IMBALANCED (Slide 8)

"Most goals focus on 1-2 categories. Win at career, lose at health. Win financially, lose your marriage. It's a zero-sum game that nobody wins."

Flaw #2: EXTERNALLY DRIVEN (Slide 9)

"83% of goals come from outside programming. Your parents. Media. Society. These are borrowed dreams. And borrowed dreams don't have staying power."

Flaw #3: SHALLOW (Slide 10)

"Focus on WHAT, not WHY. Without purpose, willpower runs out. Every. Single. Time."

Flaw #4: REQUIRES WILLPOWER (Slide 11)

"Here's a truth: if your system requires constant motivation... it's a bad system. Good systems make success feel inevitable, not exhausting."

Flaw #5: NOT EVEN GOALS (Slide 12)

"'I want to be happy' isn't a goal. It's a wish. Specificity separates dreamers from achievers."

Engagement

Ask: "Type in the chat — which of these flaws resonates most with you?" This creates participation and self-identification with the problem.
