Daily Engagement Tactics

15-20 minutes per day to boost growth

🔔

Reply to Every Comment Within 1 Hour

Instagram boosts posts with fast engagement. Set notifications for comments and reply with questions to keep the conversation going.

📨

DM New Followers with a Personal Welcome

Send a voice note or personal message: "Hey [name]! Thanks for following 💜 Any questions about our chains? I'm here!"

🔍

Engage with #myupbeads and Related Hashtags

Spend 10 mins daily liking, commenting on, and sharing posts from customers and similar brands. Be genuine, not spammy.

📖

Post 3-5 Stories Daily

Mix of: behind-the-scenes, polls, Q&As, repost customer tags, product close-ups. Stories keep you at the top of followers' feeds.

🎯

Comment on Larger Accounts First

Leave thoughtful comments on accounts with 50K-500K followers in your niche. Early comments get seen by their audience.

🤝

Collaborate with Micro-Influencers

Reach out to 5-10 creators with 5K-20K followers per week. Offer free product for honest review. Authentic reach > paid ads.
