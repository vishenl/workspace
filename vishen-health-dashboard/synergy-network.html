🔥 High Synergy Combinations

Critical pairings - take together for maximum benefit

Vitamin D3
↔️
K2-MK7
High Synergy

Calcium Metabolism Optimization

Mechanism: Vitamin D3 increases calcium absorption, while K2-MK7 directs calcium to bones (not arteries). Without K2, high-dose D3 can cause arterial calcification.

Dosing: 5000 IU D3 + 100-200mcg K2-MK7. Take together with breakfast (fat-soluble).

Zinc
↔️
Copper
Critical Balance

Mineral Balance Maintenance

Mechanism: Zinc supplementation depletes copper stores. Long-term zinc without copper causes anemia and neurological issues. Optimal ratio: 10-15:1 (zinc:copper).

Dosing: 25mg Zinc + 2mg Copper. Take together with breakfast.

Tongkat Ali
↔️
Boron
High Synergy

Testosterone Optimization

Mechanism: Tongkat Ali increases testosterone production, while Boron reduces SHBG binding. Combined effect: More T production + More free T = 30-40% bioavailable testosterone increase.

Dosing: 400mg Tongkat Ali + 6mg Boron. Take together with breakfast.

Red Yeast Rice
↔️
CoQ10
Essential Pair

Cholesterol Management + Mitochondrial Protection

Mechanism: Red Yeast Rice contains natural statins that deplete CoQ10. CoQ10 supplementation prevents muscle pain, fatigue, and mitochondrial dysfunction. Never take Red Yeast Rice without CoQ10.

Dosing: 1200mg Red Yeast Rice + 200mg CoQ10. Take together with lunch.

Lutein
↔️
Zeaxanthin
High Synergy

Macular Protection

Mechanism: Lutein and Zeaxanthin are the only carotenoids found in the macula. They work together to filter blue light and protect retinal cells. Optimal ratio: 5:1 (lutein:zeaxanthin).

Dosing: 20mg Lutein + 4mg Zeaxanthin. Take together with breakfast (fat-soluble).

Omega-3
↔️
Astaxanthin
Protective Synergy

Omega-3 Oxidation Protection

Mechanism: Omega-3 fatty acids are highly susceptible to oxidation. Astaxanthin is one of the most powerful antioxidants, preventing omega-3 degradation in the body and enhancing absorption.

Dosing: 2g Omega-3 (EPA/DHA) + 12mg Astaxanthin. Take together with meals.

⚠️ Avoid These Combinations

Negative interactions - separate timing or avoid

Separate Timing for These Supplements

❌ Zinc + Calcium

Compete for absorption. Take zinc with breakfast, calcium (if supplementing) at night.

❌ Iron + Coffee/Tea

Tannins block iron absorption by 60-90%. Separate by 2+ hours.

❌ Magnesium + High-Dose Zinc

Can interfere with absorption when taken together in high doses. Split between AM/PM.
