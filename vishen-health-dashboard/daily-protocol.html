⏰ Daily Protocol Timeline

Optimized supplement timing for maximum absorption and synergy

☀️

6:00 AM

Upon waking (fasted)

Morning Fasted

☕ Black Coffee
💪 Creatine (5g)

Why fasted: Creatine absorbs better on empty stomach. Coffee enhances fat oxidation.

🍳

7:30 AM

With breakfast

Morning Stack (Fat-Soluble)

🐟 Omega-3 (2g EPA/DHA)
☀️ Vitamin D3 + K2 (5000 IU)
🌿 Tongkat Ali (400mg)
⚡ Boron (6mg)
🔬 Zinc + Copper (25mg/2mg)
👁️ Lutein + Zeaxanthin (20mg)
🦐 Astaxanthin (12mg)
🅱️ B-Complex
🍊 Vitamin C (1000mg)

Why with breakfast: Fat-soluble vitamins (D, A, E, K) and omega-3 require dietary fat for absorption. Tongkat Ali and vision stack work best with food.

🥗

12:30 PM

With lunch

Afternoon Cardiovascular Stack

⚡ Magnesium Glycinate (200mg)
❤️ Red Yeast Rice (1200mg)
🍋 Bergamot (500mg)
⚡ CoQ10 (200mg)
🦠 Probiotics (50B CFU)

Why with lunch: Red Yeast Rice and Bergamot work on cholesterol synthesis (occurs afternoon/evening). CoQ10 essential with Red Yeast Rice.

🏋️

4:30 PM

30 min before workout

Pre-Workout Performance

💪 Citrulline (6g)
🌿 Rhodiola (300mg)
⚡ Taurine (2g)

Why pre-workout: Citrulline enhances nitric oxide for pumps. Rhodiola reduces perceived exertion. Taurine improves endurance.

🍽️

7:00 PM

With dinner

Evening Recovery Stack

🐟 Omega-3 (2g EPA/DHA)
🛡️ NAC (600mg)
🌿 Stinging Nettle Root (500mg)
🧘 Ashwagandha (600mg)
👁️ Bilberry Extract (160mg)

Why with dinner: Second omega-3 dose for 4g total. Ashwagandha reduces cortisol before sleep. NAC supports overnight detox.

💪

7:30 PM

Post-workout (if training)

Post-Workout Recovery

🥛 Whey Protein (30g)
🦴 Collagen Type II (40mg)

Why post-workout: Protein synthesis window. Collagen supports joint recovery.

🌙

10:30 PM

Before bed

Sleep Optimization

😴 Magnesium Glycinate (200mg)
🌙 Apigenin (50mg)
💤 Melatonin (0.3mg, cyclical)

Why before bed: Second magnesium dose for 400mg total (calming). Apigenin enhances GABA. Low-dose melatonin for sleep onset.

📋 Key Timing Principles

✅ DO

  • Take fat-soluble vitamins with meals containing fat
  • Split magnesium doses (AM/PM) for better absorption
  • Take omega-3 twice daily for steady blood levels
  • Use low-dose melatonin (0.3mg) cyclically, not daily
  • Take Red Yeast Rice with CoQ10 (prevents depletion)
  • Stay hydrated throughout the day

❌ DON'T

  • Take zinc and calcium together (compete for absorption)
  • Mix iron with coffee or tea (reduces absorption)
  • Take all supplements at once (overwhelms system)
  • Use high-dose melatonin (>1mg) regularly
  • Take stimulants (coffee, rhodiola) after 4 PM
  • Skip meals when taking fat-soluble vitamins
