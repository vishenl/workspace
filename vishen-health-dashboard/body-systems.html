System Interconnections

Body systems don't work in isolation. Optimizing one system creates cascading benefits throughout the body.

🧠 ↔️ ❤️ Brain-Heart Axis

Omega-3, CoQ10, and Magnesium support both cognitive function and cardiovascular health. Improved blood flow benefits brain performance.

⚡ ↔️ 💪 Hormone-Muscle Axis

Optimized testosterone (Tongkat Ali, Boron) directly enhances muscle growth and recovery. Protein synthesis depends on hormonal environment.

🛡️ ↔️ 🦠 Liver-Immune Connection

NAC and antioxidants support liver detox, which reduces immune system burden. Better detoxification = stronger immunity.

👁️ ↔️ ❤️ Eye-Cardiovascular Link

Circulatory health directly impacts eye health. Omega-3, Bilberry, and Astaxanthin protect both retinal and arterial endothelium.
