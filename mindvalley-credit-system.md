# Mindvalley Credit System: Strategic Design Document

## Executive Summary

This document outlines a comprehensive credit system designed to:
- **Increase MRR** through tiered subscriptions with predictable monthly revenue
- **Improve retention** by creating switching costs and engagement loops
- **Boost ARPU** by making premium products (Mastery, Certifications, Events) more accessible
- **Drive discovery** by incentivizing exploration beyond initial program purchases
- **Create a platform** for future third-party content marketplace

---

## Part 1: Research Insights Applied to Mindvalley

### Why Credits Will Work for Mindvalley

Based on research into ClassPass, Audible, airline miles, and behavioral economics:

| Psychological Principle | How It Applies to Mindvalley |
|------------------------|------------------------------|
| **Mental Accounting** | Credits feel like "funny money" — members spend 1,000 credits more freely than $100 cash |
| **Flat-Rate Bias** | Members prefer predictable monthly credit allotments over variable per-program pricing |
| **Cost Depreciation** | Credits purchased this month feel "free" when used next month |
| **Loss Aversion** | Fear of losing unused credits drives engagement (2x stronger than desire for gains) |
| **Goal Gradient Effect** | Progress toward earning enough credits for Mastery drives sustained engagement |
| **Sunk Cost** | Accumulated credits create powerful retention — canceling means "wasting" them |

### Key Insights from Benchmarks

**From ClassPass:**
- Dynamic pricing based on demand (peak times, popular programs cost more)
- Limited rollover creates urgency without frustration
- Tier upgrades are instant (no waiting for next billing cycle)
- 100% of late cancellation fees go to platform (revenue opportunity)

**From Audible:**
- 1 credit = 1 book regardless of price creates perceived value magic
- Annual plans with upfront credits drive commitment and better economics
- Credits expire after 12 months but unlimited accumulation otherwise
- Pause option reduces hard cancellations

**From Airline Miles:**
- Status tiers create aspirational goals and identity
- Partner ecosystems (credit cards, hotels) expand earning opportunities
- Breakage (unredeemed points) is a profit center
- Dynamic award pricing lets you control redemption costs

**Psychology Applied:**
- Goal Gradient Effect: Motivation increases as you get closer
- Visual progress: Dopamine release from seeing advancement
- Time projection: Makes goal feel achievable

### 2. Variable Reward "Spark Drops"

**Concept:** Random bonus Spark events create anticipation and delight.

| Spark Drop Type | Trigger | Sparks |
|-----------------|---------|--------|
| Morning Meditation Bonus | Random 1 in 10 morning sessions | 50 |
| Weekend Warrior | Completing 3+ activities on weekend | 100 |
| Comeback Bonus | Returning after 7+ day absence | 200 |

**Psychology Applied:**
- Variable ratio reinforcement (most addictive reward schedule)
- Surprise bonuses create stronger positive emotions
- Anticipation of reward releases more dopamine than reward itself

### 3. Discovery Challenges

**Concept:** Time-limited challenges that encourage exploring new content.

**Weekly Challenges:**
- "Try 3 meditations from Soul pillar this week" → 150 bonus Sparks
- "Start a program outside your usual pillar" → 200 bonus Sparks
- "Complete a meditation with a friend" → 100 bonus Sparks each

**Monthly Challenges:**
- "Complete one full program this month" → 750 bonus Sparks
- "Attend all Summit sessions" → 1,000 bonus Sparks
- "Meditate every day this month" → 1,500 bonus Sparks

**Psychology Applied:**
- Bounded goals create urgency without overwhelm
- Encourages discovery beyond comfort zone
- Social challenges drive viral growth

### 5. Expiration & Rollover Design

**Rollover Policy:**
- Up to 2 months' worth of Sparks roll over
- Example: Transformer (2,500/mo) can accumulate up to 7,500 Sparks

**Expiration:**
- Sparks expire 6 months after earning/purchase
- Monthly email: "You have 1,200 Sparks expiring in 30 days"
- Push notification at 14 days and 7 days

**Psychology Applied:**
- Loss aversion creates urgency
- But generous enough to not feel punitive
- Regular reminders activate "use it or lose it" motivation

## Part 7: Retention Mechanics

### Preventing Churn

| Mechanism | How It Works | Psychological Principle |
|-----------|--------------|------------------------|
| Credit Balance | Unused Sparks create switching cost | Sunk Cost |
| Progress Loss | Canceling loses streak bonuses, multipliers | Loss Aversion |
| Status Reset | Losing tier status feels like "breakup" | Status Quo Bias |
| Community | Connections in groups create belonging | Social Bonds |
| Pending Goals | "You're 80% to Mastery" | Goal Gradient |

### Pause Option (vs. Cancel)

**Pause Terms:**
- Available for monthly subscribers
- Pause for 1-3 months
- Keep existing Sparks (no new ones during pause)
- Keep tier status for 1 additional month
- Re-activation within pause period = full benefits restored

**Why This Works:**
- Reduces hard cancellations
- Keeps members in ecosystem
- Fear of losing Sparks prevents pause from becoming permanent

### Win-Back Campaigns

| Scenario | Offer | Psychology |
|----------|-------|------------|
| Paused 60+ days | "Your 2,500 Sparks miss you. Come back for 500 bonus." | Loss aversion |
| Canceled <30 days | "Complete your Mastery journey — 50% off first month back" | Sunk cost |
| Canceled 30-90 days | "Here's 1,000 Sparks free if you return this week" | Scarcity + Gift |
| Canceled 90+ days | "A lot has changed! 2 weeks free to explore" | Fresh start |

- Mastery/Certs: Instructor costs, materials

**Key Insight:** Unlike ClassPass (which pays studios), Mindvalley's content is owned. Credit redemptions have minimal marginal cost, making breakage extremely profitable.

## Part 12: Risk Mitigation

### Potential Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Devaluation backlash | Medium | High | Grandfather existing members; communicate changes 90+ days in advance |
| Gaming/fraud | Medium | Medium | Rate limits on earning; fraud detection; community policing |
| Complexity confusion | Medium | Medium | Simple tier names; clear value props; excellent onboarding |
| Cannibalization | Low | High | Price credits to protect high-value products; maintain cash option |
| Breakage too high | Low | Medium | Expiration reminders; reactivation campaigns; generous rollover |

### Grandfathering Strategy

**For Existing Members:**
- Convert current subscription to equivalent tier
- Award "Founding Member" badge
- 2,000 bonus Sparks for transition
- 6-month price lock guarantee
- No forced migration — opt-in period

## Conclusion

The Mindvalley Spark system is designed to:

1. **Solve the single-program problem** by making exploration feel "free" within tiers
2. **Surface the Meditation Library** by including it in all tiers and rewarding daily use
3. **Make Mastery accessible** through earning mechanics that let dedicated members reach premium products
4. **Increase MRR** through predictable subscription tiers
5. **Improve retention** through sunk costs, loss aversion, and status mechanics
6. **Boost ARPU** through tier upgrades and additional Spark purchases
7. **Create platform potential** for third-party marketplace expansion

The system leverages proven psychological principles from ClassPass, Audible, and airline loyalty programs while taking advantage of Mindvalley's unique position as a content owner with near-zero marginal costs.
