Principle 1

Reciprocity

Give value first—people feel obligated to give back

The Psychology

When someone gives us something, we feel a deep-seated urge to return the favor. This is hardwired into human behavior across all cultures.

Website Tactics

  • 1 Free valuable content (guides, tools, templates)
  • 2 Personalized recommendations
  • 3 Free trials or samples
  • 4 Unexpected bonuses during checkout

Principle 4

Authority

We defer to experts and credible sources

The Psychology

From childhood, we're trained to respect authority figures. Expertise signals that following advice will lead to better outcomes.

The Milgram Experiment: "Scientists at Stanford wanted to understand how Nazi soldiers could be so evil. They had ordinary college students administer what they thought were real electric shocks to others—just because a person in a doctor's medical coat said 'It's okay, continue.'"

— That's how powerful authority is. A white coat was enough to override their conscience.

Principle 5

Liking

We say yes to people we like and relate to

The Psychology

We're more likely to comply with requests from people we find attractive, similar to us, or who give us compliments. Connection creates trust.

Key Factors of Liking:

  • 👤 Physical attractiveness
  • 🤝 Similarity to us
  • 💬 Genuine compliments
  • 🎯 Shared goals or values

Website Tactics

Brand Personality

Use conversational copy that sounds human, not corporate. Show the people behind your brand.

Beautiful Design

Invest in professional photography and clean, aesthetically pleasing layouts. First impressions matter.

Story & Mission

Share your origin story and values. Let visitors see themselves in your mission.

06
Principle 6

Scarcity

What's rare becomes valuable

The Psychology

The fear of missing out (FOMO) is a powerful motivator. When availability is limited, perceived value increases dramatically.

Vishen's 3-Word Win: "We changed 'Special' to 'Black Friday Special'—three words. Sales went up 30%."

Why? "Black Friday" signals a time-limited opportunity. Implied scarcity.

⚠️ Important Warning

Scarcity must be authentic. Fake urgency erodes trust instantly and damages your brand long-term.

Ethical Scarcity Tactics

  • ⏱ Time-limited offers (real deadlines)
  • 🎟 Limited seats/spots (cohort-based)
  • 🎁 Bonus windows (early-bird specials)
  • 📦 Limited inventory (physical products)
  • 💎 Exclusive access (membership perks)

Countdown Timers

Only use when the deadline is real. Consider showing stock levels for products: "Only 3 left in stock"
