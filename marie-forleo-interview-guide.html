1

Establish Trust

0-8 min • Build credibility & rapport
+
🎯 Goal for this section

Establish Marie's 25-year authority while making her relatable. Get the audience leaning in.

★ Opening Question

"Marie, you've shown up consistently for 25 years — your first videos online were shared in 2001 — through platform shifts, algorithm changes, and entirely new rules of the game. In all that change, what has remained absolutely non-negotiable for you — no matter what the platforms demanded?"

💡 This establishes her longevity and authority while framing the conversation around timeless principles vs. tactics.
→ Follow-up

"Since announcing you'd be here, we've had so many people reach out saying, 'Marie is the reason I started creating content,' or 'She made me believe I could do this.' Why do you think your work gives people permission to step into visibility in a way that feels safe and doable?"

Interviewer note: Let her feel the love from the audience here. This sets up the emotional safety for the deeper questions coming next.
🔄 Transition to Section 2 "That idea of 'permission' is so powerful. Earlier today, I actually spoke about something I call the 'visibility wound'..."
2

The Visibility Wound

8-20 min • Go deep on fear
+
🎯 Goal for this section

Connect YOUR earlier talk to her story. Make the audience feel seen in their fear. This is the emotional heart of the interview.

"Social media feels like forcing myself to be inauthentic. It drains my energy."
"Fear of putting myself out there. The perfection has stopped me."
★ Core Question

"Earlier today, I spoke about what I call the 'visibility wound' — the fear of being seen, judged, or misunderstood. You began this journey incredibly young, putting yourself out there as a coach in your twenties. What was your relationship with being seen back then?"

💡 This ties directly to YOUR earlier talk. Creates continuity and gives Marie space to be vulnerable.
→ Follow-up

"So many people here feel the call to share their voice — but also feel 'not ready yet.' Looking back, did confidence come before visibility for you, or did visibility create confidence?"

💡 This is the chicken-or-egg question. Her answer will be liberating for the audience.
→ Go Deeper

"You admitted publicly that you still have 'shitastic days' and get mired in fear and self-doubt. For someone watching right now who's terrified to post their first video — what would you say to them from that place of shared humanity?"

Interviewer note: This section should feel like therapy. Give her space. Let silences land. The audience needs to see that even Marie has felt this.
🔄 Transition to Section 3 "So if visibility creates confidence — not the other way around — then the question becomes: how do we actually take that first step without being overwhelmed?"
3

The Breakthrough

20-35 min • Overwhelm → Clarity
+
🎯 Goal for this section

Move from the emotional (fear) to the practical (clarity). Show them a path through the overwhelm.

"It's like shouting into the wind. I hate social media but I can't grow without it."
"There's too much advice, too many platforms, too many opinions."
★ Core Question

"Marie, so many entrepreneurs today aren't blocked by lack of effort — they're blocked by overwhelm. There's too much advice, too many platforms, too many opinions. When everything feels urgent, how do you personally return to clarity — and how can others learn to do the same?"

→ The Metaphor Question

"You once compared social media to a farmer's market — a place to casually meet people and build real relationships. How did that way of seeing social media shape how you showed up, especially before it became a business?"

💡 This reframe is GOLD. It makes social media feel approachable and human.
→ Tactical Follow-up

"With endless tools and platforms competing for attention, how do you personally decide what's worth your energy — and what's just noise?"

⟿ Bridge Question

"What do you see holding people back when they've already started creating — but haven't yet broken through to meaningful traction or confidence?"

Interviewer note: The "farmer's market" metaphor is unique to Marie. If she elaborates on it well, this could be the most shareable clip from the interview.
🔄 Transition to Section 4 "So we've talked about the mindset shift — now I want to get tactical. Let's talk about the craft..."
4

The Craft

35-50 min • Practical tactics
+
🎯 Goal for this section

Give them something they can DO. Bust myths. Show the "how" behind her success.

★ Myth-Busting Question

"What's the biggest myth about growing on social media that you wish you could erase forever?"

💡 High quotable potential. Will likely generate a shareable sound-bite.
→ Integrity Question

"Can you recall a moment when you consciously chose long-term trust over short-term reach — even knowing it might slow your growth?"

💡 This reveals her values. Shows integrity > vanity metrics.
→ Values in Action

"You often talk about creating value, building trust, and having patience. How do those values actually show up in the way you create content — without chasing fast wins?"

⟿ Optional: Monetization

"You run B-School. You sell programs. But your audience loves you. What's your philosophy on selling on social media that doesn't feel gross?"

Interviewer note: This section can flex based on time. If running long, skip the monetization question — it's covered by other speakers.
🔄 Transition to Section 5 "Everything you've shared has been about playing the long game. Let's zoom out to the 10, 20, 30 year view..."
5

Long-Term Wisdom

50-60 min • Legacy thinking
+
🎯 Goal for this section

Elevate beyond tactics. Help them think about building something that lasts — not just content that performs.

★ Legacy Question

"If someone here is just starting out and wants to build influence that lasts 10, 20, 30 years, what is the one inner practice or principle you'd want them to commit to now?"

💡 This is a "write this down" moment. Frame it that way for the audience.
→ AI Question (Optional)

"This summit has a whole track on AI. As someone who's built her brand on being deeply human — is AI a threat to authentic personal brands, or a tool? What should AI never do for someone building a personal brand?"

Interviewer note: The legacy question is designed to be the "headline" moment. Slow down. Let her think. This is quotable gold.
🔄 Transition to Section 6 "Before we close, I want to ask you something more personal..."
6

The Gift

60-65 min • Emotional close
+
🎯 Goal for this section

End with emotion, not tactics. Send them off inspired and activated. Full circle back to the beginning.

★ Closing Question

"If you could go back to Marie who was bartending while building her business, knowing what you know now about social media and personal branding — what ONE piece of advice would you give her?"

💡 This brings it full circle to her origin story. Vulnerable, human, memorable.
Interviewer note: After her answer, thank her warmly. Let the audience feel the significance of the moment. Don't rush off stage.

✨ The Thread That Ties It Together

You don't have to lose your soul to win at social media. The very thing that makes you human, imperfect, and real is your greatest asset. Marie has proven this for 25 years.
