Alex Hormozi's Value Equation

Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)

Eugene Schwartz's Market Sophistication Analysis

The memory/brain training market is at Stage 4-5 sophistication. Prospects have seen many claims about memory improvement. The copy needs to be mechanism-focused, not just benefit-focused.

Critical Issue

Missing Unique Mechanism

The page doesn't explain WHY Superbrain works differently. At this sophistication level, you need a proprietary mechanism (e.g., "The Neural Pathway Activation Method" or "Jim's 10-Step Memory Architecture"). Without this, the offer blends with competitors.

Improvement

Awareness Stage Mismatch

The copy assumes problem-aware prospects, but many visitors from Facebook ads may be unaware of their memory's true potential. Add an opening section that agitates the problem before presenting the solution.

Joe Sugarman's Slippery Slope Audit

The goal is to make reading so compelling that visitors can't stop until they reach the CTA.

High-Impact Recommendations

High Priority

1. Rewrite the Hero Headline

Est. Impact

Current: "Unlock your brain's limitless potential"
Problem: Generic, doesn't speak to specific outcomes or emotions.

Recommended alternatives:

  • "Remember every name, absorb any book, and never forget what matters — in just 15 minutes a day"
  • "The same brain training Hollywood A-listers use to memorize scripts in hours (now available to you)"
  • "What if you could read 3x faster, remember everything, and think clearer than ever before?"
High Priority

2. Add Problem Agitation Section

Est. Impact

Before the solution, amplify the pain. Missing a section that makes readers feel the cost of their current situation.

Add a section like:

"Every day you struggle with your memory, you're missing opportunities...

- The promotion that went to someone who 'just seemed sharper'
- The conversation where you forgot a key client's name
- The book you read last month but can barely remember
- The feeling of mental fog when you need to be at your best

The worst part? It's not your fault. You were never taught how to use your brain properly."
High Priority

3. Elevate Jim Kwik's Authority

Est. Impact

Jim's bio is hidden behind a button click. His story (brain injury → world's top memory coach) is incredibly compelling and builds massive trust.

Recommendation: Create a dedicated "Meet Your Trainer" section with:

  • His origin story (brain injury at age 5, labeled "the boy with the broken brain")
  • Celebrity clients he's trained (Elon Musk, Will Smith mentioned in bonuses)
  • Specific credentials and achievements
  • A personal video message
Medium Priority

4. Strengthen Testimonials with Specifics

Est. Impact

Current testimonials are good but lack quantifiable results. "I improved my memory" is less powerful than "I went from forgetting my grocery list to memorizing 52 items in order."

Add metrics to testimonials: reading speed improvements, number of names remembered, exam scores, career promotions tied to memory skills.

Medium Priority

5. Add "Who This Is NOT For" Section

Est. Impact

Exclusion creates desire. Adding who shouldn't buy makes the offer feel more exclusive and genuine.

"This is NOT for you if:
- You're looking for a 'magic pill' with zero effort
- You can't commit 15 minutes a day for 30 days
- You're satisfied with your current mental performance
- You don't believe in investing in yourself"
Medium Priority

6. Improve Price Anchoring

Est. Impact

The "$30,000 worth" claim is mentioned but not broken down. Stack the value visibly.

Create a visual value stack:

  • Superbrain Quest ($299 value)
  • 100+ Additional Quests ($15,000+ value)
  • Speed Reading Bonus ($99 value)
  • Overcoming Procrastination ($49 value)
  • Private Community Access ($1,200/year value)
  • AI-Personalized Learning ($2,400/year value)
  • Total: $19,047+ → You pay: $199
Quick Win

7. Add Sticky CTA for Mobile

Est. Impact

Mobile users have to scroll back up to find the CTA. Add a sticky "Become a Member" button that appears after scrolling past the hero section.
