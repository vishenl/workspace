The Opportunity

MindvalleyStates has a solid foundation - world-class credentials with Shawn Wells (1,000+ formulations, 40+ patents), clear product differentiation through state-based naming, and access to Mindvalley's 20M+ user ecosystem. However, aggressive discounting is damaging premium positioning, the value proposition lacks specificity, and the sophisticated Mindvalley audience demands more scientific credibility than the site currently delivers.
