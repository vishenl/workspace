Ad Optimization Strategy

Specific recommendations to improve ad performance

Hook Optimization

The current hook takes ~8 seconds to land: "Imagine if you could get all of the fun of alcohol without actually wasting so much money on alcohol and damaging your health with alcohol."

On Meta, you have 1-3 seconds to capture attention. Here are stronger alternatives:

  • "I stopped drinking. But I didn't stop partying." Direct, intriguing, creates curiosity gap
  • "I threw away my bar. Here's what replaced it." Visual, specific, leverages Vishen's personal story
  • "My friends think I'm drunk. I'm not. I'm on this." Pattern interrupt, social proof implied
  • "2 pills. Zero hangover. Same social energy as 3 drinks." Specific, quantified, benefit-focused
  • "The 'social lubricant' that doesn't destroy your liver." Uses Shawn's language, health-conscious angle

Script Structure Recommendations

Section Current Recommended
Hook (0-3s) Long conditional "Imagine if..." Direct statement with intrigue
Problem (3-10s) Mentioned but not visceral Agitate: hangovers, regret, health damage
Solution (10-25s) Vishen's personal story Keep — this is strong
Credibility (25-40s) Shawn's serotonin explanation Keep — builds trust
CTA (40-45s) Missing Add: "Try BLISS risk-free. 60-day guarantee."

Questions for Shawn Wells

5 questions to gather more compelling marketing content

These questions are designed to extract specific, quantifiable information that can be used in ads and landing page copy to increase credibility and conversions.

  • What's the clinical onset time for each key ingredient, and which ingredient do users "feel" first?

    Why this matters: Specificity sells. If we can say "You'll feel Zembrin working in 45 minutes, then Affron kicks in at 90 minutes," it creates tangible expectations and reduces skepticism. Time-to-effect is a major purchase driver for nootropics.

  • How does BLISS affect serotonin differently than alcohol? Specifically, why doesn't BLISS cause the "serotonin crash" that alcohol does the next day?

    Why this matters: "Hangxiety" (next-day anxiety after drinking) is a massive pain point. If we can explain the mechanism clearly — why alcohol depletes serotonin while BLISS supports it — we have a powerful scientific differentiator for our comparison content.

  • Are there any clinical studies showing social/mood outcomes specifically (not just cognitive)? Any data on user-reported social confidence, ease of conversation, or connection?

    Why this matters: We have cognitive data (memory, focus) but BLISS is positioned as a "social lubricant." User-reported or clinical data on social outcomes would be gold for testimonials and claims. Even survey data from beta users would help.

  • What's the synergy between Affron Saffron and Zembrin? Why did you combine these two specifically for the "bliss" effect?

    Why this matters: The story of "why these ingredients together" is compelling content. If there's a 1+1=3 synergy story, it justifies the premium price and differentiates from single-ingredient competitors. Formula storytelling builds perceived value.

  • For someone who currently drinks 2-3 glasses of wine at social events, what's the recommended BLISS protocol? Should they take it before, can they still have one drink, what's the ideal scenario?

    Why this matters: Most people won't quit alcohol cold turkey. Giving them a "bridge protocol" (e.g., "Take BLISS 1 hour before, have one drink if you want, you'll likely find you don't need it") makes the product more accessible and reduces the perceived commitment required to try it.

"The more specific and scientific we can get about HOW BLISS works, the more we can justify the premium price and differentiate from generic 'mood supplements.' Shawn's expertise is our competitive moat — let's extract as much of it as possible."
