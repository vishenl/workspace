The Alcohol Problem No One Talks About

Here's something most people don't realize about alcohol:

Alcohol is a neurotoxin. And it doesn't actually increase serotonin.

That warm, social feeling you get from a drink? It's not because alcohol is making you happier. It's because alcohol is a depressant that reduces your inhibitions through mild dissociation—essentially disconnecting you slightly from your social anxiety.

The problem is the dose-response curve:

  • A little alcohol: slight reduction in inhibition
  • More alcohol: impaired judgment, slurred speech, sloppy behavior
  • The next day: depleted serotonin, anxiety, low mood, hangover

And there's another problem: alcohol creates a crutch. As the night goes on, you need more and more to maintain the effect. Your compulsion to drink increases throughout the evening.
