Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our formulations and science

What does "clinical dosage" mean?
A clinical dosage is the exact amount of an ingredient that was shown to be effective in peer-reviewed human studies. Many supplements use tiny amounts of ingredients just to list them on the label — we use the amounts that actually work.
Why are patented ingredients better?
Patented ingredients are proprietary compounds that have been researched, tested, and proven effective. They cost more because the company invested in clinical trials. Generic versions often don't have the same quality standards or research backing.
What's wrong with proprietary blends?
Proprietary blends hide the individual dosages of ingredients. A company might list 10 impressive ingredients but use ineffective micro-doses of most of them. We show exact amounts because we have nothing to hide.
Are the formulas safe to take together?
Yes! The formulas are designed to complement each other. FOCUS for morning productivity, ZEN for afternoon stress, BLISS for social situations, DREAM for sleep, and GAZM for intimate moments. Many customers use multiple formulas throughout their day.
How long until I feel results?
Many ingredients work acutely — FOCUS kicks in within 30 minutes, ZEN within 20-30 minutes, GAZM within 30 minutes. Some ingredients like Synapsa® in BLISS build up over 2-4 weeks for maximum effect.
What does third-party testing mean?
Every batch of our products is sent to independent laboratories to verify that what's on the label is what's in the bottle, and to check for contaminants like heavy metals, pesticides, and microbes. This ensures quality and safety.
Why is the melatonin dose so low in DREAM?
Research shows 0.3-0.5mg of melatonin is often more effective than higher doses (3-10mg). Higher doses can cause next-day grogginess and disrupt your body's natural melatonin production. Less is truly more with melatonin.
Can I take these with medications?
While our products use well-researched ingredients, we always recommend consulting with your healthcare provider before combining supplements with prescription medications, especially for mood, sleep, or hormone-related conditions.
