The structure mirrors how humans naturally make decisions: understand the problem, see a new possibility, experience value, then commit.

The 6 Core Principles

Master these before writing a single word

🧠 The Psychology

These principles are the "operating system" that runs underneath your entire masterclass. Violate them and your conversion will suffer—even if your script is technically perfect. They're based on decades of direct response marketing wisdom combined with transformation education insights.

1

Keep it Tight but Leave them Hungry

People often feel masterclass content is enough on its own. Give real value, but create strategic incompleteness. They should think "I need more of this" not "I've got enough to practice."

2

Let them Experience a Breakthrough Immediately

"Result in Advance" isn't advance enough if it takes weeks to see results. Design exercises that create direct, immediate results or shifts—something they feel RIGHT NOW in the masterclass.

3

Explain the Cost of NOT Buying

Christie Marie Sheldon does this masterfully: "How much does it cost to let your life stay the same?" Be honest, sharp, and direct—but not salesy. Make inaction feel expensive.

4

Objection-Case Study Pattern

Don't just list objections—pair them with case studies of people who had the SAME objection and overcame it. This is proof that the objection doesn't have to stop them.

5

Sprinkle Social Proof Throughout

Don't save all testimonials for one section. Scatter short stories and examples of student success throughout the entire masterclass. Constant validation builds cumulative belief.

6

Story-Content Looping

From Lisa Nichols: Tell engaging stories that grip the audience, then lead to a lesson. Use "The Valley" (the dip before the rise) to create emotional investment before the teaching.

Pre-Webinar: The Killer Headline

Everything starts with the right hook

🧠 The Psychology

Your headline is the first filter. It determines WHO shows up and in WHAT state. A great headline pre-frames the entire experience—it sets expectations, creates curiosity, and attracts the right audience while repelling the wrong one. The headline should make the right person think "This is exactly what I need right now."

Headline Framework

Your headline should communicate:

  • The Promise: What transformation will they experience?
  • The Mechanism: What's the unique method or approach?
  • The Timeframe: When can they expect results?
  • The Authority: Why should they trust this source?

✏️ Your Killer Headline

Write your masterclass headline here. Make it specific, benefit-driven, and curiosity-inducing.

✏️ Your Banner Tagline

A shorter supporting statement that reinforces the headline.

1

Intro (Host)

7 minutes maximum

Set the pace, establish credibility, and get commitment

🧠 The Psychology

The first 7 minutes determine whether someone stays or leaves. You're competing against Netflix, social media, and a million distractions. Your job is to create an emotional hook that makes them feel "I'm in the right place" while building enough credibility and curiosity to keep them watching. Energy is contagious—if you're excited, they'll feel it.

1.1 Opening with Story & Excitement (Yet Be Gentle & Relatable)

Come in pumped and excited, but balance it with authenticity. Share the actual honest story of WHY you chose this author. Be vulnerable about your own struggles and failures.

💡 The Balance

Excitement without authenticity = salesy. Authenticity without excitement = boring. You need BOTH.

Example Opening (Unlimited Abundance)

"Hi everyone, this is Vishen Lakhiani and welcome to our new session of Unlimited Abundance with the one and only Christie Marie Sheldon. Now, I don't know how you got here but what I know is that you are here to learn about abundance blocks and to experience healing and clearing off some of your major abundance blocks."

✏️ Your Opening Statement

Use the pattern: "I don't know how you got here...but I know you're here to [TITLE] and [SUBTITLE]"

1.2 Why This Topic, Why Now

Start with the topic and what it is. Give an unarguable truth. You're getting them back in the state that got them to register.

🧠 The Psychology

People register for webinars days or weeks in advance. By the time they show up, they may have forgotten WHY they were excited. This section re-activates that original desire and connects it to current world events or data.

✏️ Why This Topic Now

Connect the topic to current events, survey data from your list, or a pressing problem in the world.

1.3 Introduce Host

Build your credibility stack using these four elements:

  • Personal Results: Visual proof that backs up your claims—statistics, awards, results, recognition
  • Positioning: What makes them conclude you're a "big deal"? Books, associations, companies, experience linked to something they know and respect
  • Celebrity: Do you know famous people with visual proof? Are there niche heroes you can link yourself to?
  • Testimonials: They may believe YOU can do it, but can THEY? Show people like them who succeeded

✏️ Your Credibility Stack

List your personal results, positioning elements, celebrity connections, and key testimonials.

1.4 Why the Guest/Author

(If you have a guest and you're the host) — 3 minutes

Explain why the guest is uniquely qualified using three proof types:

  • Personal Story: Your personal experience with them. (Example: For Wildfit, Vishen shares his body transformation)
  • Fascinating Facts: Unique credentials or achievements. (Example: Michael Beckwith presented an award to Nelson Mandela)
  • Celebrity Proof: Pictures with celebrities, receiving awards, etc.

✏️ Why This Author/Guest

Document the author's unique qualifications, fascinating facts, and celebrity connections.

1.5 Social Proof (5 Case Studies)

Hit them with 5 case studies in the first 90 seconds. These are NOT testimonials—they are structured stories.

🧠 The Psychology

Case studies are more powerful than testimonials because they tell a story with a transformation arc. The viewer sees themselves in the "before" state and gets excited about the "after" state. The P.A.R. formula makes the transformation believable and replicable.

The P.A.R. Formula for Case Studies

  • P - Problem: What was their situation before?
  • A - Application: What did they do/learn?
  • R - Result: What happened after?
Example Case Study Opening

"How did this pharmacist from Adelaide change her life after just 3 days? And how did this single mom from Ohio go from $30K in debt to running a 6-figure business in 8 months?"

✏️ Your 5 Case Studies

Write 5 case studies using the P.A.R. formula. Include pictures if possible.

1.6 Get Them to Commit

Ask people to verbally commit to going all out. This creates psychological investment.

🧠 The Psychology

The Commitment and Consistency principle (Cialdini): Once people make a small commitment, they're more likely to follow through on larger ones. Getting them to say "yes" to paying attention makes them more likely to say "yes" to buying later.

  1. Ask them to commit to paying attention and absorbing everything. Hint on "Showing up vs Playing Full Out"
  2. Tell them to get rid of all distractions—Skype, FB, Instagram, etc.
  3. Get them to start saying "Yes" in their head. Make it fun—use phrases like "Hell yeah!", "Count me in!", "Woo Hoo!"

✏️ Your Commitment Statement

Write the exact words you'll use to get their commitment.

1.7 Make Them Rate Themselves (Optional)

This should match your Outcomes Engine precisely. Grab from Sales Copy under "What You'll Learn."

Ask two types of questions:

  • Self-Analysis: "On a scale of 1-10, how would you rate yourself on..."
  • Desire: "On a scale of 1-10, how much would you want to gain these abilities..."
Example (Love Energies)

"1. Better Self Awareness: I am aware of my own energies and how they affect my partner and our relationship.

2. Increasing Emotional Intelligence: I successfully manage my emotions, soothe myself, and come back to center when I experience relationship stress."

✏️ Your Self-Rating Questions

List 3-5 outcomes they can rate themselves on.

1.8 Bring On the Guest

First, the host talks about why the guest is uniquely qualified. Then the guest introduces themselves and shares their story.

⏱ Time Check

All of Part 1 should be completed in 5-8 minutes maximum. No more!

2

Introduce the Trainer & Problem

5 minutes

Deep dive into the author's story and agitate the problem

🧠 The Psychology

Before people will buy a solution, they need to feel the WEIGHT of the problem. This section uses story to create emotional connection with the author, then agitates the problem so viewers feel the cost of NOT solving it. The author's vulnerability ("Before I became who I am...") creates trust and relatability.

2.1 The Author's Story & Big Why

Adopt the Valley Model from Lisa Nichols. Start with vulnerability:

Opening Pattern

"Before I became who I am, you should know that..."

Ensure the author shares their BIG WHY—their mission and why they do what they do. This creates emotional investment and trust.

✏️ The Author's Story (Valley Model)

What was the author's "valley" moment? What transformation did they experience?

✏️ The Author's Big Why

What mission drives this person? Why do they dedicate their life to this work?

2.2 Explain the Problem

Create Curiosity/Excitement

Use the pattern: "You're going to discover XXX... and (something you wouldn't imagine to be true)"

Exacerbate the Problem

In real terms, why does this impact the viewer? What is the negative impact on their:

  • Finances
  • Happiness
  • Relationships
  • Career
  • Health
⚠️ Don't Give Away the Agenda

Don't blatantly say "First we'll do X, then Y..." Keep it sounding like a conversation. Use mystery and curiosity. Talk more about the BENEFITS of what they'll learn. It IS okay to hint at an exercise at the end to keep people hooked.

Give the Audience a Target

Tell them they're going to see how amazing the results are and how easy it is with a special exercise later. This gives them something to look out for and keeps them watching.

✏️ The Problem Statement

What is the core problem your audience faces? How does it impact their life?

2.3 Ask Them to Define Their Why

Think back about that outcome you selected. WHY is it important to you?

Ask them to take a second to think about their big why, then write it down. This is another commitment you're asking them to make.

Prompt with questions:

  • Why is this important to you?
  • Why do you need to make a change in your life?
  • Is it because you have unmet potential?
  • Is it because you want the best relationship ever?
  • Is it because you want to contribute to the world?

Then congratulate them: "Your life is about to change. Your shift starts NOW."

2.4 Handle Objections in Advance

🧠 The Psychology

Every viewer has objections running in the back of their mind. If you don't address them, those objections become reasons NOT to buy. By naming them first, you take away their power. The viewer thinks "Wow, they understand me" rather than "They're trying to sell me something."

The 8 Categories of Objections

Choose 3-5 that apply to your product:

Belief

You've tried to solve this before and failed. Why should this time be different?

Ability

Could you even learn this given all the time and resources in the world?

Trust

Does it sound too good to be true? Why should they have faith in you?

Fear

Fear of change, failure, or the unknown. In reality, the only thing to fear is staying put.

Conflict

They want change but don't see how without betraying who they are.

Confusion

Where do I start? How do I finish? Can I do it if [unique situation]?

Timing

Is now the right time? Did they miss the boat? Is it too early?

Length of Time

How long will this take? (Usually just 10 mins/day—less than time on social media!)

The 3-Step Objection Reframing Process

  1. Acknowledge & Soften: "You may be wondering if you can or can't do what I'm going to share with you today."
  2. Validate & Reframe: "That is a valid concern because what you really want to know is... will this time be different? Will you finally get the solution you've been looking for?"
  3. Present a Target: "I believe you can, and as we go through the presentation, I'm going to show you how this unique approach works for people from all different backgrounds with their own unique challenges. Look for that and it should help you see that you can do this."

✏️ Your Top 3-5 Objections & Responses

List the objections most relevant to your product and how you'll address them.

2.5 Give Hope

🧠 The Psychology

You never speak to them directly—you speak to the little voice in them. "Now you may be saying to yourself..." Nothing is as powerful as their self-talk. This plants positive thoughts in their inner dialogue.

Get them to visualize the solution in the present tense. Use the line:

Power Question

"Imagine if you mastered this skill, where could you be 3 years from now? 3 months?"

5 Hope Frameworks (from Fladlien)

  1. Money: "If I transferred $100 million to your account, what would you do today? This week? This year? What charitable things would you pursue?"
  2. Time: "What will you do with all this free time when the problem is no longer something you think about? What noble pursuits open up?"
  3. Trends: "By 2025, there will be X billion people doing Y. What do you think happens to those who are ready in advance?"
  4. The Magic Wand: "If I could wave a magic wand over your [problem] and it was magically gone, what would you immediately notice? How would your day-to-day be different?"
  5. Belief: "Do you believe, truly believe, that you are capable of ___? And do you deserve it?"

✏️ Your Hope Statement

Give them a target of a new YOU in their mind.

2.6 Hint at Solution & Create Intrigue

This is where you create an Open Loop for the future. Use phrases like "BUT there is another way."

Example (Lifebook)

"At the end of this masterclass you will learn how to take Lifebook, the ENTIRE program. And you DO NOT have to pay for it. Hold on till the end of this Masterclass and I'll tell you how..."

For Live Webinars

  • Promise something that will only be shown live and edited out of the recording
  • Talk about how a recent event changed things dramatically, which you'll reveal later
  • Create suspense by admitting you're doing something you've never done before

✏️ Your Open Loop

What will you tease that keeps them watching until the end?

⏱ Time Check - Part 2

All of Part 2 should be completed in 5 minutes.

3

What You Know is Incomplete

5 minutes

Break the old paradigm and shift their thinking

🧠 The Psychology

Before people can accept a new solution, they need to reject the old one. This section creates a "pattern interrupt" in their thinking by identifying a common belief (BRULE = Bullshit Rule) that's been holding them back. When the old paradigm crumbles, they're ready to embrace something new.

3.1 Context and Present BRULE (Bullshit Rule)

Talk about the commonly accepted solution and the little good it has done for the world. What is everyone doing that isn't working?

✏️ What is the Common Belief?

What does everyone think is the solution that actually isn't working?

✏️ What is the Common Enemy?

What external force, industry, or system keeps this false belief alive?

Then transition: "But... this solution couldn't go into the depth it needed to. It focused on just one thing... but there is more you need to know."

3.2 The Paradigm Shift

Expand upon why the current solution isn't enough and reveal the answer.

🧠 The Psychology

We need to make the audience feel as if the future will be worse if they do not take action now. We also need to make them feel like they are not alone. "We are all in this together" creates connection and reduces the fear of change.

✏️ The New Paradigm

What is the NEW way of thinking that makes everything click?

3.3 The Defined Outcome

What's the ONE thing they will now be learning to do?

Give them a roadmap of what will be covered and the results to expect. When going through every learning, tell them WHY this is important to their success.

✏️ By the End of This Class You Will Learn To:

State the clear, specific outcome they can expect.

⏱ Time Check - Part 3

All of Part 3 should be completed in 5 minutes.

4

Straight Up Content

20-40 minutes

Deliver real value with exercises and case studies

🧠 The Psychology

This is where you earn the right to make an offer. The content must be genuinely valuable—people who don't buy should still feel satisfied and come back. But there's a balance: give enough to create belief, not enough to feel "complete." The emotional states you evoke here determine buying behavior later.

💡 Timing Notes

Get to the first HOW in 15 minutes. Cover it for 15 minutes. Gloss over the next 4 with case studies and trial closes for 25 minutes. Trial close happens around minute 35-55.

Before You Plan Your Lesson: Answer These 3 Questions

✏️ Audience

What specific audience are you going to serve?

✏️ Feeling

What are they going to FEEL as they implement what you teach?

✏️ Result

What outcome would your audience consider a success?

4.1.1 Choosing the Right Emotional States

🧠 The Psychology

The emotional state someone is in determines whether they'll buy. Whittle down your content to ask: "What sections will keep people in the BEST emotional states to buy later?" Some emotions help conversion, others kill it.

❌ Fear

If scared, they won't buy. Make fear of inaction stronger than fear of trying.

❌ Confusion

Present content in a very organized way. Confused minds don't buy.

✅ Enthusiasm

How to allow them to act on this TONIGHT!

✅ Safety

Teach in a way that eliminates downsides. Trust creates safety.

✅ Anger (Channeled)

Direct anger at the common enemy. "Doesn't it make you mad we don't learn this in schools?"

✅ Connection

"You will not be alone." Combat loneliness with community.

✏️ Which States Are You Injecting?

Choose the emotional states you'll consciously create in your content.

4.1.2 The Steps and How To #1

Teach the Most Important Item First (even if it's out of order)

Never rely on your author to know the most important step. YOU should know from your survey. If Step 5 is most important based on survey data, cover Step 5 first.

💡 Pro Tip

Ask the guru: "What portion do you get the MOST kudos from in prior webinars?" In a great sales webinar, people who didn't buy ALSO feel satisfied, so they come back. Go DEEP with one step to create tension.

Teaching Framework for Each Step:

  1. Explain the concept
  2. Give an exercise
  3. Share a case study of client success
  4. Share another case study
  5. Recap the step
💡 Transformation Praxis Modalities

Include at least one: Critical Self-Reflection, Learning/Watching, Rate of Application, Social Discourse, or Altered States (like meditation).

✏️ Step 1 - Concept

What is the first (or most important) concept you'll teach?

✏️ Step 1 - Exercise

What exercise will they do to experience this concept?

✏️ Step 1 - Case Studies

What success stories demonstrate this concept?

4.1.3 The CVSC Framework for Each Step

🧠 The Psychology

Each teaching step should follow Context → Vision → Strategy → Commitment. This sequence builds understanding, creates desire, delivers value, and locks in agreement before moving to the next step.

Context

WHY is this step important?

Vision

Make them see it in their mind

Strategy

The actual HOW (30-35% of content)

Commitment

"Do you believe you can do this?"

Context Example

From Jim Rohn: A martial arts instructor says "You can't know what it's like to walk down the street and not feel afraid." Jim's response: "Sign me up!" Until you SEE the promise, you won't endure the pain.

Vision Pattern

Start with "Picture this..." Make them see a slice of life with this tool in their hands. You can also paint a vision with a micro-story.

Commitment Phrases

  • "The power of this technique is in its simplicity. Do you believe you can do this in just 5 mins a day?"
  • "Do you think this, rather than what you're currently doing, will yield better results?"
  • "Deal?" "Agreed?" "Got it?"

After Commitment, transition with: "Now that you have learned X... it's time to Y." This gives them ownership.

4.1.4 Cover Steps 2-5 (Gloss Over)

Remember: You went DEEP on Step 1. Now cover the remaining steps more briefly with case studies and trial closes.

✏️ Step 2

✏️ Step 3

✏️ Step 4

✏️ Step 5

Trial Close Questions to Scatter Throughout:

  • "Let me see hands if you're thinking about doing this with me... Anyone not?"
  • "How many of you wish they knew this information 5 years ago? 5 months? 5 days?"

This section should take 25-35 minutes. You should be at approximately minute 45 now.

5

Recap & Transition into Close

5-10 minutes

Summarize value, create yes momentum, and bridge to the offer

🧠 The Psychology

This section has three jobs: (1) Remind them of all the value they received, (2) Get them saying "yes" repeatedly to prime the buying decision, and (3) Create a clear choice between two paths. The phrase "So far" implies there's more to come.

5.1 Recap: "So Far We Have Covered..."

Give a 60-second recap before you go into the half-dozen yesses.

Recap Pattern

"In our brief time together today, I've given you the exact formula that I've spent years perfecting. [List all the benefits you've given them]. All this and much more in the limited time we had today."

✏️ Your Recap Statement

Summarize all steps/benefits covered.

5.2 Show the Possibilities of the Full Training

Hint on the product. Create curiosity and convince them that what's being revealed is important to practice, but DON'T give too much "how to implement" training.

💡 Quick Method

Paste highlighted chapters from the Quest into slides. "Don't worry if you can't recall all 10 daily routines. It's all in the Quest."

✏️ Product Hint

How will you tease the full program?

5.3 More Social Proof

Talk about people you know who implemented these changes. Add more case studies and testimonials.

Powerful Line

"[Author name], think about it... some of your next success stories could be on this call right now."

5.4 The Two Choices: Two Paths

🧠 The Psychology

This is a classic persuasion technique: reduce complexity to a binary choice. The biggest mistake they can make is ignoring the information and NOT applying it. That's the real danger. By framing two clear paths, you make the decision simple.

Transition Pattern

"Let me ask you a question—you've spent this time with me so far, and you've learned some amazing stuff, but how would you like me to help you beyond that? How would you like to achieve this, and this, and this?"

Paint the Two Paths:

  • Path 1: Do nothing (everything stays the same)
  • Path 2: Live in alignment with this new knowledge. Experience true [BENEFITS]
Example (Alex)

"Imagine if you had the other 4... in addition to the one we just gave you. What would happen if you did not continue? What would happen if you did continue?"

✏️ Path 1 - Do Nothing

What happens if they continue where they are?

✏️ Path 2 - Take Action

What transformation awaits if they continue with you?

5.5 The Yes Momentum

🧠 The Psychology

Ask them 6 questions to get them to say "yes" 6 times. This builds commitment and consistency. When you can't see their face, use this phrase: "I want to ask you a couple of questions and all I want to know is: Does the word 'YES' pop into your mind?"

Example Yes Questions:

  • "Do you feel confident harnessing the power of X?"
  • "Were you happy you came for the webinar today?"
  • "Do you realize the advantages of X and how it can improve your life?"
  • "Can you see yourself coming back to these lessons to keep improving?"
  • "Even with this short session, would you say this time was well spent?"
  • "The power of this technique is in its simplicity. This can be done in just 5 mins a day. Agree?"

✏️ Your 6 Yes Questions

Then transition: "Now if the answer is YES to any of those, I want to invite you to enroll in [PROGRAM NAME]..."

6

The Close

15-20 minutes

Present the offer, drop the price, stack bonuses, and call to action

🧠 The Psychology

The close isn't about pressure—it's about helping them make a decision they already want to make. You've built desire through content, now you're simply showing them the path to get what they want. The price drop creates a "deal" feeling, bonuses add unexpected value, and the guarantee removes risk.

The Close Summary Checklist

  • Offer: Product name, tagline, benefit statement and highlight
  • Prices: Link to high $$, drop it, drop it again, reveal, call to action
  • Bonuses: Headline, 3 benefits proof, future pace
  • Guarantee: Reverse the risk or turn it into a benefit
  • Objections: Validate it, reframe it, shatter it, re-engage
  • Scarcity: The penalty for waiting to sign up
💡 Trial Closes vs Hard Closes

No hard closes at 15, 30, 45, 60 min marks. Instead, scatter TRIAL CLOSES throughout. Don't say "Keep listening and you'll be given a way to get your course etc." Nowadays, you LOSE sales because of too much honesty about selling.

6.1 Introduce Product (5 minutes)

✏️ Product Name

✏️ Product Tagline

Key Ideas for Each Section

Walk them through what's in the program. Show what they're getting.

💡 Product Tour with Shortcuts (Fladlien)

"You can access intuition at Theta level of mind (lesson), and if you want to get there FAST, this is how the program trains you to do it."

✏️ Program Sections/Weeks

6.2 Share Benefits (Derived from Outcomes on Sales Copy)

Encapsulate the totality of benefits in ONE statement:

✏️ One Sentence Benefit Statement

Highlight Major Components (One Benefit Per Slide)

✏️ List 5 Core Benefits

6.3 Future Pacing

🧠 The Psychology

Future pacing helps them emotionally experience the result BEFORE they buy. When they can "feel" the transformation, the purchase becomes an investment in something they've already started experiencing. This is derived from the "Who You Will Be" section of your sales copy.

Benefit + Future Pace Pattern

BENEFIT: Develop Crystal-Clear Intuition

FUTURE PACE: You Make The Right Choices With Remarkable Consistency. You have an uncanny ability to intuitively make the right choices and create your own luck. Uncertainty and second-guessing yourself are now a distant memory.

✏️ Your Future Pacing Statements

For each major benefit, describe who they become.

6.4 Social Proof

Flash the testimonials from your Sales Copy. Grab the 5x15 testimonials that relate to the benefits you just mentioned.

6.5 Drop the Price

Price Comparison Methods:

  • Live Event Comparison: "Recently we sold seminar access for $4,995 at the Ritz Carlton. With travel and hotel, people spent around $8,000. But not everyone can afford that..."
  • One-on-One Coaching: "An 8-week coaching with [Author] would typically cost $10,000..."
  • Economies of Scale: "Regular companies charge $1,000 for education like this. We get such high economies of scale we can drop price to $400."

Step Ladder Price Drop

Don't go from $4,000 to $399. You need to step down gradually.

1

Anchor with high comparison ($8,000 live event)

2

First drop (Regular price: $1,500)

3

Second drop (Special price: $995)

4

Webinar Unlock Price (Today only: $495)

⚠️ Never Say "SAVE"

Jason Fladlien: "Say things like 'You should be LUCKY to be able to get this at this price. I negotiated a special deal for this.'"

Price Framing

Once you reveal the price, FRAME it to reduce friction:

  • Divide price over 8 weeks to get daily value: "That's less than the cost of a Starbucks muffin per day."
  • "All Access is just $2 a day. What's that for you? Less than a cup of coffee."

✏️ Your Price Drop Sequence

6.6 Bonuses

"You're not just getting XXX. You also get these amazing bonuses."

🧠 The Psychology

Keep stacking value, adding to the dollar value. Keep going back to the original offer slide with a new element added each time. The REPETITION is key. If a bonus is REALLY good, bring it to the front.

Standard Mindvalley Bonuses to Include:

  • Bonus #1: The Platform - Quest Platform works everywhere, world-class production, microlearning in minutes a day
  • Bonus #2: Community - Access to the tribe
  • Bonus #3: Special Audios - Meditations, exercises, etc.
  • Bonus #4: Live Calls (when available)

✏️ Your Bonuses

6.7 The Guarantee

🧠 The Psychology

Very few will claim money back. They just want to know that YOU believe in what you're selling. The guarantee removes risk and demonstrates confidence. "You know you have a winning offer when you're thinking—these people have got to be idiots NOT to buy what I've got."

Guarantee Sharpening Options:

  • The 800lb Gorilla "Blame Me": Take full responsibility for their success

7

Crushing Objections

10+ minutes

Handle resistance, create urgency, and close strong

🧠 The Psychology

Pattern Interrupt is a technique to change a particular thought or behavior. A pattern is a meta-process "turned on" by a specific stimulus (a "trigger"). By addressing objections before someone brings them up, you interrupt the negative pattern and redirect their attention.

7.1 The Pattern Interrupt Formula

  1. Approach the objection head-on before they bring it up: "You might be thinking that this is too expensive..."
  2. Then redirect their attention with something UNEXPECTED: "...but not more expensive than [X]."
  3. Tie it back to your solution.
Pattern Interrupt Examples

Prospect: "I'm not ready to buy yet."
Response: "That's right, you're not ready to buy yet, because you haven't asked me the one question that will have you totally and completely ready to go home with this today."

⚠️ Golden Rule

NEVER argue or pressure your prospect. This will cause them to reject you out of their reality bubble. Instead, respect their reality, enter it, and do a subtle shift.

Common Questions to Address:

  1. How fast do you see results?
  2. How can I be sure it works?
  3. Am I really getting a discount?
  4. How much time is needed?
  5. Tell me about the bonus.

Objection Killer Examples:

  • Ability: "If you really want to make a change, you can learn this. It's all in the process. At one time, you couldn't even walk, talk, or go to the bathroom by yourself. You had to learn."
  • Fear: "I get it, it's scary to step out of your comfort zone. But you and I know that if you do what you've always done, you'll get what you always got. I'm not asking you to set aside your fear. I'm asking you to make your WHY bigger than your FEAR."

7.2 Scarcity

🧠 The Psychology

Don't FAKE scarcity—ENGINEER it into your business model. Offers without scarcity don't sell as well. The fear of loss is always greater than the desire to succeed. Even Disney does this: "We're going to sell Cinderella on VHS till Christmas. On New Year's Eve, it goes back in the vault."

Scarcity Tactics:

  • Remind them of what they've already lost: "Every day you're not sleeping, you're cutting your performance by 40%. Every day you're shaving 3 days off your life."
  • Remind them how quickly they're still losing: Make the ongoing cost visceral.
  • Take away the bonuses: "The product will be here—but past tomorrow at 2pm, you will no longer get the bonuses."
  • Take away the opportunity: Create genuine urgency.
