Our Story

Started in a Hamburg Kitchen. Now Loved Worldwide.

In 2019, we were two moms looking for a better way to keep our phones close while chasing toddlers. When we couldn't find one, we made our own.

Today, Upbeads employs over 20 working mothers who hand-knot each chain from home. Every bead is sustainably sourced. Every purchase plants a tree.

We're not just making phone chains. We're building a community of women supporting women.

Sophie & Michel Founders, Upbeads
