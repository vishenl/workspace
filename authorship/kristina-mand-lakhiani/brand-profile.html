Brand Identity & Positioning

Core Philosophy: "Flawesome"

Flaws + Awesome = Flawesome

  • Embracing your fully awesome self, including all imperfections
  • Living an "imperfectly authentic life"
  • Your flaws are not things to fix, but sources of strength and self-discovery

Positioning Statement

Kristina Mand-Lakhiani is the co-founder of Mindvalley and Wall Street Journal best-selling author who helps ambitious high-achievers and perfectionists achieve authentic self-acceptance and personal freedom through the philosophy of "Flawesome living," unlike traditional self-help that focuses on fixing yourself because she believes your greatest potential comes from embracing who you already are - flaws and all.

Brand Voice & Tone

Sophisticated Yet Accessible: Philosophical without being academic. Deep without being heavy. Vulnerable without being weak. Inspirational without being preachy.

Content & Methodology

The Flawesome Philosophy - Three Pillars

🗺️ Honesty (The Map)

You can't know where you're going until you're honest with yourself. Radical self-honesty about beliefs, values, feelings. Awareness of masks you wear.

⛵ Kindness (The Boat)

Self-compassion as vehicle for transformation. Forgiveness of self and others. Integrating honesty with kindness. Moving beyond self-judgment.

🌊 Courage (The Sail)

Courage to live your truth. Facing fear of rejection and judgment. Taking leaps of faith. Showing up authentically.

Dragons Concept

"Meeting Your Dragons"

  • Dragons = your flaws, shadows, imperfections
  • Instead of slaying dragons, befriend them
  • Your dragons hold your greatest strengths
  • Transform shame into power

Core Teachings

1. Authenticity Beyond the Buzzword

Most people confuse authenticity with oversharing. True authenticity = alignment between inner truth and outer expression. Multiple layers to authenticity (like an onion).

2. The Perfectionism Trap

Perfectionism is a mask to avoid vulnerability. High-achievers use perfectionism as protection. The "Perfect You" vs "The Real You." Cost of perfectionism: disconnection from self.

3. Emotional Mastery

ABC of emotions (Awareness, Being with them, Conscious response). Emotional first aid techniques. Moving from reactive to proactive with emotions.

4. Living By Your Own Rules (LBYOR)

Identify societal expectations vs. your truth. Uncover "Brules" (Bulls**t Rules) you've accepted. Redefine success, happiness, love on your terms. Create conscious choices aligned with authentic self.

Personal Story & Brand Narrative

Origin Story Arc

Act 1: Soviet Childhood

Born and raised in Estonia (Soviet Union). Oppressive system with strict rules. Gender programming and perfectionism. Cultural conditioning around "proper" behavior. Lack of freedom and self-expression.

Act 2: Career & Government Work

Estonian government position. Non-profit sector (UN, Oxfam, AIESEC). High-achieving but unfulfilled. Following "should" path.

Act 3: Love & Mindvalley

Decided to leave high-level job. Moved across Atlantic for love. Co-founded Mindvalley with husband Vishen. Built company to $100M+ valuation. Had two children (Hayden and Eve).

Act 4: Success But...

Outward success but inner struggle. Perfectionism persisted despite achievements. Maintaining masks and roles. People-pleasing and expectations.

Act 5: Crisis & Transformation

Divorce from Vishen. Lost powerful business partnership. COVID pandemic (moved countries). Forced to face shadow side. Rock bottom moment.

Act 6: Becoming Flawesome

Chose to embrace flaws instead of hide them. Wrote book during lockdown. Discovered authentic self. Living by own rules. Teaching others the path.

What Makes Her Relatable

  • Not presenting "perfect" life
  • Admits ongoing work on herself
  • Vulnerable about mistakes
  • Shows imperfect moments
  • Accessible despite success
