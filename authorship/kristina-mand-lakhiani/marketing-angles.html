Core Marketing Angles

1

The Perfectionism Trap

"Why Successful People Are Often the Most Miserable"
🎯 Target: High-achievers, Type A personalities, overachievers

Core Message: You achieved everything you were "supposed to" - the degree, the job, the house. You check all the boxes. But you're miserable. Why? Because you climbed a ladder that was leaning against the wrong wall.

Perfectionism is not a virtue - it's a prison
The more you achieve, the emptier you feel
Success without self-acceptance is torture
Your inner critic is destroying you
Recognition → Relief → Hope
Content Ideas
  • Video: "Confessions of a Recovering Perfectionist"
  • Article: "I Built a $100M Company and Still Felt Like a Failure"
  • Workshop: "Breaking Free from the Perfectionism Prison"
  • Quiz: "How Severe Is Your Perfectionism?"

"If you're exhausted from trying to be perfect, this is for you."

2

The Masks We Wear

"Have You Forgotten Who You Really Are?"
🎯 Target: Identity seekers, life transitioners, authenticity seekers

Core Message: You've played so many roles - the good employee, the perfect parent, the dutiful child - that you've forgotten who you actually are beneath it all. Every morning you put on masks. Every interaction is a performance.

You've become what others wanted
The "Grand Mask Paradox"
Your life looks perfect but feels fraudulent
The cost of maintaining the image
Recognition → Grief → Liberation

"Ready to take off the mask and meet the real you?"

3

Your Dragons Are Your Power

"What If Your Flaws Are Actually Your Greatest Strengths?"
🎯 Target: Shadow work curious, spiritual seekers, psychology enthusiasts

Core Message: Everything you've tried to hide, fix, or eliminate contains your greatest power. Your "dragons" - your flaws, shadows, imperfections - aren't meant to be slayed. They're meant to be befriended.

Dragons = flaws, shadows, imperfections
What you resist, persists
Jung's shadow work in modern context
Your shame contains your gifts
Curiosity → Recognition → Empowerment

"Are you brave enough to meet your dragons?"

4

Brules (Bullshit Rules)

"Life By Whose Rules?"
🎯 Target: Rebels, non-conformists, entrepreneurs, questioners

Core Message: You're living by rules you never agreed to. Rules about success, relationships, parenting, career - rules handed down from family, culture, society. "Brules." Time to write your own.

Question everything you "should" do
Whose dreams are you living?
Permission to disappoint people
Define success on YOUR terms

"What would you do if you stopped caring what 'they' think?"

5

The Success-Happiness Divide

"Stop Making the Impossible Choice Between Success and Wellbeing"
🎯 Target: Burnout entrepreneurs, workaholics, hustle culture victims

Core Message: You've been sold a lie: that you must sacrifice happiness for success. But what if there was another way? What if success built on self-acceptance was both more sustainable AND more fulfilling?

The false choice: success OR wellbeing
Hustle culture is toxic
Burnout is not a badge of honor
You can have both

"Ready to succeed without sacrificing your soul?"

6

The Flawesome Philosophy

"Flaws + Awesome = Flawesome"
🎯 Target: Personal growth enthusiasts, Mindvalley audience

Core Message: Forget self-improvement. Forget fixing yourself. You're not broken. You're flawesome - flawed and awesome at the same time. Your imperfections aren't bugs; they're features.

Flawesome = Flaws + Awesome
Reframing imperfections as assets
You're already whole, right now
The freedom of full acceptance

"Ready to become flawesome?"

Core Marketing Principles

10 Principles for Kristina's Brand Marketing

  1. Lead with vulnerability and story - Authenticity is the brand
  2. Address specific pain (perfectionism) directly - Don't be generic
  3. Offer paradigm shift - Acceptance > Improvement
  4. Provide clear framework - Honesty, Kindness, Courage
  5. Use "Flawesome" as memorable hook - It's ownable and catchy
  6. Leverage Mindvalley platform - 20M reach is a superpower
  7. Balance sophistication with accessibility - Smart but not elitist
  8. Always include strong social proof - Numbers and testimonials
  9. Multi-touch conversion paths - Book → Quest → Membership
  10. Community building as core strategy - Not just transactions
