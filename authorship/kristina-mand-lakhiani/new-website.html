The Flawesome Philosophy

Flaws + Awesome = Flawesome

Unlike traditional self-help that focuses on fixing yourself, I believe your greatest potential comes from embracing who you already are - flaws and all.

Your flaws are not things to fix. They are doorways to your power. The journey from perfectionism to authentic self-acceptance is the path to true fulfillment.
🗺

Honesty

The Map

You can't know where you're going until you're honest with yourself. Radical self-honesty about your beliefs, values, and the masks you wear.

⛵

Kindness

The Boat

Self-compassion is the vehicle for transformation. Moving beyond self-judgment through forgiveness of self and others.

🌊

Courage

The Sail

The courage to live your truth. Facing the fear of rejection and judgment to show up authentically in every moment.

I've Been Where You Are

I co-built a $100M+ company while battling perfectionism. I survived a public divorce while maintaining authenticity. I emerged stronger - now teaching what I learned the hard way.

As an entrepreneur, Wall Street Journal best-selling author, speaker, and mother, I've spent 20+ years in personal transformation - collaborating with the world's leading thinkers in consciousness, relationships, and human performance.

Born in Soviet Estonia, I learned early what it means to live by others' rules. My journey from perfectionist achiever to "flawesome" human wasn't linear - it was forged through real challenges, real failures, and real transformation.
