Driving to 7 Days to Flawesome

50+ short-form video ideas with hooks, story angles, and CTAs designed to build audience and drive traffic to Kristina's YouTube challenge series

The Social-to-YouTube Flywheel

This strategy uses short-form content (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) as top-of-funnel awareness drivers that lead viewers into the 7 Days to Flawesome YouTube challenge. Each short video is a "taste" of transformation that creates hunger for the full experience.

The Pattern: Viral Hook → Insight/Story → "Want the full transformation? Join my 7 Days to Flawesome challenge on YouTube"

Posting Strategy

  • Frequency: 1-2 posts per day across platforms
  • Mix: 40% Stories, 30% Educational, 20% Quotes, 10% Behind-the-scenes
  • Best Times: 6-9am and 7-10pm local time for target audience
  • Engagement: Reply to comments within first hour to boost algorithm
  • Cross-post: Same content adapted for each platform's format
