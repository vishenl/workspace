🐉 Pillar 1 Script

Meet Your Dragons

4 short-form videos designed to reframe flaws as power sources and drive viewers to the 7 Days to Flawesome challenge

💜 Dear Kristina

These scripts are designed for how you naturally speak - from the heart, not a teleprompter. Each video has one key line to remember (marked with 💬) and bullet points you can riff on in your own words. The editing team will handle the rest. Just be yourself - vulnerable, wise, and real. That's your superpower.

1 Your Flaws Are Not Bugs - They're Features

⏱️ 60-90 seconds 📱 TikTok / Reels / Shorts
🎯 Hook (First 3 Seconds)
Every fairy tale got it wrong. The dragon isn't guarding the treasure. The dragon IS the treasure.
Start with confident energy, slight smile, like you're about to share a secret
💜 Your Message

Talk about these ideas in your own words:

  • We're taught our whole lives to "slay our dragons" - fix our flaws, hide our shadows
  • But what if everything we've been told is backwards?
  • Your dragons - your flaws, your shadows, your "imperfections" - they're not enemies
  • They're parts of you that have been waiting to be befriended
  • The things you're most ashamed of? Often your greatest sources of power
You cannot shame yourself out of being you. You can only hate yourself trying.
✨ Close

Wrap up with:

  • Brief pause, softer energy
  • Invite them into your world
I go deeper on this in my 7 Days to Flawesome challenge. Link in bio if you're ready to meet your dragons.

2 The Building With the Beautiful Facade

⏱️ 60-90 seconds 📱 TikTok / Reels / Shorts
🎯 Hook (First 3 Seconds)
Imagine a beautiful building. Stunning facade. But the basement? Full of darkness no one's allowed to see.
Thoughtful, almost confessional tone - like sharing something personal
💜 Your Message

Talk about these ideas in your own words:

  • Most of us are like this building
  • We spend SO much energy polishing the outside - the part people see
  • Meanwhile, there's a basement (or attic) full of things we're terrified to look at
  • Our shadows. Our shame. Our "unacceptable" parts
  • Here's the problem: maintaining that facade is EXHAUSTING
  • And the basement doesn't go away just because we ignore it
The energy you spend hiding who you really are... is energy you could be using to actually LIVE.
✨ Close

Wrap up with:

  • Gentle invitation, not pressure
  • Connect to transformation
I teach how to clean that basement - gently - in my 7 Days to Flawesome challenge. It's free on YouTube.

⚡ B-Roll Options

3 You Cannot Shame Yourself Out of Being You

⏱️ 45-60 seconds 📱 TikTok / Reels / Shorts
🎯 Hook (First 3 Seconds)
You cannot shame yourself out of being you.
Direct eye contact. Firm but compassionate. Like you're telling them something they need to hear.
💜 Your Message

Talk about these ideas in your own words:

  • Read that again. (Pause for effect)
  • You cannot shame yourself out of being you
  • You can only hate yourself trying
  • Every time you criticize that part of yourself you don't like...
  • Every time you try to "fix" your flaws through self-punishment...
  • You're not getting better. You're just getting more exhausted.
What if the answer isn't to fight yourself... but to finally make peace with yourself?
✨ Close
Day 5 of my 7 Days to Flawesome challenge is all about this. Link in bio.

🎬 Editing Notes

📹 Visual

0:00Tight close-up for hook - this is powerful

0:05Hold on face, let the words land

✏️ Text Overlay
  • Hook line as text - appear word by word
  • "Read that again" with pause
  • Consider full-screen text moment
🎵 Audio

Minimal or no music - let her words carry the weight

⚡ Pacing

This one is shorter and punchier. Don't over-edit. Let the silence breathe.

4 From Perfectionist to Flawesome (Personal Story)

⏱️ 90-120 seconds 📱 TikTok / Reels / Shorts
🎯 Hook (First 3 Seconds)
I built a hundred million dollar company. I had the perfect family, the perfect career, the perfect life. And I was absolutely miserable.
Vulnerable confession energy. This is the real you sharing the real struggle.
💜 Your Story

Share your journey (in your own words):

  • At 40, I had everything I was "supposed" to have
  • Beautiful family - a boy and a girl
  • Co-founder of Mindvalley - career success beyond my dreams
  • An "Instagrammable" life. A beautiful facade.
  • But something was terribly wrong inside
  • I remember a colleague said "I missed you" and without thinking I replied: "I missed me too"
  • That's when I knew I'd lost myself in the pursuit of perfection
I had spent so long trying to be the perfect version of myself... that I'd forgotten who I actually was.
✨ The Shift

What changed:

  • I stopped trying to slay my dragons
  • I started befriending them instead
  • I learned that my flaws weren't problems - they were doorways
  • That's when I became "Flawesome"
🎯 Close
If this resonates, join me for 7 days of becoming flawesome. It's free on YouTube. Link in bio.
