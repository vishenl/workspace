Quick Reference

Essential brand information at a glance for marketing decisions

Core Philosophy

"Flawesome" — Your greatest potential comes from self-acceptance, not self-improvement. Your flaws are not problems; they're sources of power.

Target Audience

Successful perfectionists (ages 28-55, 65% female) who look successful externally but struggle internally with self-doubt, anxiety, and feeling unfulfilled.

Unique Position

"The Anti-Perfectionism Philosopher for High-Achievers" — combining entrepreneurial credibility with philosophical depth and vulnerable authenticity.

The 3 Pillars

  • Honesty — Get real with yourself (The Map)
  • Kindness — Be compassionate with yourself (The Boat)
  • Courage — Live your truth (The Sail)

Key Differentiators

  • Built $100M+ company (real credibility)
  • Unique "Flawesome" concept
  • Soviet background perspective
  • Lived transformation (divorce, reinvention)

Product Ecosystem

  • "Becoming Flawesome" — WSJ #1 Bestseller

  • Speaking Engagements

Top Marketing Angles

Proven hooks and messaging frameworks for campaigns

1

The Perfectionism Trap

"Why Successful People Are Often the Most Miserable"

Target high-achievers feeling empty despite external success. Message: Success without self-acceptance is torture.

2

The Masks We Wear

"Have You Forgotten Who You Really Are?"

Target identity seekers in life transitions. Message: You've become what others wanted, not who you are.

3

Your Dragons Are Your Power

"What If Your Flaws Are Actually Your Greatest Strengths?"

Target shadow work curious audiences. Message: Your dragons aren't meant to be slayed—they're meant to be befriended.

4

Brules (Bullshit Rules)

"Life By Whose Rules?"

Target rebels and non-conformists. Message: You're living by rules you never agreed to.

5

Success-Happiness Divide

"Stop Choosing Between Success and Wellbeing"

Target burnout entrepreneurs. Message: You can have both—but only if you start with self-acceptance.

6

The Flawesome Philosophy

"Flaws + Awesome = Flawesome"

Core brand concept. Message: Your imperfections aren't bugs; they're features.
