✅ What I Love (Keep These)

1. The "Clone" Framing

Brilliant positioning. "Build your clone" is concrete, tangible, and exciting. Much better than abstract "learn AI." People can visualize the outcome. KEEP

2. CAPTURE → BUILD → TEST Framework

Simple, memorable, repeatable. This framework can be used forever. Perfect for our audience who wants actionable methods. KEEP

3. The Showcase at the End

Everyone presents their best clone (3 min each). Creates accountability, proof of results, and community validation. Brilliant. KEEP

4. Build Sprint Concept

45 minutes to perfect ONE clone with facilitator support. Creates focused mastery vs. surface-level coverage. KEEP

5. Specific Tools Named

ChatGPT, Granola, Buffer, Claude — tangible, not abstract. People know exactly what they're getting. KEEP

6. Time-Boxed Segments

Every activity has a specific duration. Creates urgency and momentum. Prevents death-by-PowerPoint. KEEP

7. "The Aman Tokyo Principle"

"Invisible service" — Great metaphor for how AI should work. Elevates the concept beyond basic automation. KEEP

8. Homework Between Days

Real-world testing between Day 1 and Day 2. Creates proof of concept and surfaces real questions. KEEP

⚠️ Concerns & Modifications Needed

1. Duration: 8 Hours vs. 14 Hours

Marisha proposes 4 hours × 2 days = 8 hours total.
Our survey showed 65.5% are intermediate users who've experimented and want DEPTH.

Risk: 8 hours may feel too surface-level for our audience. However, shorter = higher completion rates.

RECOMMENDATION: Extend to 6-7 hours/day (12-14 total)

2. Clones Don't Match Survey Demand

Survey showed what people ACTUALLY want:

Survey Rank What They Want Selection % In Marisha's Plan?
#1 AI Work Companion (automate tasks) 38.7% Partial
#2 Custom GPT (personal assistant) 36.5% Yes
#3 Organize life + business 34.5% No
#4 Design visuals + videos 31.4% No

RECOMMENDATION: Add Organization Clone or Visual Clone

3. Granola AI Is Niche

Granola AI is a meeting transcription tool that's relatively new and may not work for everyone's meeting setup (especially international users with different meeting tools).

RECOMMENDATION: Offer alternatives (Otter.ai, Fireflies, native Zoom transcription)

4. Pricing Higher Than Market Research Suggested

Marisha: $1,295 early bird, $1,995 regular
Our strategy: $997 with ascension model behind it

Survey showed only 30.5% are "Likely" or "Definitely" buying at $999-$1,999 range.
61% are "Open to buying" but price-sensitive.

RECOMMENDATION: Consider $997 entry with upsells, OR justify $1,295 with added value

5. Missing Integration with Mastery Phases

Marisha's plan is standalone. No mention of how this connects to Phase 1, 2, 3 of the Mastery program.

RECOMMENDATION: Add clear "What's Next" path to Phases at closing

6. No Visual/Video Clone

31.4% of survey respondents want to learn visual/video creation with AI (Midjourney, Canva AI, HeyGen). This is completely missing.

RECOMMENDATION: Add as optional 5th clone or swap with Meeting Clone

🚨 Critical Concerns

1. Time Savings Claims May Hurt Credibility

"39-57 hours per week saved" = 78-114 hours of weekly work BEFORE clones?

Most people don't work that many hours. This claim risks:

  • Sounding unbelievable to skeptical intermediate users (65.5% of audience)
  • Setting expectations that can't be met → refund requests
  • Feeling like classic marketing hype our audience rejects

Breakdown of claims:
• Communication Clone: 15-23 hrs/week = 3-4 hours/day on email?
• Decision-Making: 10-17 hrs/week = 2-3 hours/day on decisions?

RECOMMENDATION: Temper to "15-25 hours/week" which is still transformational but believable. Or use "equivalent value" framing instead of literal hours.

2. Annual Value Claims Are Extreme

"$203K-$1.48M/year value" assumes $100-500/hour rates.

Our audience composition from survey:

  • Entrepreneurs: 23.8%
  • Coaches: 14.7%
  • Solopreneurs: 14.5%
  • Employees: 13.8%

Many of these people don't bill at $100-500/hour. The value claim may feel disconnected from their reality.

RECOMMENDATION: Use "time back for what matters" framing vs. dollar values, OR show range starting at $50/hour.
