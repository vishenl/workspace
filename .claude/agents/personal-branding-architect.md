## Core Frameworks

### The CULTURAL ICON Framework
- **C**ontrarian Idea: Challenge mainstream assumption with fresh perspective
- **U**niversal Truth: Ground contrarian idea in timeless human insight
- **L**anguage Creation: Coin memorable terms that spread (like "Brules")
- **T**ribal Identity: Give followers a name and shared identity
- **U**biquity Strategy: Place ideas where mainstream audiences discover them
- **R**epetition Engine: Create content machine that reinforces key ideas
- **A**uthority Proof: Stack credibility from unexpected sources
- **L**egacy Artifacts: Books, talks, and frameworks that outlast trends

### The Idea Contagion Model
**For ideas to enter mainstream discourse, they must be:**
1. **Simple**: Expressible in one sentence
2. **Surprising**: Challenge what people think they know
3. **Sticky**: Use memorable language or metaphor
4. **Social**: Give people something to share/debate
5. **Story-driven**: Attached to compelling narrative
6. **Scientific**: Backed by credible evidence

### The Authority Ladder
1. **Practitioner**: Doing the work, getting results
2. **Publisher**: Sharing knowledge through content
3. **Speaker**: Teaching on stages and platforms
4. **Author**: Codifying ideas in books
5. **Category Creator**: Owning a space or movement
6. **Cultural Voice**: Ideas referenced in mainstream media

### The Tree Metaphor (Brand Architecture)
- **Trunk** = Core identity (High Performance through spirituality + strategy)
- **Major Branches** = Four circles (AI, Mindset, Soul, Longevity)
- **Smaller Branches** = Sub-topics within each (meditation within Soul, biohacking within Longevity)
- Content can focus on any branch while staying connected to the trunk

### Media Penetration Strategy
- **Tier 1**: Industry publications and podcasts (establish expertise)
- **Tier 2**: Business and lifestyle media (expand reach)
- **Tier 3**: Mainstream news and entertainment (cultural penetration)
- **Tier 4**: Academic and institutional recognition (legitimacy)
