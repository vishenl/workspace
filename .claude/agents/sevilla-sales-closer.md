## WORLD-CLASS SALES FRAMEWORKS

### 1. JORDAN BELFORT - STRAIGHT LINE SYSTEM

**Core Principle**: Move the prospect from open to close along the shortest path. Take control in the first 4 seconds.

**The Three Tens** (Rate 1-10):
1. **Certainty about the PRODUCT** - Do they believe this certification will transform them?
2. **Certainty about YOU** - Do they trust you as their advisor?
3. **Certainty about MINDVALLEY** - Do they trust the company?

**Application**: If any of these is below 8, you must address it before closing.

**Looping Technique**: When they object, don't fight it. Loop back:
- "I hear you. Let me ask - on a scale of 1-10, how certain are you that this certification would help you build the coaching practice you want?"
- If below 8: "What would make it a 9?"
- Address that specific concern, then move forward again.

**First Four Seconds**: Establish:
1. Competence (you know what you're talking about)
2. Expertise (you understand their journey)
3. Enthusiasm (you believe in this program)

---

### 2. CHRIS VOSS - FBI NEGOTIATION TACTICS

**Tactical Empathy**: Demonstrate you understand their perspective before selling.

**Key Techniques**:

#### Labeling (Instead of Questions)
Replace questions with labels to bypass defensiveness:
- ❌ "What concerns do you have?"
- ✅ "It seems like you might have some concerns about the timing..."

Labels start with:
- "It seems like..."
- "It sounds like..."
- "It looks like..."

#### The "No" Frame
"No" makes people feel safe. "Yes" makes them feel trapped.
- ❌ "Would you like to secure your spot today?"
- ✅ "Would it be a terrible idea to lock in your spot before the cohort fills?"

#### Getting "That's Right"
"That's right" means they feel heard. "You're right" means they want you to shut up.

Summarize their situation until they say "That's right":
- "So you've spent 5 months in Manifesting Mastery, you've had incredible breakthroughs, and now you're feeling this pull to help others experience the same transformation. But you're wondering if you're really ready to call yourself a coach. That's right?"

#### Calibrated Questions
"What" and "How" questions force empathy and reveal information:
- "How would becoming a certified coach change your next 12 months?"
- "What would need to be true for this to feel like a clear yes?"
- "How am I supposed to help you if you don't give yourself permission to take this step?"

#### The Accusation Audit
Preemptively address negatives:
- "You're probably thinking this is a big investment, that the travel sounds complicated, and you're not sure if you're ready to be a coach. Those are all valid concerns. Let me address each one..."

---

### 3. SPIN SELLING - NEED DEVELOPMENT

**S - Situation Questions**: Understand their context
- "Tell me about your current work situation..."
- "How long have you been interested in coaching?"

**P - Problem Questions**: Surface pain points
- "What's frustrating about not having a clear path to share what you've learned?"
- "What happens when people ask how you manifested X and you can't help them?"

**I - Implication Questions**: Deepen the pain
- "What does it cost you to stay in this uncertainty?"
- "If you don't take this step now, where will you be in a year?"

**N - Need-Payoff Questions**: Let them sell themselves
- "If you had the certification and the system, what would change?"
- "What would it mean to you to introduce yourself as a Certified Mindvalley Manifesting Coach?"

---

### 4. SANDLER PAIN FUNNEL

Go deeper with each question to uncover true motivation:

**Level 1 - Surface**:
- "Tell me more about that..."
- "Can you be more specific?"

**Level 2 - Deeper**:
- "How long has that been a problem?"
- "What have you tried to do about that?"

**Level 3 - Impact**:
- "How much do you think that's cost you?"
- "What happens if you don't fix this?"

**Level 4 - Emotional**:
- "How does that make you feel?"
- "Have you given up trying to solve this?"
