## PART 1: AUTHOR EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

### Initial Research Protocol

When evaluating a potential Mindvalley author, conduct comprehensive research:

**1. WEBSITE ANALYSIS**
- Homepage messaging and positioning
- About page / bio / credentials
- Methodology / framework / unique system
- Existing courses / products / offerings
- Testimonials and social proof
- Media mentions and features
- Community size and engagement
- Production quality of existing content

**2. CONTENT ANALYSIS**
- YouTube / podcast appearances
- Books and publications
- Speaking engagements
- Social media presence and style
- Teaching methodology
- Language patterns and voice

**3. AUDIENCE ANALYSIS**
- Who do they currently serve?
- What transformation do they promise?
- What pain points do they address?
- Demographics and psychographics
- Engagement levels and sentiment

### Mindvalley Fit Criteria

**MUST-HAVE CRITERIA:**

| Criteria | Description | Weight |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| **Authentic Expertise** | Real credentials, experience, results | Critical |
| **Unique Methodology** | Proprietary framework/system | Critical |
| **Transformation Focus** | Creates real change, not just information | Critical |
| **Camera Presence** | Engaging on video, teachable | High |
| **Existing Audience** | Some proven following/traction | Medium |
| **Mindvalley Values Alignment** | Growth-oriented, ethical, inclusive | Critical |

**DIFFERENTIATORS TO LOOK FOR:**

1. **Lineage/Heritage** - Authentic tradition (like Zen Samurai's 16 generations)
2. **Scientific Backing** - Research-validated methods
3. **Celebrity/Elite Access** - Trained high-profile clients
4. **Unique Story** - Compelling origin/transformation narrative
5. **Category Creation** - Pioneer of a new approach
6. **Cultural Depth** - Connection to ancient wisdom traditions

**RED FLAGS:**

- Unverifiable credentials
- Hype without substance
- Controversial past/statements
- Poor production quality
- No existing audience
- Generic methodology (nothing unique)
- Spiritual bypassing or harmful practices
- MLM or predatory sales tactics

## PART 2: QUEST DESIGN FRAMEWORK

### Quest Structure Options

**14-DAY QUEST (Starter/Introduction)**
- Best for: New authors, simpler methodologies, entry-level topics
- Daily commitment: 15-20 minutes
- Structure: 2-3 days per core concept, building progressively
- Goal: Quick wins, habit formation, taste of transformation

**21-DAY QUEST (Habit Formation)**
- Best for: Behavioral change, daily practices, mindset shifts
- Daily commitment: 15-25 minutes
- Structure: Week 1 (Foundation), Week 2 (Practice), Week 3 (Integration)
- Goal: Establish new patterns, create lasting change

**6-WEEK QUEST (Deep Dive)**
- Best for: Skill acquisition, moderate complexity
- Daily commitment: 20-30 minutes
- Structure: Bi-weekly themes, building complexity
- Goal: Competence in new area, visible transformation

**12-WEEK QUEST (Full Transformation)**
- Best for: Major life changes, complex methodologies, deep work
- Daily commitment: 20-30 minutes
- Structure: Monthly phases with integration weeks
- Goal: Complete identity shift, mastery-level change

## PART 3: AUTHOR POSITIONING STRATEGY

### Positioning Framework

**THE POSITIONING FORMULA:**

```
[Author Name] is the [Authority Descriptor] who helps [Target Audience]
achieve [Transformation] through [Unique Mechanism],
unlike [Old Way/Competitors] because [Key Differentiator].
```

**Example:**
"Sensei Zen Takai is the 16th-generation Samurai Master who helps leaders and growth-seekers achieve emotional self-regulation and embodied presence through the Five Elements Moving Meditation, unlike traditional sitting meditation or martial arts because his 1,000-year lineage combines ancient warrior wisdom with practical daily practices accessible to modern life."

### Authority Stack Elements

Build author credibility through these elements:

**1. CREDENTIALS**
- Academic degrees
- Professional certifications
- Years of experience
- Institutional affiliations

**2. ACHIEVEMENTS**
- Students taught/lives impacted
- Books published
- Awards received
- Companies/brands built

**3. ACCESS**
- Celebrity clients
- Fortune 500 companies trained
- Elite organizations served
- Media features

**4. LINEAGE/HERITAGE**
- Tradition/school trained in
- Masters studied under
- Generational knowledge
- Cultural authenticity

**5. SCIENCE**
- Research backing
- Clinical studies
- Peer-reviewed publications
- Expert endorsements

**6. STORY**
- Personal transformation journey
- Crisis that led to discovery
- Why they teach this
- Mission/purpose

### Voice & Messaging Guidelines

**MINDVALLEY AUTHOR VOICE:**

Every Mindvalley author should sound:
- **Sophisticated**: Honors audience intelligence
- **Evidence-Based**: Research-backed, credible
- **Inspirational**: Uplifting without hype
- **Authentic**: Real, vulnerable, human
- **Transformational**: Focus on becoming, not just learning

**KEY MESSAGING ELEMENTS:**

1. **Primary Promise**: The main transformation
2. **Unique Mechanism**: What makes it work (the "how")
3. **Key Differentiator**: Why this vs. alternatives
4. **Pain Point Address**: What struggle it solves
5. **Social Proof**: Evidence it works
6. **Invitation**: Low-pressure call to action

## PART 5: AUDIENCE RESEARCH & OPTIMIZATION

### Feedback Analysis Framework

When analyzing audience feedback (like A-Fest reviews):

**1. SENTIMENT ANALYSIS**
- Overall rating distribution
- Positive vs. negative ratio
- Neutral/constructive feedback

**2. THEME EXTRACTION**
- What concepts resonated most?
- What practices were mentioned?
- What emotional responses occurred?
- What requests were made?

**3. QUOTE MINING**
- Powerful testimonial quotes
- Specific outcomes mentioned
- Emotional language used
- Requests for more content

**4. IMPROVEMENT OPPORTUNITIES**
- What was missing?
- What confused people?
- What could be expanded?
- What objections appeared?

### Optimization Process

**ITERATE POSITIONING BASED ON:**

1. **High-Resonance Elements**: Double down on what works
2. **Unexpected Hits**: Find surprise successes
3. **Common Requests**: Address what audience wants
4. **Language Patterns**: Use their words back to them
5. **Emotional Peaks**: Identify transformation moments

**FEEDBACK → REFINEMENT LOOP:**

```
Initial Positioning
      ↓
Live Presentation/Content
      ↓
Audience Feedback Collection
      ↓
Theme & Pattern Analysis
      ↓
Positioning Refinement
      ↓
Updated Marketing Materials
      ↓
[Repeat]
```

## PART 7: IMPLEMENTATION WORKFLOW

### New Author Evaluation Process

**STEP 1: INITIAL RESEARCH (30-60 minutes)**
```
1. Scrape and analyze website
2. Review all visible content
3. Watch 2-3 video appearances
4. Read testimonials and reviews
5. Check social media presence
6. Research background/credentials
```

**STEP 2: ASSESSMENT (15-30 minutes)**
```
1. Complete Author Scorecard
2. Identify unique methodology
3. Assess Mindvalley fit
4. Note potential concerns
5. Determine Quest potential
```

**STEP 3: POSITIONING (30-60 minutes)**
```
1. Define transformation promise
2. Identify unique mechanism
3. Craft positioning statement
4. Determine marketing angles
5. Select primary audience segment
```

**STEP 4: QUEST DESIGN (60-90 minutes)**
```
1. Determine optimal Quest length
2. Structure curriculum by week/day
3. Identify core practices
4. Define transformation milestones
5. Create Quest overview document
```

**STEP 5: MARKETING VISION (60-120 minutes)**
```
1. Create quest-proposal.html
2. Design visual presentation
3. Include curriculum, testimonials
4. Add marketing angles
5. Store in /authorship/author-name/
```

### Refinement Process (With Feedback)

**WHEN AUDIENCE FEEDBACK IS PROVIDED:**

1. **Analyze Feedback Data**
   - Read all reviews/comments
   - Extract themes and patterns
   - Identify high-resonance elements
   - Note improvement areas

2. **Update Positioning**
   - Strengthen what resonated
   - Address gaps identified
   - Incorporate audience language
   - Refine marketing angles

3. **Revise Marketing Materials**
   - Update quest-proposal.html
   - Refine messaging and copy
   - Add testimonial quotes
   - Enhance visual presentation

4. **Create Additional Assets**
   - Masterclass outline
   - Sales page copy
   - Email sequences
   - Social media strategy
