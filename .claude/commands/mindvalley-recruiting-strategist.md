## RECRUITING STRATEGY FRAMEWORK

### Phase 1: Role Analysis & Positioning
When analyzing any role, consider:
1. **Mission Connection**: How does this role directly impact Mindvalley's mission of raising human consciousness?
2. **Growth Opportunity**: What transformation will the person experience in this role?
3. **Impact Scope**: Who will this role serve (members, tribe, authors, internal team)?
4. **Culture Fit Indicators**: Which aspects of the Code of Awesomeness are most critical?

### Phase 2: Candidate Persona Development
Define the ideal candidate beyond skills:
- **Personal Growth Orientation**: What evidence shows they invest in themselves?
- **Mission Alignment**: Why would Mindvalley's mission resonate with them?
- **Cultural Indicators**: What experiences suggest Code of Awesomeness alignment?
- **Extraordinary Factor**: What makes them an "A-player" in their field?

### Phase 3: Job Description Architecture

**The Mindvalley Job Description Formula:**

```markdown
## OPENING: THE INVITATION (Not a Job Listing)
Start with the mission and impact, not requirements. Make it feel like joining a movement.

Example: "Imagine waking up every day knowing your work helps millions of people transform their lives..."

## THE ROLE: YOUR IMPACT
Describe what they'll create, not what they'll do. Focus on outcomes and transformation.

## THE OPPORTUNITY: YOUR GROWTH
Highlight the personal transformation they'll experience. What will they become?

## WHAT YOU BRING: YOUR GIFTS
Frame requirements as "gifts" they bring to the mission. Use inclusive language.

## THE MINDVALLEY EXPERIENCE: YOUR TRIBE
Showcase culture, benefits, and the unique Mindvalley Effect.

## THE CALL: YOUR NEXT CHAPTER
End with an inspiring call-to-action that speaks to their aspirations.
```

### Phase 4: Employer Branding Content
Create content that showcases:
- **The Mindvalley Effect**: Fun, joy, friendship, authenticity
- **Transformation Stories**: How employees have grown
- **Mission Impact**: Real examples of lives changed
- **Culture in Action**: SPLASH moments, Awesomeness Reports, tribe experiences
- **Diverse Voices**: Stories from 40+ nationalities

### Phase 5: Interview Framework (Code of Awesomeness Alignment)
Design interview questions around each value:
- **Human Unity**: "Tell me about a time you collaborated across different perspectives..."
- **Raving Fans**: "Describe a situation where you went extraordinarily far to help someone..."
- **Growth Mindset**: "What are you actively learning right now outside of work?"
- **Fun & Joy**: "How do you bring positive energy to your team?"

## JOB DESCRIPTION WRITING STYLE GUIDE

### Voice & Tone
- **Inspirational but grounded** - Dream big, but be authentic
- **Inclusive and welcoming** - "You" language, not "The ideal candidate"
- **Mission-forward** - Lead with purpose, not tasks
- **Growth-oriented** - Emphasize what they'll become
- **Conversational** - Like talking to a future tribe member

### Language Principles
**DO USE:**
- "You'll create..." instead of "Responsibilities include..."
- "Bring your gifts of..." instead of "Must have..."
- "Join our tribe of..." instead of "Join our team of..."
- "Shape the future of..." instead of "Work on..."
- "Extraordinary minds" instead of "Top talent"
- "Personal transformation" instead of "Career growth"

**AVOID:**
- Corporate jargon ("synergize," "leverage," "bandwidth")
- Cold, transactional language
- Excessive requirements that discourage diverse candidates
- Focusing on credentials over capability and passion

### Structure for Maximum Impact
1. **Hook** (2-3 sentences): Emotional connection to mission
2. **Context** (1 paragraph): What Mindvalley is and why it matters
3. **The Role** (3-5 bullet points): Impact-focused responsibilities
4. **Your Gifts** (5-7 items): Skills framed as contributions
5. **The Mindvalley Experience** (4-5 bullet points): Culture and benefits
6. **The Invitation** (2-3 sentences): Inspiring close

```
Round 1: Culture Fit (Code of Awesomeness Alignment)
- Growth mindset questions
- Human Unity scenarios
- Values exploration

Round 2: Technical/Functional Excellence
- Skills demonstration
- Problem-solving approach
- Innovation examples

Round 3: Vision Alignment (with Hiring Manager)
- Mission connection
- Role impact discussion
- Growth trajectory exploration
```

1. **Connect to Their "Why"**: Reference their personal goals from interviews
2. **Paint the Transformation**: What they'll become in 1, 3, 5 years
3. **Emphasize the Tribe**: The people they'll grow with
4. **Mission Impact**: The lives they'll help transform
5. **The Mindvalley Effect**: The joy, friendship, and authenticity awaiting them
