# Viral Title Formulas for Thumbnail Text

## Rules for Thumbnail Text
- **Maximum 5 words** (must be readable at small size)
- **Bold, high contrast** font with outline/shadow
- **NEVER place over face**
- **Use ALL CAPS** for impact

---

## Formula 1: The Number Promise
**Structure:** [Number] + [Benefit/Object]

**Examples:**
- "7 SECRETS"
- "3 MISTAKES"
- "10X YOUR INCOME"
- "5 HACKS"
- "1 SIMPLE TRICK"

**Best For:** List videos, tips, tutorials

---

## Formula 2: The Challenge/Provocation
**Structure:** [Provocative Statement]

**Examples:**
- "YOU'RE DOING IT WRONG"
- "STOP DOING THIS"
- "WHY YOU'RE FAILING"
- "THIS IS A LIE"
- "THEY LIED TO YOU"

**Best For:** Contrarian takes, myth-busting, corrections

---

## Formula 3: The Transformation
**Structure:** [Before State] → [After State]

**Examples:**
- "$0 → $10K"
- "BEGINNER → PRO"
- "FAT → FIT"
- "BROKE → RICH"
- "0 → 1M SUBS"

**Best For:** Before/after content, progress videos, transformations

---

## Formula 4: The Secret/Reveal
**Structure:** "THE [Adjective] [Subject]"

**Examples:**
- "THE REAL TRUTH"
- "FINALLY REVEALED"
- "THE SECRET METHOD"
- "HIDDEN TECHNIQUE"
- "THEY DON'T WANT YOU TO KNOW"

**Best For:** Exposé content, insider knowledge, revelations

---

## Formula 5: The Warning
**Structure:** [Urgent Warning]

**Examples:**
- "DON'T DO THIS"
- "WATCH BEFORE..."
- "I WAS WRONG"
- "HUGE MISTAKE"
- "WARNING..."

**Best For:** Cautionary content, mistakes to avoid, urgent updates

---

## Formula 6: The Question
**Structure:** [Provocative Question]

**Examples:**
- "IS THIS REAL?"
- "DOES IT WORK?"
- "WORTH IT?"
- "SCAM?"
- "WHY?"

**Best For:** Reviews, investigations, testing content

---

## Formula 7: The Result
**Structure:** [Impressive Outcome]

**Examples:**
- "IT WORKED"
- "INSANE RESULTS"
- "LIFE CHANGED"
- "MIND BLOWN"
- "GAME CHANGER"

**Best For:** Results videos, testimonials, proof content

---

## Formula 8: The Time Frame
**Structure:** [Time] + [Result]

**Examples:**
- "IN 24 HOURS"
- "30 DAY RESULTS"
- "1 WEEK LATER"
- "OVERNIGHT SUCCESS"
- "IN 5 MINUTES"

**Best For:** Challenge videos, quick results, time-based content

---

## Text Placement Guide

| Face Position | Text Placement |
|---------------|----------------|
| Lower 2/3 of frame | Top third |
| Upper 2/3 of frame | Bottom third |
| Centered | Left or right side |
| Left side | Right side |
| Right side | Left side |

---

## Color Combinations for Text

| Background Type | Text Color | Outline Color |
|-----------------|------------|---------------|
| Dark/moody | White or Yellow | Black |
| Light/bright | Black or Red | White |
| Colorful | White | Black |
| Face close-up | Yellow | Black |
| Blue tones | Orange or Yellow | Black |
| Warm tones | White or Cyan | Black |

---

## Font Style Recommendations

**Best fonts for thumbnails:**
- Impact
- Bebas Neue
- Oswald
- Anton
- Montserrat Black

**Avoid:**
- Thin/light weights
- Script/cursive fonts
- Decorative fonts
- Fonts with serifs at small sizes
