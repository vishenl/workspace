The Big Domino

The ONE Belief to Change

FLADLIEN PRINCIPLE: "If you can get someone to believe ONE thing, all other objections become irrelevant."

Big Domino Statement:

"Without a SYSTEM, you'll keep starting over. Random spiritual practices = random results. A structured system = predictable transformation."

Why This Big Domino Works:

  • Addresses core frustration: Inconsistency and lack of progress
  • Creates desire: "A system" = the solution they've been missing
  • Builds urgency: Time wasted without a system = opportunity cost
  • Positions the product: The 7-Gate System™ IS the system

Once They Believe This...

  • Price objection dissolves ("If this system works, it's worth any price")
  • Time objection dissolves ("22 weeks is nothing compared to years of wandering")
  • Skepticism dissolves ("A proven system reduces risk")
