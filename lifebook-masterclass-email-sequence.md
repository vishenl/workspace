## VOICE NOTES FOR VISHEN'S STYLE

When writing in Vishen's voice:

1. **Personal vulnerability** - He shares his own struggles and journey
2. **Direct questions** - He asks the reader to reflect ("Sound familiar?")
3. **Simple, short sentences** - Punchy. Clear. No fluff.
4. **Pattern interrupts** - Uses line breaks for emphasis
5. **Inclusive language** - "We" and "together"
6. **Bold statements** - He's not afraid to be definitive
7. **Name-dropping with context** - He mentions experts but explains why they matter
8. **Warmth** - Signs off with "With love, Vishen"
9. **Strategic P.S.** - Always adds value in the postscript
10. **Action-oriented CTAs** - Clear, benefit-focused calls to action
