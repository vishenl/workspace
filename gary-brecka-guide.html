Root Cause Philosophy

Stop treating leaves when you should be testing the soil

"One thing causes everything. It's not an explosion — it's a domino effect. You don't have 17 things wrong with you; you have ONE thing causing 17 symptoms."

The Tree Analogy

If you had a leaf rotting on a tree, a true arborist wouldn't touch the leaf. They'd test the soil. Find nitrogen is missing → add nitrogen → leaf heals.

The Truth About Inheritance

There is NO hypertension gene. No diabetes gene. The human genome is designed to NOT pass diseases. You inherit deficiencies, not diseases.

Key Principles

  • Domino Effect: In the human body, rarely do multiple systems fail at the same time. Usually one thing goes wrong that causes everything.
  • Find the First Domino: When someone has multiple autoimmune diseases, gut dysbiosis, mental illness, anxiety — there's ONE root cause.
  • Deficiency = Expression: When you deprive the human body of certain raw materials, you get the expression of disease.
