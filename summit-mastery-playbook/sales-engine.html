SALES INFRASTRUCTURE

Sales & Conversion Engine

Strike while the iron is hot. From delayed "book a call" to live program expert lobby. From complex feature stacks to simple, compressed-time offers.

BIGGEST TACTICAL CHANGE

The Live Sales Lobby Model

BEFORE: "Book a Call with an Advisor"

  • Tiny text buried at the bottom of the sales page — "My god, as a 50-year-old, I cannot even read that text" (Vishen)
  • Customer books a call 1–2 weeks later
  • By then, emotional energy is gone
  • Logical buyers swirl without support
  • "Today's non-buyer becomes tomorrow's non-buyer too"

AFTER: "Talk to a Program Expert"

  • Button appears on dashboard during the event as soon as the offer is made
  • Clicks into a LIVE Zoom room with trained program experts
  • Agents greet people, answer questions in real-time, in a GROUP setting
  • Logical buyers lean in: "Wait, that's MY question too. Can I listen?"
  • Small pods form naturally, just like the back of a physical room
  • UPW (Tony Robbins) example: 1,200 people hit the lobby at once. Only needed 5 salespeople + 5 chat support.

Key Insight: "At in-person events, you'd make the offer, people go to the back of the room. Emotional buyers put down credit cards. Logical buyers talk to salespeople. Other logical buyers overhear and lean in: 'Wait, that's my question too!' Before you know it, small groups form around each salesperson. The lobby virtualizes this exact experience."

"I want to take care of my right-fit client. If they have questions in the moment, I don't want to say 'book a call sometime.' Let's get your question answered right now."

PRICING PSYCHOLOGY

Price Reveal Strategy

Always On the Sales Page

Full price transparency. Never hide the price.

"I would feel icky about hiding the price. That'd be a turnoff." — Bari

People can always go direct to the sales page and buy without ever talking to anyone.

But Don't Lead With It

Don't announce the price at the START of the event.

"When you say 'I'm going to sell you something and here it is,' now they're listening to everything through the lens of 'is this worth it?' versus enjoying the journey."

  • Let them fall in love with the method first
  • "I want them thinking: 'This is so amazing. Where do I get more?'"

Reveal When They're Ready

The offer comes at the right moment on Day 2.

"I thought you'd never ask. I know exactly who you are, what you're struggling with, and this will solve those pain points."

Frame as service, not sales: "Because I love you and want you to succeed, I've already thought of the natural next step."

"I don't think of it as hiding the price. I think of it as revealing the investment when you're ready to hear it."

HORMOZI PRINCIPLE

Simplicity Converts

"If you have to explain it, cut it. Talk to them like they're a fourth grader."

— From Hormozi launch debrief

One offer, 10 angles, NO value stack, NO feature stack, NO buffet.

The Dean/Tony AI clone offer was their SIMPLEST ever:

  • Least deliverables they'd ever had
  • Very simple bonus structure
  • 3 months inside their club for continuity
  • The "Billion Dollar Blueprint" that Tony and Dean designed
  • "Really simple offer"

BEFORE: Feature Stack Overload

"You get Module 1 about X, and Module 2 about Y, and 14 bonuses, and a mastermind, and weekly calls, and a resource library, and..."

AFTER: One Clear Promise

"Build your AI clone in 30 days. Get 15 hours/week back."

Current MV problem: "Build 5 AI clones that give you 20–40 hours back every week" — hits the credulity ceiling AND feels overwhelming.

The fix: "Build ONE clone. Get your time back. Then get an army later."

PRICING INSIGHT

Compressed Time = Higher Value

The key insight: $997 for 30 days instead of $997 for 6 months — HIGHER conversions.

Why: "You're paying us more to get it done faster."

The pitch: "If we told you it would take 6 months and cost $3,000, it would still be a steal. But we compressed the entire journey into 30 days. No extra hires, no confusion, no wasted months."

$4K-8K
AI Mastery / 5 months / ~1,000 students (consistent across prices)
$997
AI Clone (new) / 30 days / higher conversion rate
Shorter
Shorter + Simpler = Easier Decision

Momentum beats duration: "Shorter horizon, faster decision. Time becomes the tool, not the enemy."

Then sell the complex thing on the BACK END: "Now they have a clone. Now they want to know how to use it, update it, build another one."

"What normally would take 6 months in 30 days. We made the price $997 for 30 days. And we had way higher conversions."

BUYER PSYCHOLOGY

Overwhelm & Refund Psychology

Current Refund Rate

~13%

Industry standard for online courses

But driven by impulse buyers — emotional buyers who buy and then regret.

Flatline technique being used: "Try for 2 weeks, attend 4 live classes, full refund if you want."

The Deeper Issue: Content Shame

Too much content creates shame:

  • "I feel guilty because it's one more thing I'm gonna buy that I'm not gonna consume"
  • "I promised myself I wouldn't do that again"
  • "It sounds amazing, but I just don't have time"

The Fix: Less Content, More Outcome

  • Simple offers. Fast results.
  • Eric Edmeades' programs have 80% completion rate — bring him in to advise on stickiness
  • Less stuff, more results = lower refunds, higher satisfaction

"People just feel completely overwhelmed. If we're just piling on things — 'And you get a bonus, and you get this' — sometimes that works against you. They just want less stuff, more results."
