EXECUTION ROADMAP

Implementation Roadmap

6-phase deployment plan. Phase 1 (method/framework) is the prerequisite that unlocks everything else. Without it, no other change will stick.

Critical Prerequisite

Nothing Works Without the Method

The #1 insight from the entire session is that Mindvalley does NOT currently have a unified method for most of its mastery programs. Without a method:

  • You can't select the right speakers (what are you selecting them FOR?)
  • You can't create session continuity (no FOMO if you miss a session)
  • You can't have a compelling through-line (messaging → content → offer)
  • You can't stop the buffet problem
  • The 3-day cadence can't work (there's no "step 3" to miss)

Two programs that DO have methods: Manifesting (Vishen + Reagan) and Speaking (Eric Edmeades). These can serve as templates for every other mastery category.

"Without the method, we cannot have an accurate speaker selection, because what are we selecting them for?"

Prerequisite — Blocks All Other Phases

Phase 1 — Define the Method/Framework

Timeline: 2-3 weeks Owner: Vishen + Bari (collaborative workshop)

Deliverables

  • One core method per mastery category (AI, Manifesting, Speaking, etc.)
  • The "one belief" for each — what is the single transformation promise?
  • 5-7 steps that make up the method (these become summit sessions)
  • The "enhanced opportunity" — what does the mastery program deliver that the summit can't?

Actions

  • Workshop with Bari to define the method for AI Mastery first (highest revenue potential)
  • Audit Manifesting and Speaking programs — they already have methods. Document and replicate the pattern.
  • Test: Can you explain the method in one sentence? If not, it's not simple enough.

"People buy frameworks, they buy methods. 'There's a formula and a framework I could follow? Well, maybe I could do that.'"

Phase 3

Curriculum Restructure

Timeline: 3-4 weeks Owner: Vishen + Inny + curriculum team Depends on: Phases 1 + 2

Deliverables

  • Restructured AI Mastery curriculum — 3 months instead of 5, outcome-focused
  • Potential 3-tier structure: Beginner (clone basics) → Intermediate (workflow automation) → Advanced (vibe coding, app building)
  • Each tier has clear, measurable outcomes — not tool mastery
  • Bring in Eric Edmeades for stickiness methodology (his programs = 80% completion)

Key Problems to Solve

  • Attendance drops to 15-20% in final month when content gets too technical
  • Teaching Airtable because Vishen loves it (computer science brain) vs. what Julie needs
  • Miss one class = feel lost, can't catch up (no progressive scaffolding)
  • Current curriculum designed like a university program — needs to be designed like a transformation journey

What the DATA Says Works (High Engagement)

  • Image generation
  • Video
  • Custom GPTs
  • Vibe coding (simple version)
  • "Trying everything together"

What the DATA Says Fails (Low Engagement)

  • AI agents
  • Airtable automations
  • Custom AI applications
  • "Building brains"

Principle: Sell the outcome, teach the minimum mechanism needed to achieve it.

"What normally would take 6 months in 30 days. Shorter horizon, faster decision."

HOUR 4 ADDITIONS

New Action Items from Hour 4

Additional strategic priorities surfaced in the final hour of the session.

1
Rename All Summits — Drop "Mastery"
Immediately remove "mastery" from all summit/event names. "AI Mastery Summit" becomes something else. Reserve "mastery" exclusively for the $5K high-ticket product. Current naming creates bait-and-switch perception.
2
Rewrite Solopreneur Messaging
Replace "be replaced by AI" fear messaging with leverage messaging: "Zero overhead. Never hire again. Every dollar you keep." Target the busy, successful solopreneur — not the aspiring one with lots of time.
3
Compress Mastery Programs
Move away from 5-month academic format. Target: 1 month or a few weeks. Teach 5 things well instead of 15 things poorly. Position shorter = more valuable. Stop dropping prices — charge more for compressed transformation.
4
Define Mindvalley's Ultimate Outcome
Answer: "A student comes on our campus — the transformation we want to create is... what?" All 4 pillars (Manifesting, Entrepreneur, Longevity, Speaking) should be identities working toward one ultimate outcome, like Tony's ecosystem.
5
Inject Consciousness Engineering Into Mastery
Stop deferring to academic teachers after Vishen's mindset opener. Weave Models of Reality + Systems for Living throughout. Move from paradigm shift (intellectual) to identity transformation (emotional embodiment).
6
Separate MVU from Sales Strategy
MVU = community event. DNA stays. Don't apply summit sales formula. KPI: 70% of next year's tickets in first week, not mastery conversions. Explore focused virtual weekend to feed in-person attendance (20-30% convert).
7
Kill Hybrid Event Plans
In-person and virtual events must be separate. Hybrid lowers conversion on both sides. Speakers pander to live audience (Jimmy Fallon analogy). Virtual events only work when there's no in-person audience competing for attention.
8
Map Cross-Pillar Customer Journey
People enter through manifesting/spirituality but buy AI/entrepreneurship. Understand these pathways. Silva Method is the #1 gateway. Design the campus so each pillar reinforces the others — not isolated product lines.
9
Give Vishen Top Billing on All Marketing
Bari noted Vishen should be featured more prominently in hero shots. "Usually it's the person we want them to most know, and then everyone flanking them — versus him just being a big one of many." Outcomes should be higher on the page.
