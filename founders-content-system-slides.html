Part 6

The Content Flywheel

One Piece of Content. Multiple Platforms.
📧 Newsletter → 💼 LinkedIn → 🎬 YouTube → ✂️ Clips → 📱 TikTok/IG
How It Works
  • Write your newsletter (core content)
  • Extract LinkedIn posts (each lesson = one post)
  • Turn it into a video script with AI
  • Record from two angles
  • Create clips for TikTok/Instagram
  • Send reflection email to continue conversation
