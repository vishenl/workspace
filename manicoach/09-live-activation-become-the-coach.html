1. Setting the Intention

Before closing eyes, Regan asks participants to tune into their intention: What does it look like when you have become the most phenomenal manifesting coach?

The Calibration Questions:

  • If you came to me in 6 months or a year and said "It worked, it's amazing" — how would you know?
  • Is it that your clients come back saying they're having miraculous results?
  • Is it that your sessions feel fun, easy, and incredible?
  • Is it that you wake up every day in love with your work?
  • Is it that you've generated a certain amount of money and clients keep coming back?

Then: Summarize that into one word — the center point of this activation.

3. The Movie Screen Visualization

A huge screen appears in front of you. A movie is playing — and it's a movie of you already living the reality where you are the most phenomenal manifesting coach.

What to Notice in the Movie:

  • What is happening in this reality?
  • Who's around you? Who's NOT around you?
  • What are you saying to yourself?
  • How do you KNOW that your intention has materialized?

Three Ways to Connect

"You may see this screen, you may feel it, you may just simply know that you are connecting to it. Either way is perfect."

4. The Three Dials

Three dials appear on the side of the movie screen. Each one calibrates a different dimension of the experience.

1

Brightness

Turn it UP — movie becomes brighter and brighter. Full white light.

Turn it DOWN — pitch black. Take all the light out.

Settle it in the middle — exactly where it's meant to be.

2

Volume

Turn it UP — all the sounds AND everything you're saying to yourself. Full sound.

Turn it DOWN — take all sound out of the movie. Silence.

Settle at the perfect volume — internal and external sounds aligned.

3

Feeling (Only Up)

This dial is different — you only turn it up. It increases how good it FEELS to be you in the movie.

Turn it all the way to FULL FEELING. Stronger and stronger and stronger.

Why Calibrate?

Turning brightness and volume up/down/settle teaches the subconscious that it has control over the experience. The feeling dial only goes up because that's the frequency you want to lock in before jumping into the screen.

5. Jump Into the Screen

— Regan Hillyer

2

3

Triple It

4

Jump Back Out

Keeping this state, vibration, and frequency — jump out of the screen. You are once again witnessing yourself having materialized your ideal reality as a powerful, incredible, service-driven manifesting coach.

4

Drop the Screen Down

5

Choose It

At a soul level, across every single reality and dimension: simply choose it. With every single cell of your being. "It is done. It is done. It is done."
