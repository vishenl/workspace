Close Eyes & Drop In

"Close your eyes. Allow yourself to drop into the space, being aware of your breath. Align yourself with your intention to shift into truly embodying this new state."

4

Create the Golden Ball (Key Step)

"Pull up a golden ball of light in front of you. You may see it, you may feel it, or you may just know that it's there. Either way is perfect. Within this ball is the frequency, the state, the vibration of [desired state] in the most powerful aligned way for you."

5

Track the Location (Key Step)

Ask: "Where is this ball right now? Is it in front of you? Off to the side? How far away?"

6

Click, Drag & Drop

"Go ahead and click that ball, drag it gently towards you, and now drop it into your heart."
Where to drop: Heart (default), OR where they feel the unwanted state (gut, head, shoulders)

"Breathe into your heart. Feel what you feel as you notice this frequency running through every single cell of your being, vibrating and radiating from your heart."

"Now allow this golden ball to IMPLODE into every single cell of your being. Allow every cell to open, expand, fuse, alchemize, and become ONE with this vibration. Running through your body, your mind, your emotions, your energetic field."
Use IMPLODE not explode — we want energy going IN, not out.

9

Anchor with Direct Command

"Allow yourself to remember that embodying this is your natural state."
Use first person: "I am power. I am power. I am power."
NOT "You are powerful" — that creates dissociation.

10

Bring Them Out & Integrate

"Take a deep breath into your heart. Ground and anchor all of these shifts. Gently come back into your body, into this space. Open your eyes."
Ask: "What do you notice that's different?"

7. Key Points & FAQ

What if they can't visualize?

They don't need to SEE it. Say: "You may see it, you may feel it, or you may just know that it's there. Either way is perfect." If they say "I don't know where it is," ask: "If you DID know, where would it be?" Don't accept "I don't know" — they DO know. Sometimes you need to ask 4 times.

Eyes open or closed?

Eyes OPEN for the initial question (what state do you want to change?) — you want them accessing their 3D reality. Watch their eye patterns as they access the state.
Eyes CLOSED for the rest of the process.

Why "I am" instead of "You are"?

If you say "You are powerful," they hear it as someone else being powerful. Using "I am powerful" lands directly in their system — full association. In hypnosis, this is called a direct command.

Why IMPLODE instead of EXPLODE?

"Explode" can feel intense and also implies energy going OUT. We want energy going IN — fully associated, fusing, becoming ONE with the state. Imploding creates deeper integration.

One state at a time or multiple?

One at a time. If they have 10 states to change, you can do it 10 times, but handle piece by piece. Multiple states at once gets muddled and confused. Checklist approach — tick them off one by one.

What if something deeper comes up?

This can happen — you move something and realize there's something underneath. That's when you continue: "What's this pain sharing with you now? What do you believe about it?" Then go to a deeper layer. This is part of a full session, not just one isolated tool.

Does this create heart coherence?

Yes, it naturally creates more heart coherence and brings the whole system into coherence. Brain waves will naturally drop and shift. You don't need a brain monitor — just trust the process.
