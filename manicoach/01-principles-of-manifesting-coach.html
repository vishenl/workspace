1. Introduction: The Heart of Service

A Manifesting Coach is a special type of person — someone walking the path with a heart of service who genuinely cares about helping others while simultaneously committed to their own evolution and growth.

The Dual Commitment

You can only help someone to the extent that you are also helping yourself. When you meet someone who is truly in their own alchemy, evolution, and striving to be the best version of themselves — there's an unmistakable energy and vibration about them.

Expand: Being More YOU

This work is about being more YOU — not becoming someone else. It's about connecting with the version of you that already exists in the future (the one helping people, living the reality you desire) and folding that version into the now using quantum entanglement principles.

When someone walks into a room and there's just something about them — that magnetic quality that makes you want to talk to them — it's usually because they're being the fullest version of themselves. Not a lower version, not an unconscious default version, but a consciously chosen higher version.

The version of you who is a Manifesting Coach — out there helping people, guiding them through deep processes, helping them restructure reality — that version already exists in the future. Your job is to fold that version into the now, to BE that person today.

Expand: Great Power, Great Responsibility

You'll hear words like "witch" and "magic" in this work. You'll learn how to bend and restructure reality through the quantum field. This comes with serious personal responsibility.

It's not enough to say "I have good intentions." You need to understand the principles, hold the field correctly, and bring the right energy. When you manifest with yourself, you're in your own field. When you hold space for someone else's manifestation, you open a joint field — a shared field — and information transfers. If you're not crystal clear, grounded, in integrity, and authentic, you can create a mess for both of you.

2. The Quantum Field & Shared Field Concept

You are connected to the quantum field where you have access to all information simultaneously, regardless of time, space, or distance. Through the observer effect and specific tools, you can alter information in the quantum field, which then creates a different external reflection in your reality.

The Shared Field (The Bubble)

When you work with someone as a Manifesting Coach, you create a shared field — like a bubble. Inside this bubble, you and your client have your individual fields, but together you're entering a shared quantum field. As you guide them through processes, you are holding the space and accelerating their process.

Expand: Why Coaching Accelerates Results

Because it's not just the client observing and doing — it's also you observing and doing. We know from the observer effect that 1 + 1 ≠ 2. When two people get together to pray, manifest, or hold intention together, everything accelerates through the field.

Your client may not even realize what's happening. They might think, "Oh, this manifesting coach is guiding me through a process." What's actually happening is you are supercharging their manifestations and amplifying them.

How you amplify (without "doing" anything extra): You are NOT doing Reiki, sending energy, or putting your hands on them. The amplification happens simply through your embodiment of these principles and guiding them through the process. When you hold a deep knowing that "it's done" — with no other option, no Plan B — reality has to fall into place quickly.

Desire vs. Longing: A Critical Distinction

✓ Desire (Soul Whisper)

  • Your soul whispering that something has already happened in the future
  • Feels expansive and exciting
  • Surrendered to HOW it shows up
  • Creates expansion in your field
  • "This is amazing. This feels so good. I'm super surrendered as to how it shows up."

✗ Longing (Attachment)

  • Attachment to it happening a certain way
  • Control energy: "This MUST happen"
  • Puts the whole field into contraction
  • Prevents the field from rearranging
  • "It has to be this day, this way, or else..."
Expand: Why This Distinction Matters for Coaching

Important: This doesn't mean having no desires. Desire is beautiful and necessary. The issue is when desire becomes longing — when there's attachment and control that contracts the field.

If you're holding space for clients and you're in "longing" energy — "I've got to make sure she makes this amount of money" — what happens? Control energy. Contraction. The whole field collapses. Everything gets stuck.

Instead, be crystal clear, embody the intention, know that "this or better will manifest, of course, obviously" — but be completely unattached to HOW that happens. Be open to listening, trusting, and taking action to participate and accelerate — not to MAKE it happen, but to CO-CREATE.

Expand: Q&A — Can I Do Multiple Activations?

Question: "Can I do creative activations for a particular goal multiple times, or does that create longing?"

Answer: You can do 100 activations — even 200 — IF you're not in longing, attachment, control, or manufactured manifestation energy. If it's putting you in that anxious space, stop — it's not helping. But if you're doing them from a place of "of course this is happening, I'm accelerating the process, this is surrendered manifestation" — go for it. It's not about the number; it's about the frequency you're in while doing them.

3. The Role of a Manifesting Coach

You Are NOT Creating FOR the Client

You are not taking on their dreams and prayers and making them happen. You are protecting the integrity of the client sitting in the observer seat, amplifying their energy, and accelerating the process. The minute you think YOU made something happen — you've lost it.

What You Actually Do

  • Hold a field of infinite possibility — where absolutely anything powerful and good could happen
  • Protect the energetics within the shared bubble
  • Amplify and accelerate through your presence and principles
  • Guide them through processes using the tools you're taught
  • Expect miracles — hold a frequency that overpowers any doubt they may have
Expand: Coach vs Mentor — You Don't Need Their Results

Question from session: "Do I have to have manifested something myself before I can help someone else manifest it?"

Answer: NO. You are a coach, not a mentor. A mentor says "I have done this, let me show you the steps." A coach says "I can hold the field and help you manifest whatever you want."

Your client might want to manifest a rare Ferrari you've never owned. You can still help them. However, this doesn't mean you're not working on yourself — you're still committed to being your best version and manifesting in your own life. It just means you don't need to have created every single result your client wants.

Expand: You Are NOT the Oracle

You are not expected to have all the answers. You don't need to have created the exact result your client wants. You are the facilitator, not the oracle in the mirror.

If you start acting like the oracle, suddenly you become responsible for their results — which doesn't work because they need to be responsible for their own transformation. They're the ones who have to assimilate everything, take action, and align their life.

Expand: Keep Your Modalities Separate

If you're already a gifted practitioner (Reiki, oracle cards, psychic readings, etc.), do NOT mix these into your Manifesting Coach sessions. Mindvalley can certify you in the manifesting system — they cannot control or certify your intuitive downloads or other modalities.

Why this matters: If you're halfway through a session and you say "I'm receiving a download about your future..." — Mindvalley can't verify that. They can't control if your intuition is accurate. You might tell someone something harmful.

Solution: Keep them as separate containers. After a manifesting session, if the client wants an oracle reading, book that separately. Don't pull oracle cards halfway through a manifesting session.

Expand: When Personal Triggers Come Up

Question: "What if the client shares something that triggers my own stuff?"

Answer: You WILL have moments where clients reflect things back to you that are your stuff to look at. Here's how to handle it:

  1. Recognize it: "Oh wow, this is making me think about my father and how this might be affecting my life."
  2. Tag it: Put a mental tag on it — "that's my piece, not theirs."
  3. Drop it out of the field: Literally visualize click-drag-drop, moving it out of the shared bubble.
  4. Bring your attention back: Return full focus to the client and their intention.
  5. Process it LATER: That night, during lunch, whenever — grab it back and work on it. It's valuable information for your own growth.

You're not bypassing or shoving it under the rug. You're acknowledging it, setting it aside for the session, and processing it afterwards. If you do this cleanly, the client won't feel your personal stuff in the field.

4. Judgment → Curiosity → Appreciation

When working with clients at different stages, you must hold a sovereign system that is completely free of judgment. Clients will share things that may trigger you, shock you, or that you morally disagree with.

Expand: Real Session Example (Disturbing Content Warning)

Regan shared a story from about 15 years ago. A client came in with relationship challenges — fear around intimacy, patterns of sabotaging relationships. As they dug deeper, a childhood memory came up.

The client revealed that as a child, she would find small puppies or kittens, take them home, and burn their feet on the stove until they screamed. Then she would care for them, wrap them up, nurse them back to health.

It took every ounce of Regan's being not to drop into judgment. By staying neutral, she was able to help the client discover the root: her father used to beat her, and right after, he would hug her and say "I love you." Pain became linked to love.

The client was recreating this pattern — creating pain in relationships, then finding love through the healing. Without judgment, they could see it and shift it. At the end, Regan shared her truth: "When you shared that, I had judgment — but I switched to curiosity. Thank you for being brave enough to share so we could unlock this."

The Three States Exercise

Try This: The Water Bottle Exercise

Look at any object (Regan used a water bottle). Move through these three states and notice what happens in your body:

  1. JUDGE IT: Really judge it. "Why is it that shape? The label is tearing. It's not even that big." Notice: body tenses, frowning, negative thoughts.
  2. GET CURIOUS: "I wonder why it was made this size? Who designed this company? What's their story?" Notice: body relaxes, mind opens.
  3. APPRECIATE IT: "This is such a beautiful vessel. It hydrates us. Someone had a vision to help people stay hydrated." Notice: warmth, openness, positive state.

Your Escape Route from Judgment

When you fall into judgment (and you will — you're human), use this sequence:

1. Notice: "Wow, I'm in judgment right now."
2. Get Curious: "I wonder why that is? What's underneath this?"
3. Appreciate: "Thank you for being brave enough to share this so we can unlock it."

Expand: Q&A — What If They Want Something I Disagree With?

Question: "I'm from Colombia. People sometimes come to me wanting to manifest bigger drug operations. Can I still be curious and appreciative?"

Answer: You can be curious and appreciative of their bravery in sharing — and still not help them. If they want to manifest something illegal or harmful, you say "I can't help you with this."

Here's the thing: if you're aligned with the principles, you probably won't even match with that client. You won't get halfway through a session before realizing "this isn't my client." Non-judgment doesn't mean approval or participation in harm.

5. The Ethics of Manifesting

1 Never Impose Yourself on a Client

Never push your own desires, timeline, identity, or what YOU think they should be doing onto them. Don't project your stuff onto them. If you think they could dream bigger, there are tools to guide them to discover that themselves — not by saying "I think it should be 300K, not 100K."

2 No Rescuing, Saving, or Fixing

Do NOT try to rescue, save, fix, or force them. When you do, you disempower them by taking away their responsibility. They need to be consciously responsible for everything they manifest in their life.

3 Respect the Process

Some transformations require time. They might need another session, emotional processing, time to cry, breathe, or ask questions. Trust and listen to that natural process instead of rushing to "the next thing."

4 Honor Both Sovereignties

You are both sovereign beings. Their truth, desires, boundaries, and how much they share — that starts and stops with them. If they're not telling you the deeper story, don't blame them. Ask yourself: "Where am I not creating enough safety and connection?"

Expand: There's Never an Unresourceful Client

If a client isn't going deep, isn't sharing, isn't connecting — it's not a "bad client" or "bad player." Look back at the container. How can you create more safety? More coherence? More connection? They can only go as deep as they proportionally feel safe with you.

Expand: Q&A — What If They Ask for My Advice?

Question: "What if they ask 'Is 100K enough? Is that too much? What do you think?'"

Answer: You don't put your advice onto them. Instead, you ask questions and use tools to have them go deeper and discover that answer themselves. Your job is to facilitate their clarity, not impose your perspective.

6. The 5 Stages of Client Manifestation

Clients will arrive at different stages. Recognize where they are so you can meet them there and guide them forward.

1

Confusion

They don't know what they want. They show up excited but when asked "What are we manifesting today?" they say "I don't know." They might think they know, but after 2 minutes you realize they're confused. You have tools for this.

2

Desire (Unfocused)

They have wishes and desires but lack clarity or alignment. They want to manifest something, but there are misaligned pieces underneath the surface. Your job is to help them go into these places and find alignment.

3

Alignment

They know what they want and need support to embody it, accelerate it, or break through a plateau. Example: "My business is here, I want to scale it there. I know exactly what to do, I just want it to happen quicker or with less stress."

4

Embodiment

They're aligned and ready to integrate the new identity and behaviors needed to accelerate. This is where you help them BE the version that matches the outcome they desire.

5

Manifestation (Integration)

Results are happening! They need integration support. "It's so good, everything's amazing." Now: How do we make sure this compounds? Who do you need to become for the next level? Don't dismiss these clients — help them with "What's next?"

Expand: Q&A — What If Nothing Happens in the Session?

Question: "What if the client isn't able to manifest in the session? How do you manage expectations?"

Answer: There's always a time process. You're not expecting "$100K to appear by the end of the session." Sometimes you work with a client and results show up 3 months later. 6 months later. Years later. Someone might come back years later saying "I tracked it back to when we did that session — thank you."

The only way sessions "don't work" is if the client is completely closed: "I don't know if this works. I don't believe in it." That's why setting it up properly at the beginning matters. The client is responsible for being open. You're responsible for holding the space crystal clear and aligned.

7. Soul-Aligned vs Ego-Driven Desires

As a Manifesting Coach, you help clients set aligned intentions that come from their soul, not their ego. This is critical — great power requires great responsibility.

✓ Soul-Aligned Intentions

  • Come from a frequency of deep truth and knowing
  • Generally have emotion and resonance
  • Feel expansive (even if you don't know how)
  • Increase options in life
  • Feel authentic: "This is just ME"
  • Natural, feels like you can't NOT have it

✗ Ego-Driven Desires

  • Arise from fear: "If I don't do this, bad things happen"
  • Arise from comparison: "Everyone else did it"
  • Arise from scarcity: "I won't have/be enough"
  • Come from societal conditioning: "My family expects..."
  • Driven by validation: "My father will finally be proud"
  • Feel contracting, heavy, pressured
Expand: The Danger of Unconscious Manifestation

So many people think they know what they want, but they're creating a life totally out of alignment with their soul. They're on autopilot, manifesting what society, family, or their industry expects.

They make the money, have the kids, achieve big things — and feel empty inside. They manifested it all, but from the wrong place. Your job is to help them manifest from a place that actually nourishes them, that's truly aligned, that actually serves their soul.

Expand: How to Help Clients Distinguish (Without Telling Them)

You guide them through questions — not by telling them "you're in your ego." The right questions lead to their own "aha" moment.

✗ Don't Say

"You're totally in your ego right now. I don't think we should be creating this. You're just seeking validation from your father."

✓ Do Ask

Questions that lead them to realize: "Oh my god... my entire life has been created trying to get validation and love from my father."

The "aha" and clarity needs to come from THEM, with you holding the space and asking the right questions. If you just tell them, you break rapport, they don't feel understood, they find someone else.

Expand: Money Is Not Inherently Ego

Key clarification: Manifesting money (or anything specific) is NOT automatically from ego. It's the frequency behind it that determines if it's soul-aligned or not.

✗ Ego-Driven Money Goal

"I want $1M in sales commissions so I can prove I'm the top salesperson, call my parents, and show everyone I made it."

✓ Soul-Aligned Money Goal

"I want $1M because with more resources I can do amazing things, bless my children, contribute to causes I care about, and live more abundantly — my soul tells me I'm meant for more."

Regan has had clients making huge amounts of money from pure ego — they shifted the frequency and now make MORE money from a completely different place. She's also had clients with tiny money goals because their ego told them "that's all I can make, I'm not good enough" — when their soul actually wanted $10M/year.

Practice: Check Your Own Creations

To get good at seeing this in clients, first see it in yourself:

• Where have you created from ego unconsciously?
• Where have you created from soul alignment?
• Where did you think it was soul-aligned and later realized it wasn't?

No judgment — get curious, then appreciate the learning. Your soul knows in seconds. Check in: Does this light you up? Does it create expansion? Or does it feel heavy and pressured?

Expand: Q&A — Cultural Conditioning & Soul Alignment

Question: "I grew up Asian with an austerity mindset. Now I'm in a Western environment and want a Ferrari. Is that still ego?"

Answer: You have to tune in and check. Regan grew up on a sailboat sailing around New Zealand — no TV, no internet, no people. She could create a story that she "shouldn't be around people." Is that soul-aligned? Only she can feel that.

Use your "sword of discernment." Tune in: Soul-aligned or not? Compare it to past experiences — what did soul alignment feel like? What did ego feel like? Trust what you feel. Then let it unfold and see if it confirms.

Expand: Q&A — Trauma vs Soul Alignment

Question: "You mentioned ego vs soul-aligned, but also trauma and childhood conditioning. If we believe our soul chose this life, is trauma soul-aligned?"

Answer: Don't mix up these categories. We all have trauma, limiting beliefs, childhood wounds — things blocking us from true soul-aligned manifestation. Whether the trauma was "soul-aligned" doesn't matter. It's information. It's there. What matters is: Where are we going? Is the FUTURE intention soul-aligned or not? Don't get lost analyzing the past — focus on clarity for where they're heading.

8. Key Takeaways

Before Every Session, Remember:

✓

Get grounded and clear. If you're not in a good state (argument with partner, emotional loop), take responsibility to clear it before the session.

✓

Be neutral. Not over-excited, not distracted. Grounded neutrality creates coherence and safety.

✓

Hold the field of infinite possibility. Expect miracles. Whatever they desire, hold "this or better, of course."

✓

You facilitate — you don't create FOR them. Protect their observer seat, amplify and accelerate, but never take responsibility for their results.

✓

When your stuff comes up — tag it, drop it out. Recognize it, set it aside, and process it AFTER the session. Bring your system back to full presence with them.

The minute you think you actually did this and created this — you've lost it. We step out of the way, bring our system into coherence, hold the intention, use the tools, and allow the field to collapse it into form.

— Regan Hillyer
