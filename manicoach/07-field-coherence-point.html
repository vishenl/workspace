1. The Origin: Richard Bartlett

This tool comes from Richard Bartlett (B-A-R-T-L-E-T-T), Regan's mentor for over 15 years — the same teacher behind Click-Drag-Drop Energy Imprinting. He focused on understanding and working with the structure of reality.

Regan's Admission: "When I was using this tool, I actually didn't understand how it worked. I was just like, 'it's amazing!' I could not explain the science behind it. So it's actually quite comforting to know that you could not understand one thing I say, and it will still be really powerful."

The tool is extremely simple for the client to experience — almost too simple. But how it works is fairly complex and deep. The simplicity is what makes it magical.

2. What This Tool Does

The Field Coherence Point brings your client's entire field — their quantum field, every layer — into coherence. You're accessing the pattern that is blocking them and allowing it to collapse and dissolve away.

When to Use It

  • Limiting beliefs — "I don't have enough money to retire"
  • Looping patterns — "I always overspend" or "I always end up in crazy relationships"
  • Emotional states — sadness, fear, feeling stuck
  • Sensations in the body — anything they don't want
  • Identity conflicts — torn between two paths (business vs. motherhood)
  • Unknown blocks — "I feel blocked but I don't know why"

When They Don't Know the Block

If your client says "I just feel blocked at work and I don't know why" — perfect! You don't need to unpack the 10 blocks underneath. Through the quantum field, you're accessing one pinpoint that houses ALL of that information. We're working with all the geometries, wave patterns, frequencies, and key codes sitting there.

Best Timing in a Session

This tool is great early in a session. It heightens all other tools you use after. The client has been completely rewired, they're in the field of infinite potentiality, and their big old blocking pattern has dropped away. Time bends faster after this. You can add it later, but Regan prefers it early, then stacking other tools on top.

3. The Practitioner State

The Cardinal Rule

You must be in the field of your heart. Do not start until you are there.

What This Means

  • You are not in the mind
  • You are not trying to control an outcome
  • You are not visualizing
  • You are not sending energy or information
  • You are not healing them
  • You are not touching points because of Chinese medicine or any system
  • You are doing nothing

If Your Mind Is Active

Regan's technique: Put your mind in an elevator and send it down. Breathe into your heart. Get into that state. Don't touch the client until you're congruently there.

"Anyone can do this. I'm not Regan Hillyer right now. If I was like, 'Hi, I'm Regan Hillyer and I'm going to heal this person with the two points' — it's not gonna work. You have to literally get out of the way into the nothingness. It's magic, God, great spirit, universe — you are not there."

Non-Attachment to Outcome

The less attached you are to the outcome, the more space there is for an even better outcome to take place. You're not observing your wife in this outcome. You're simply holding the field, holding space, witnessing the point of information, and allowing the field to collapse.

4. The Complete Process

POINT 1 (old pattern) + POINT 2 (desired outcome) → JOIN → COLLAPSE

1

Define What You're Working On

Ask: "What's going on in your life that you don't want, that you want to change?"
Then: "If you weren't living that reality, how would it be? What's the outcome?"

You can get them into details — visioning and dreaming about the new reality.

2

Explain the Process (High Level)

"I'm gonna guide you through an amazing process. It's very magical, deep, and profound. It's going to help you drop out the pattern of the thing you no longer want to experience, and allow your life to simply reorganize itself so you can live now in this new reality."

3

Get Conscious Consent for Touch

"We're going to use a small amount of touch on the body. Is it okay with you that I place my hands on your body at some point?"

4

Prepare Them for Reactions

"After I do this, you might notice some sensations. Some people notice tingling. Some might feel a little dizzy. Some people, if it's very strong, their physical body might actually collapse. If this happens, we're gonna catch you. You're safe. Please allow it to happen. The deeper you surrender and lean into this process, the deeper the result. Don't fight it. Don't control it."

5

Invite Client to Stand, Eyes Closed

Have them stand with eyes closed. Connect them briefly with their intention, then invite them to release any attachment to the outcome, any agenda, any expectations. Just surrender and drop into the space.

6

Notice Point 1 (Old Pattern)

Intuitively notice one point on their body. NOT with logic. NOT because Chinese medicine says sadness is stored in the lungs. Just notice where your attention goes — like a 5-year-old would point.

Gently place ONE finger on that point. Don't stab them — just a gentle touch they can feel. This is Point 1, the pinpoint in their field where the old pattern information is stored.

7

Notice Point 2 (Desired Outcome)

Keep your first hand there. With your other hand, notice where Point 2 shows up — the point housing all the information for the state, outcome, and manifestation they want. Touch it the same way. Could be front, back, side — anywhere.

8

Join the Points Together

Create a measurement. In your mind's eye, with your attention, notice that these points are not separate. Connect them — imagine a line, a bridge. They become one. Don't move your hands. Don't run energy. Just observe that they're now linked.

9

Hold & Allow the Recalibration

Stay in the field of your heart. Do nothing. The more you do nothing, the more miraculous the result. Allow them to recalibrate — whatever that looks like. Changes in breath, sweating, dizziness, body movement, falling back, full collapse, unconsciousness.

Your only job: Make sure they're safe. Don't prompt their body. Don't move their arm back. Just support them if they need to fall onto a chair or be caught.

10

Reorientate & Integrate

When they come out of recalibration, feel where they're at. Sometimes they're like "Wow!" and sometimes like a newborn baby. Sit with them. When ready, reaffirm: "This is your reality now. How does this feel? What do you notice that's different?"

5. What's Happening Behind the Scenes

The Measurement Effect

We are constantly creating unconscious measurements — how far to raise your leg when walking, how many steps to the door, how close the chair is. When you measure one point to another, you create a different relationship to it.

When you join two points that were perceived as separate — measuring them together — you create a moment in time. In that moment, all the information changes as to how it's running.

The Wave Function Collapse

"When we hold these two points in neutral awareness, we scramble the old pattern so it can no longer hold its shape. The wave function collapses. In that gap — where there's no past identity running, no attachment to the pattern — that's when the change happens. That's why miracles are instantaneous and don't take time."

How Fast Is the Change?

Even one second is too long. It's instantaneous. The body then catches up. If someone is less open or trying to control the process, it can take longer to assimilate. But in the quantum field, it's already done the moment you join the points.

Pure Potential

In that micro-moment gap, the system enters pure potential — infinite possibilities. The field reorganizes itself to create the most miraculous result for the client, potentially even beyond their Point B, beyond what they asked for. That's why we don't collapse INTO a specific outcome — we just join the points and get out of the way.

6. Physical Reactions & What They Mean

The Nervous System Recalibrating

Why do people go unconscious? Why does the body fall back? It's not the unconscious mind shutting down — it's the nervous system restabilizing. All of the old pattern was stored in the nervous system, and now it has to recalibrate. If it's been held for years, that recalibration can be significant.

Common Reactions

  • Tingling sensations
  • Feeling dizzy or lightheaded
  • Legs going numb
  • Heavy sweating
  • Body wanting to move or sway
  • Emotional release (tears, laughter)
  • Visions or thoughts coming through
  • Partial collapse or buckling
  • Full unconsciousness

Always Feels Good

"I don't know why, but always through the reactions and releases, it feels really good for people. Blissful. Joyful. Even if they're completely knocked out unconscious, when they come back they're like 'I feel so rested.' There's always a very joyous reaction." — They're recalibrating back to their natural state.

No Reaction ≠ No Change

If they don't feel dizzy, collapse, or have any visible reaction — it doesn't mean it hasn't worked. As long as you're in your heart and doing the process properly, it works every single time. Sometimes the client is controlling the process, or their system is stiffer, and the change takes longer to assimilate. But the change happened in the quantum field the moment you joined the points.

7. Safety Considerations

Physical Setup

  • Chair behind them: Have a sturdy chair directly behind the client
  • Professional catcher: Have a second person ready on the side
  • Barefoot: Regan takes her shoes off for better grounding and stability
  • Clear space: Remove obstacles they could bump into
  • Be ready to catch: Even with a catcher, be prepared yourself

Large Clients

If you have a really large client and aren't confident you can catch them, have them stand in front of a bed or have the chair safely against the wall. Help them down onto the chair if they start falling. Have two people available to support.

What NOT to Do

  • Don't freak out if they go unconscious — this is normal
  • Don't move their arm or prompt their body — let it move naturally
  • Don't be attached to "how fast they fell" or what reaction they had
  • Don't contract in fear — stay in celebration that the process is working

Legal Considerations

Have clients sign a standard safety disclaimer/waiver as part of your intake process. Get conscious consent for touch before the session. Explain potential physical reactions so they're prepared and feel safe.

8. FAQ

Can I do this over Zoom?

No. This requires physical touch. There are ways to push it into virtual, but it's not recommended. Safety-wise it can get messy — someone freaking out at home while unconscious. It's not designed for that.

Can I do this on myself?

Yes, but less effective. There's always a part of you scanning for points and running the process. It works, but not as powerfully as receiving it fully from someone else.

How often can I do this?

Once per session maximum. That's enough for one session. You can do it again in the next session, even on the same issue (there can be layers). Over time, you can keep going with different limiting beliefs or patterns.

Can this be a standalone tool?

Yes. You can use it by itself. Though it's particularly powerful when integrated into a larger session where you've built connection first.

Does it work on children?

Yes. For children who are autistic or have other issues, you can feel two points — one for what they want to let go, one for what they want — and do the process. You need conscious consent appropriate to the situation.

Does it work on animals?

Yes. Same process. If the pet has a behavior you want to change, get them in a calm, connected state and do the two points. You'll know if you're pushing something not aligned for them.

Why standing? Can they sit?

Standing gives their body more freedom to move. Sitting works too (especially for safety with large clients), but standing lets you see more of what's happening and gives them more capacity to feel the movement.

How long do I hold the points?

It varies. Sometimes it's seconds — point, point, join, collapse, done. Sometimes it's longer as they recalibrate. Once you join the points, it's already done. You can let go whenever you feel the recalibration starting. Very intuitive.

Do I tell them which point is which?

No. You don't want them in their mind expecting anything. Keep it simple. Just touch the points and let the process happen.

What if they don't know what's blocking them?

Perfect! You don't need to unpack it. Whatever they're experiencing — "I feel blocked at work" — is enough of an access point. The quantum field houses all the information at that pinpoint.
