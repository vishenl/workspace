Then: **Summarize that into one word** — the center point of this activation.

## 3. The Movie Screen Visualization

A huge screen appears in front of you. A movie is playing — and it's a movie of you **already living the reality** where you are the most phenomenal manifesting coach.

### What to Notice in the Movie:

- What is happening in this reality?
- Who's around you? Who's NOT around you?
- What are you saying to yourself?
- How do you KNOW that your intention has materialized?

**Three Ways to Connect:** You may *see* this screen, you may *feel* it, you may just simply know that you are connecting to it. Either way is perfect.

## 4. The Three Dials

Three dials appear on the side of the movie screen:

### Dial 1: Brightness
- Turn it UP — movie becomes brighter and brighter. Full white light.
- Turn it DOWN — pitch black. Take all the light out.
- **Settle it in the middle** — exactly where it's meant to be.

### Dial 2: Volume
- Turn it UP — all the sounds AND everything you're saying to yourself in your own head. Full sound.
- Turn it DOWN — take all sound out. Silence.
- **Settle at the perfect volume** — internal and external sounds aligned.

### Dial 3: Feeling (Only Up)
- This dial is different — **you only turn it up**
- It increases how good it FEELS to be you in the movie
- **Turn it all the way to FULL FEELING**

**Why Calibrate?** Turning brightness and volume up/down/settle teaches the subconscious it has control over the experience. The feeling dial only goes up because that's the frequency you lock in before jumping in.
