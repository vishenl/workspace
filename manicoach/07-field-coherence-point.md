The Field Coherence Point brings your client's entire field — their quantum field, every layer — into coherence. You're accessing the pattern that is blocking them and allowing it to collapse and dissolve away.

## 4. The Complete Process

```
POINT 1 (old pattern) + POINT 2 (desired outcome) → JOIN → COLLAPSE
```

### Step 1: Define What You're Working On

**Ask:** "What's going on in your life that you don't want, that you want to change?"
**Then:** "If you weren't living that reality, how would it be? What's the outcome?"

You can get them into details — visioning and dreaming about the new reality.

### Step 2: Explain the Process (High Level)

> "I'm gonna guide you through an amazing process. It's very magical, deep, and profound. It's going to help you drop out the pattern of the thing you no longer want to experience, and allow your life to simply reorganize itself so you can live now in this new reality."

### Step 3: Get Conscious Consent for Touch

> "We're going to use a small amount of touch on the body. Is it okay with you that I place my hands on your body at some point?"

### Step 4: Prepare Them for Reactions

> "After I do this, you might notice some sensations. Some people notice tingling. Some might feel a little dizzy. Some people, if it's very strong, their physical body might actually collapse. If this happens, we're gonna catch you. You're safe. Please allow it to happen. The deeper you surrender and lean into this process, the deeper the result."

### Step 5: Invite Client to Stand, Eyes Closed

Have them stand with eyes closed. Connect them briefly with their intention, then invite them to release any attachment to the outcome, any agenda, any expectations. Just surrender and drop into the space.

### Step 6: Notice Point 1 (Old Pattern)

**Intuitively** notice one point on their body. NOT with logic. NOT because Chinese medicine says sadness is stored in the lungs. Just notice where your attention goes — like a 5-year-old would point.

Gently place ONE finger on that point. This is Point 1, the pinpoint in their field where the old pattern information is stored.

### Step 7: Notice Point 2 (Desired Outcome)

Keep your first hand there. With your other hand, notice where Point 2 shows up — the point housing all the information for the state, outcome, and manifestation they want. Touch it the same way. Could be front, back, side — anywhere.

### Step 8: Join the Points Together

**Create a measurement.** In your mind's eye, with your attention, notice that these points are not separate. Connect them — imagine a line, a bridge. They become one. Don't move your hands. Don't run energy. Just observe that they're now linked.

### Step 9: Hold & Allow the Recalibration

Stay in the field of your heart. Do nothing. The more you do nothing, the more miraculous the result. Allow them to recalibrate — whatever that looks like.

**Your only job:** Make sure they're safe. Don't prompt their body. Don't move their arm back. Just support them if they need to fall onto a chair or be caught.

### Step 10: Reorientate & Integrate

When they come out of recalibration, feel where they're at. Sometimes they're like "Wow!" and sometimes like a newborn baby. Sit with them. When ready, reaffirm: "This is your reality now. How does this feel? What do you notice that's different?"

## 6. Physical Reactions & What They Mean

### The Nervous System Recalibrating

Why do people go unconscious? Why does the body fall back? It's not the unconscious mind shutting down — it's the **nervous system restabilizing**. All of the old pattern was stored in the nervous system, and now it has to recalibrate.

### Common Reactions

- Tingling sensations
- Feeling dizzy or lightheaded
- Legs going numb
- Heavy sweating
- Body wanting to move or sway
- Emotional release (tears, laughter)
- Visions or thoughts coming through
- Partial collapse or buckling
- Full unconsciousness

### Always Feels Good

> "I don't know why, but always through the reactions and releases, it feels really good for people. Blissful. Joyful. Even if they're completely knocked out unconscious, when they come back they're like 'I feel so rested.'"

### No Reaction ≠ No Change

If they don't have a visible reaction — it doesn't mean it hasn't worked. The change happened in the quantum field the moment you joined the points. Sometimes the client's system is stiffer, and the change takes longer to assimilate.

## 7. Safety Considerations

### Physical Setup

- **Chair behind them:** Have a sturdy chair directly behind the client
- **Professional catcher:** Have a second person ready on the side
- **Barefoot:** Take shoes off for better grounding and stability
- **Clear space:** Remove obstacles they could bump into

### Large Clients

Have them stand in front of a bed or have the chair safely against the wall. Have two people available to support.

### What NOT to Do

- Don't freak out if they go unconscious — this is normal
- Don't move their arm or prompt their body — let it move naturally
- Don't be attached to "how fast they fell"
- Don't contract in fear — stay in celebration

### Legal Considerations

Have clients sign a standard safety disclaimer/waiver. Get conscious consent for touch before the session.

## 8. FAQ

### Can I do this over Zoom?

**No.** This requires physical touch. Safety-wise it can get messy — someone unconscious at home alone.

### Can I do this on myself?

**Yes, but less effective.** There's always a part of you scanning for points and running the process.

### How often can I do this?

**Once per session maximum.** You can do it again in the next session, even on the same issue (there can be layers).

### Can this be a standalone tool?

**Yes.** You can use it by itself, though it's particularly powerful integrated into a larger session.

### Does it work on children?

**Yes.** You need conscious consent appropriate to the situation.

### Does it work on animals?

**Yes.** Same process. Get them in a calm, connected state.

### Why standing? Can they sit?

Standing gives their body more freedom to move. Sitting works too, especially for safety with large clients.

### How long do I hold the points?

It varies. Once you join the points, it's already done. Very intuitive — you'll feel when the recalibration starts.

### Do I tell them which point is which?

**No.** You don't want them in their mind expecting anything.

### What if they don't know what's blocking them?

Perfect! Whatever they're experiencing is enough of an access point.
