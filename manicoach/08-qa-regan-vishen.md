## 4. Homework: Create Your Alter Ego

Like Beyonce has Sasha Fierce — create an alter ego for yourself. Sometimes it's hard to say "I'm gonna be more confident" or "I'm gonna dress more sexy." So pretend it's not you. Pretend it's your doppelganger.

### The Exercise

1. **Name your alter ego** — Give it a name you like to say (you don't have to share it)
2. **How does it dress?**
3. **How does it speak?**
4. **Where does it live?**
5. **What's its lifestyle?**
6. **Spend 5-10 minutes** playing with this before bed

### How to Use It

In moments — in a meeting on Zoom, walking into a mall — just step into that alter ego and watch what happens. You can program yourself very quickly.

### Vishen's Example

> "During quantum jumping, I saw the healthy version of myself with space for exercise next to my desk. I forgot about it. But earlier, while Regan was teaching, I unconsciously got off my chair, moved it, and started doing one-legged squats. I wondered, 'Why am I doing this?' — I had subconsciously stepped into my alter ego."

## 5. How Regan Manifests (Advanced)

> "If you asked me, 'Regan, what does your life look like in 10 years?' — I actually don't know. And that might create a lot of 'Well, you should know! You're the manifestation girl!' But I know it's gonna be so freaking good. I know what it feels like. I know how amazing, aligned, abundant, joyous, and expansive it is. How that looks in the 3D? I have no idea."

### Why No Specific Vision?

"The minute I try to go 'Well, it could look like this' — I actually put a cap and a ceiling on it. I create a box that doesn't need to be there."

**Important Distinction:** This is for advanced practitioners. When clients are starting out, they still need: "What do you want? What's your intention?" The box IS important for beginners.

### The Advanced Practice

1. Connect with the **highest version of you** — the version right before they fold back into source/consciousness
2. What's their dream? Their prayer? What change did they want to see?
3. Say: **"That's my intention NOW"** — not in the future
4. The mind doesn't even know how big or shiny or abundant it is
5. Surrender to that frequency and live your life in alignment with it

### What Happens

"Manifestations start appearing that you're like, 'Whoa, I couldn't even dream this up!' Divine synchronicities line up because you're in alignment. You're bending time and folding your future so rapidly that it's beyond what you could consciously tap into."
