## 4. The Four Energy Centers

The golden ball drops through each energy center in sequence. At each center: **spin → emit frequency → clear old → activate new**.

### Center 1: Brain (Crown → Brain Center)

The ball drops from above the crown into the center of your brain.

- **Spin:** Faster and faster until it becomes a blur of golden white light, illuminating the brain center

### Center 2: Third Eye (Vision Center)

The ball floats from the brain center into the third eye.

- **Spin:** Turn it on, spin faster and faster. Frequency floods your vision vortex

### Center 3: Throat (Expression Vortex)

The ball floats from the third eye into the center of the throat.

- **Spin:** Faster and faster, assimilating all information from the beingness of your highest self
- **Clear:** Old patterns that stopped you from saying YES, saying NO, embodying your expressed truth — the truth of your soul
- **Activate:** Throat opens and expands, creating more space to hold higher frequencies with ease, grace, and flow

### Center 4: Heart (The Portal)

The ball floats from the throat into the very center of the heart. This center gets the most intensive work.

- **Spin:** Permeating the frequency of your highest essence through the heart
- **Clear:** Old information that stopped the heart being the true portal for all information in and out. Heart returns to crystalline state
- **Activate:** Heart opens and expands to hold even more of this frequency

**Breath 1:** Deep breath into the heart. Hold. Spin faster. Sip in more air. Expand, open. The vibration shifts, alchemizes, recalibrates. Expands your capacity to receive.

**Breath 2:** Hold. Float with the next sip of air. Open up the back of the heart. The wings of the heart expand and float. Even the little nooks and crannies of the heart open.

**Breath 3:** Third and final breath. Open, expand. Spin faster and faster. Creating even more space to anchor, hold, ground, and stabilize the purest frequency of your highest self.
