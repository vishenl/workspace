## 1. The Heart of Service

A Manifesting Coach is someone walking the path with a heart of service who genuinely cares about helping others while simultaneously committed to their own evolution and growth.

### The Dual Commitment

> You can only help someone to the extent that you are also helping yourself. When you meet someone truly in their own alchemy and evolution — there's an unmistakable energy about them.

### Being More YOU

This work is about **being more YOU** — not becoming someone else. It's about connecting with the version of you that already exists in the future and folding that version into the now using quantum entanglement principles.

When someone walks into a room and there's just *something* about them — that magnetic quality — it's usually because they're being the fullest version of themselves. Not a lower version, not an unconscious default version, but a consciously chosen higher version.

### Great Power, Great Responsibility

You'll learn how to bend and restructure reality through the quantum field. This comes with serious personal responsibility.

When you hold space for someone else's manifestation, you open a joint field — a shared field — and information transfers. If you're not crystal clear, grounded, in integrity, and authentic, you can create a mess for both of you.

## 2. The Quantum Field & Shared Field

You are connected to the quantum field where you have access to all information simultaneously, regardless of time, space, or distance. Through the observer effect, you can alter information in the quantum field, which then creates a different external reflection in your reality.

### The Shared Field (The Bubble)

When you work with someone as a Manifesting Coach, you create a **shared field** — like a bubble. Inside this bubble, you and your client have your individual fields, but together you're entering a shared quantum field. As you guide them through processes, you are holding the space and **accelerating their process**.

> "The truth is seen with eyes unclouded by longing."
> — Emily Fletcher

### Desire vs. Longing

| Desire (Soul Whisper) ✓ | Longing (Attachment) ✗ |
|-------------------------|------------------------|
| Your soul whispering something has already happened | Attachment to it happening a certain way |
| Feels expansive and exciting | Control energy: "This MUST happen" |
| Surrendered to HOW it shows up | Puts the whole field into contraction |
| Creates expansion in your field | Prevents the field from rearranging |

### Q&A: Can I Do Multiple Activations?

**Question:** "Can I do creative activations for a particular goal multiple times, or does that create longing?"

**Answer:** You can do 100 activations — even 200 — IF you're not in longing, attachment, or control energy. If it's putting you in that anxious space, stop. But if you're doing them from "of course this is happening, I'm accelerating the process" — go for it. It's not about the number; it's about the frequency you're in.

## 3. The Role of a Manifesting Coach

### You Are NOT Creating FOR the Client

You are not taking on their dreams and making them happen. You are **protecting the integrity of the client sitting in the observer seat**, amplifying their energy, and accelerating the process. The minute you think YOU made something happen — you've lost it.

### What You Actually Do

- **Hold a field of infinite possibility** — where anything powerful and good could happen
- **Protect the energetics** within the shared bubble
- **Amplify and accelerate** through your presence and principles
- **Guide them through processes** using the tools you're taught
- **Expect miracles** — hold a frequency that overpowers any doubt they have

### Coach vs Mentor — You Don't Need Their Results

**Question:** "Do I have to have manifested something myself before I can help someone else manifest it?"

**Answer:** NO. You are a **coach**, not a mentor. A mentor says "I have done this, let me show you the steps." A coach says "I can hold the field and help you manifest whatever you want."

Your client might want a rare Ferrari you've never owned. You can still help them. This doesn't mean you're not working on yourself — you're still committed to being your best version.

### You Are NOT the Oracle

You are not expected to have all the answers. You are the **facilitator**, not the oracle in the mirror.

If you start acting like the oracle, suddenly *you* become responsible for their results — which doesn't work because they need to be responsible for their own transformation.

### Keep Your Modalities Separate

If you're already a gifted practitioner (Reiki, oracle cards, psychic readings), do NOT mix these into your Manifesting Coach sessions. Mindvalley can certify you in the manifesting system — they cannot certify your other modalities.

**Solution:** Keep them as separate containers. After a manifesting session, if the client wants an oracle reading, book that separately.

### When Personal Triggers Come Up

You WILL have moments where clients reflect things back to you that are your stuff to look at. Here's how to handle it:

1. **Recognize it:** "Oh wow, this is making me think about my father."
2. **Tag it:** Put a mental tag — "that's my piece, not theirs."
3. **Drop it out of the field:** Visualize moving it out of the shared bubble.
4. **Bring attention back:** Return full focus to the client.
5. **Process it LATER:** Work on it after the session.

## 4. Judgment → Curiosity → Appreciation

When working with clients, you must hold a sovereign system that is **completely free of judgment**. Clients will share things that may trigger you, shock you, or that you morally disagree with.

### Exercise: The Water Bottle

Look at any object. Move through these three states and notice what happens in your body:

1. **JUDGE IT:** "Why is it that shape?" Notice: body tenses, frowning.
2. **GET CURIOUS:** "I wonder why it was made this size?" Notice: body relaxes, mind opens.
3. **APPRECIATE IT:** "This is such a beautiful vessel." Notice: warmth, openness.

### Your Escape Route from Judgment

1. **Notice:** "Wow, I'm in judgment right now."
2. **Get Curious:** "I wonder why that is?"
3. **Appreciate:** "Thank you for being brave enough to share."

### Real Session Example

Regan shared a story from about 15 years ago. A client came in with relationship challenges. As they dug deeper, the client revealed childhood behavior that was deeply disturbing (harming animals as a child).

It took every ounce of her being not to drop into judgment. By staying neutral, she was able to help the client discover the root: her father used to beat her, and right after, he would hug her and say "I love you." Pain became linked to love.

**At the end, Regan shared:** "When you shared that, I had judgment — but I switched to curiosity. Thank you for being brave enough to share so we could unlock this."

## 5. The Ethics of Manifesting

### 1. Never Impose Yourself

Never push your own desires, timeline, or what YOU think they should be doing onto them. If you think they could dream bigger, there are tools to guide them to discover that themselves.

### 2. No Rescuing, Saving, or Fixing

Do NOT try to rescue, save, fix, or force them. When you do, you **disempower** them by taking away their responsibility.

### 3. Respect the Process

Some transformations require time. They might need another session, emotional processing, time to cry or breathe. Trust that natural process.

### 4. Honor Both Sovereignties

You are both sovereign beings. Their truth, desires, boundaries — that starts and stops with them. If they're not sharing deeply, ask: "Where am I not creating enough safety?"

## 6. The 5 Stages of Client Manifestation

Clients will arrive at different stages. Recognize where they are so you can meet them there and guide them forward.

| Stage | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **1. Confusion** | They don't know what they want. "What are we manifesting?" "I don't know." You have tools for this. |
| **2. Desire (Unfocused)** | They have wishes but lack clarity or alignment. Misaligned pieces underneath the surface. |
| **3. Alignment** | They know what they want, need support to embody or accelerate. "I know what to do, I just want it quicker." |
| **4. Embodiment** | They're aligned and ready to integrate the new identity and behaviors needed to accelerate. |
| **5. Manifestation** | Results are happening! They need integration support. "What's next? How do we compound this?" |

### Q&A: What If Nothing Happens in the Session?

There's always a time process. You're not expecting "$100K to appear by the end of the session." Sometimes results show up 3 months later. Years later.

The only way sessions "don't work" is if the client is completely closed. **The client is responsible for being open. You're responsible for holding the space crystal clear.**

## 7. Soul-Aligned vs Ego-Driven Desires

As a Manifesting Coach, you help clients set **aligned intentions** that come from their soul, not their ego.

| Soul-Aligned Intentions ✓ | Ego-Driven Desires ✗ |
|---------------------------|----------------------|
| Come from deep truth and knowing | Arise from **fear** |
| Feel **expansive** | Arise from **comparison** |
| **Increase** options in life | Arise from **scarcity** |
| Feel authentic: "This is just ME" | Come from **societal conditioning** |
| Have emotion and resonance | Driven by **validation** |

### The Danger of Unconscious Manifestation

So many people think they know what they want, but they're creating a life totally out of alignment with their soul. They're on autopilot.

They make the money, have the kids, achieve big things — and feel **empty inside**. They manifested it all, but from the wrong place.

### How to Help Clients Distinguish

You guide them through **questions** — not by telling them "you're in your ego."

- **Don't say:** "You're totally in your ego. You're just seeking validation from your father."
- **Do ask:** Questions that lead them to realize: "Oh my god... my entire life has been created trying to get validation from my father."

The "aha" needs to come from THEM.

### Money Is Not Inherently Ego

Manifesting money is NOT automatically from ego. It's the **frequency behind it** that determines if it's soul-aligned.

- **Ego:** "I want $1M so I can prove I'm the top salesperson."
- **Soul:** "I want $1M because with more resources I can do amazing things — my soul tells me I'm meant for more."

### Practice: Check Your Own Creations

- Where have you created from ego unconsciously?
- Where have you created from soul alignment?
- Where did you think it was soul-aligned and later realized it wasn't?

No judgment — get curious, then appreciate the learning.

## 8. Key Takeaways

### Before Every Session, Remember:

- **Get grounded and clear.** If you're not in a good state, take responsibility to clear it before the session.
- **Be neutral.** Not over-excited, not distracted. Grounded neutrality creates coherence and safety.
- **Hold the field of infinite possibility.** Expect miracles. Hold "this or better, of course."
- **You facilitate — you don't create FOR them.** Protect their observer seat, amplify and accelerate.
- **When your stuff comes up — tag it, drop it out.** Process it AFTER the session.

> "The minute you think you actually did this and created this — you've lost it. We step out of the way, bring our system into coherence, hold the intention, use the tools, and allow the field to collapse it into form."
> — Regan Hillyer
