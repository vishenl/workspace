Part One

The Visibility
Wound

Why founders hide—and how to heal

"Hey Hayden, check it out..."

In 2015, I stood outside Steve Jobs' house. I'd been running Mindvalley for 10 years. $30-40M revenue. No VC.

But I couldn't share on social media. My hack? Record for an audience of one: my 6-year-old son.

"Miss you Hayden. See you soon."

That way, nobody could tell me I sucked.

(The funny thing? My son didn't really care.)

The Visibility Wound

A fear of being seen. Shockingly common among founders.

"Who am I to share this?"

"What will people think?"

"I'm not an expert."

"My friends are lawyers—I sell meditation CDs."

I told Jay Shetty:

"I'm 44 years old. I'm too old.
I don't have your hair."

Jay said:

"V, it's all bullshit excuses.
You're fine. Just start."
