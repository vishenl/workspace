Section 1: Teaching Recap

Goal: Remind them of the value they received. Create emotional connection to the content. Set up the "tip of the iceberg" frame.

Slide 1

Opening Hook — Biggest Breakthrough

⏱ ~45 seconds
Word-for-Word Script

Alright everyone, PAUSE 2 SEC I want you to do something for me.

Think back to the last three days. PAUSE

What was your biggest breakthrough?

Not just "Oh, that was cool." I mean the one insight that made you think — "THAT could change everything for me."

Take a moment. PAUSE 3 SEC Write it down if you haven't already.

Because here's the thing... that one insight could be worth millions. Literally millions. And you got it for free.

Delivery Notes

  • Start with calm, reflective energy
  • Make eye contact during pauses — let them actually think
  • Build intensity on "could be worth millions"
Slide 2

Value Quantification — Expert Rates

⏱ ~60 seconds
Word-for-Word Script

Let me put these last three days in perspective for you.

Brendan Kane — the guy who built a million followers in 30 days — his consulting rate is $3,000 an hour. You got hours of him. For free.

John Lee? His keynote fee is $100,000. Companies pay that for ONE hour on stage. You got multiple sessions.

Add it all up — the combined expertise you received over three days? At minimum, $50,000 worth of knowledge. PAUSE

And you got it for the price of showing up.

Delivery Notes

  • Let the numbers land — slow down on each stat
  • Use hand gestures to emphasize the values
  • "$50,000" should feel like a revelation, not a boast
Slide 3

Tip of the Iceberg Frame

⏱ ~30 seconds
Word-for-Word Script

But here's what I need you to understand... PAUSE

That was just the tip of the iceberg.

What you learned these three days? Think of it like this: We gave you the keys to a Ferrari. PAUSE

Now... you need the driving lessons.

Delivery Notes

  • Shift from gratitude to anticipation
  • The Ferrari metaphor should create a visual — gesture like you're holding keys
  • End with a slight smile — you're about to reveal something good
