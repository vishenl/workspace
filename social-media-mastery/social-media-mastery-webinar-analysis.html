Executive Summary

This is a solid informational pitch with excellent teachers and strong social proof, but it significantly underperforms on Fladlien fundamentals. The close is leaving substantial conversions on the table.

Primary Issues: No running totals on bonuses (critical), only ~8 CTAs instead of 26+, no guarantee section, weak stair-step pricing, and incomplete objection handling. These are all fixable with targeted rewrites.

Critical Gaps — Fix These First

CRITICAL

Gap #1: Missing Running Totals on Bonuses

You have 8 excellent bonuses worth $24,500 — but the audience never hears a cumulative total until the very end. Each bonus feels incremental rather than additive. This is the #1 most impactful missing element in the entire close.

Impact: This alone could increase conversions 10-15%

Fix: After EVERY bonus, state the running total: "That's $X in bonuses so far. But wait, there's more..."

CRITICAL

Gap #2: No Guarantee Section
