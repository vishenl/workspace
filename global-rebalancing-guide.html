The Big Picture

We are witnessing a historic shift in global economic power. The US dollar's dominance is weakening, China is rising as the world's trading hub, and traditional allies are quietly diversifying away from America.

For everyday investors with retirement accounts, this creates both risks and opportunities. Understanding these shifts can help you make more informed decisions about your portfolio.

NINE KEY TRENDS

What's Driving the Shift

DECLINING Trend 1 of 9

The Dollar Is Losing Ground

The US dollar had its worst first-half performance in over 50 years, falling 11% in early 2025. This isn't a temporary blip — it's structural change driven by policy uncertainty.

Why It's Happening

  • Tariff policies triggered global loss of confidence
  • Foreign Treasury ownership dropped from 50% to 33%
  • Major banks forecast another 10-20% decline

Gold Is the Big Winner

When people lose faith in governments and currencies, they turn to gold. Gold is up 55% in 2025, hitting $4,000/oz for the first time in history.

Key Evidence

EXPANDING Trend 3 of 9

China Is Winning the Technology Race

Despite US tariffs, China posted a $1.2 trillion trade surplus — the largest ever. More importantly, they now lead in 66 of 74 critical technologies tracked globally.

The Tech Shift

  • 2000: US led 90%+ of technologies
  • 2025: China leads 89% (66 of 74)
  • US now leads in only 8 technologies
  • China dominates: EVs, batteries, solar, nuclear, 5G

WARNING Trend 5 of 9

The AI Bubble Is Real — But Not All Stocks Are Equal

The "Magnificent 7" tech stocks make up 35% of the S&P 500 — the highest concentration ever. But valuations vary wildly. Some are dangerously overpriced; others offer reasonable value.

Why It's a Bubble

  • AI infrastructure spending: $400B/year
  • Actual AI revenue: ~$13B (OpenAI)
  • Capex-to-revenue ratio is 30:1 — unsustainable
  • Even Bezos and Altman warn of overinvestment

DATA Trend 7 of 9

"GOP Is Better for Business" Is a Myth

Despite common perception, historical data shows the opposite. Since 1947, every major economic indicator has performed better under Democratic administrations.

Key Economic Indicators (1947-2024)

  • Stock Market: +10.8%/yr vs +6.9%/yr
  • GDP Growth: +4.3%/yr vs +2.5%/yr
  • Job Creation: +2.6%/yr vs +1.0%/yr

Blue = Democratic | Red = Republican

Important: Correlation ≠ causation. Many factors affect the economy. But the pattern is consistent across 75+ years and multiple peer-reviewed studies (Blinder & Watson, American Economic Review, 2016).

Average annual growth rates by party (1947-2024)

Current Relevance

With a Republican administration in office through at least January 2029, historical patterns suggest potential headwinds. The tariff policies and resulting global confidence loss align with this historical pattern — though unique factors like AI and global realignment may override past trends.

For International Investors: Crypto Considerations

Arguments For Allocation
  • Regulatory clarity improving (ETFs approved)
  • Low correlation to traditional assets long-term
  • JPMorgan now accepts BTC as collateral
  • Hedge against local currency devaluation
  • Stablecoins useful for cross-border payments
Arguments Against
  • Failed as inflation hedge in 2025
  • Extreme volatility (-23.5% in one quarter)
  • Quantum computing poses existential risk
  • Trades like risk asset during stress
  • Gold outperformed 10:1 in 2025

Expert Advice

"Allocate, don't speculate." — NYDIG Research. If you want crypto exposure, keep it small (1-5% of portfolio), use regulated ETFs (IBIT, FBTC), and treat it as a long-term allocation rather than a trade. Don't try to time cycles.
