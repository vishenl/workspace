Interactions & Timing Notes
Important rules to follow
🔄
ALA chelates minerals — Alpha Lipoic Acid is taken at 1 PM (fed). Zinc and Magnesium are at night. Keep 6+ hours apart.
🔗
Collagen needs Vitamin C — Always take Vitamin C with your collagen at 5 PM. Without it, collagen peptides cannot form strong cross-linked fibers.
⏰
L-Tyrosine moved to morning fasted — Was post-gym in your old stack. Dopamine precursors work BEFORE exertion, not after.
☀️
Vitamin D3 must be daily — London + light brown skin = significantly reduced synthesis. Your 41.8 ng/mL needs to reach 60–80. Take 5,000 IU every day Oct–Apr.
💊
Boron changed to daily — Your SHBG is high at 66.9 (binding up testosterone). Every-2-days dosing is insufficient. Take 6–10mg daily to lower SHBG.
🍷
On alcohol days — Skip Niacin (flushing), reduce Berberine to 500mg or skip (both liver-processed). Add DHM + Glutathione protocol.
🧪
Zinc at night may cause nausea — If empty stomach bothers you, take 3–4 nuts with it, or switch to Zinc Bisglycinate (gentler form).
🌿
Ashwagandha caution — Nightshade family. Given your histamine sensitivity with NAC, start at 300mg and monitor for any itching before increasing.
