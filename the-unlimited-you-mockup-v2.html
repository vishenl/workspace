− Is this really free? What's the catch?
Yes, it's genuinely free—no credit card required. We offer this because once people experience these techniques, some choose to deepen their practice through our paid programs. But this course stands completely on its own with no obligation.
+ I've tried meditation before and couldn't quiet my mind. Will this work?
Yes—because Silva is active meditation. The guided audio uses Alpha-frequency tones that help your brain shift states naturally. You're doing mental exercises, not just sitting there. Many who struggled with traditional meditation find this approach much more engaging.
+ How long until I see results?
Many people feel noticeably calmer after their first Centering Exercise. Visualization methods often produce shifts within days to weeks. Like any skill, more practice means stronger results.
+ How much time do I need to invest?
The Centering Exercise takes 15-20 minutes daily. Each lesson takes 15-30 minutes to read. Total: about 2-3 hours per week initially, dropping to 15-20 minutes daily once you've learned the techniques.
+ Is this scientific or "woo-woo"?
Jose Silva was an electronics repairman who applied scientific thinking to the mind. The Alpha brainwave state is measurable with EEG. Studies at Duke University and research by Dr. J.B. Rhine have validated many of these principles. This is practical, not mystical.
