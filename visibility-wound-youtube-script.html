2. WHAT IS THE VISIBILITY WOUND?

4:00 - 6:00

"So what exactly is the visibility wound?"

The Visibility Wound

A deep-seated fear of being truly seen. The belief that if people really knew you — your ideas, your face, your voice — they would reject you. So you hide. Even when you're successful. Especially when you're successful.

"It shows up in different ways:"

"You have something important to say... but you don't say it."

"You watch less qualified people get the spotlight... and you stay silent."

"You build something amazing... but you let others take the credit."

"You have a win... but you can't bring yourself to share it."

The Irony

"Here's the cruel irony: the visibility wound often hits successful people the hardest. Because the more you have to lose, the more terrifying it is to be seen."

"I've met billionaires who can't post on LinkedIn. CEOs who hide behind their company accounts. Founders who've built empires... but can't show their face."

"And every single one of them is leaving millions of dollars on the table. Because in the new economy, attention is the asset. And they're invisible."

This section is educational. Slower pace. Help them recognize themselves in what you're describing.

5. HOW TO HEAL THE VISIBILITY WOUND

10:30 - 13:00

"So how do you actually heal this?"

"I'm not going to pretend I'm fully healed. The visibility wound doesn't disappear — it comes back. It came back for me just this March. I had wins I couldn't share. Opportunities I was afraid to talk about."

"But I've learned to work with it. Here's what helps:"

5 Steps to Heal the Visibility Wound

  • 1 Name it — You can't fight what you can't see. Call it what it is: a wound, not a weakness.
  • 2 Start with an audience of one — Like I did with Hayden. Write for one person. Talk to one person. Make it small.
  • 3 Share the messy — Vulnerability creates connection. Your failures are more interesting than your wins.
  • 4 Separate identity from content — A post that doesn't land isn't a reflection of your worth. It's just a post.
  • 5 Remember the cost of silence — Every day you hide is a day someone who needed your message didn't hear it.

"Here's the reframe that helped me most:"

"Staying invisible isn't humble. It's selfish."

"You have ideas that could help people. Experiences that could inspire them. A perspective that could change how they see the world."

"And you're keeping it to yourself... because you're afraid of what strangers on the internet might think?"

"That's not protecting yourself. That's depriving others of what you have to give."

"Staying invisible isn't humble — it's selfish" is a pattern interrupt. It reframes everything. Deliver with conviction.
