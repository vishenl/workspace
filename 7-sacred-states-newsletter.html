What Does "Unfuckwithable" Actually Mean?

Let me be precise about this, because it's not just a catchy word.

Unfuckwithable means you're truly at peace and in touch with yourself. Nothing anyone says or does bothers you, and no negativity can touch you.

It doesn't mean you don't feel emotions. You do — deeply.

It doesn't mean you don't care. You care — profoundly.

It doesn't mean you're detached from life. You're more engaged than ever.

But you've reached a state where external circumstances — the chaos, the criticism, the setbacks, the uncertainty — simply cannot disturb your inner core.

The ancient Greeks had a word for this: Ataraxia. A state of serene calmness that cannot be shaken by external events.

But here's the thing: this isn't about adopting Stoic philosophy or any particular belief system. This is about something far more practical.

"Your goals will only match your frequency."

This single insight changed everything for me.

The Hidden Flaw in How We Approach Life

Here's what I've realized after 22 years of studying human transformation:

Most people try to change their external circumstances while operating from a broken internal state.

They set goals from anxiety — and create more things to be anxious about.

They make decisions from confusion — and create more confusion.

They build relationships from scarcity — and attract scarcity.

Contrary to popular belief: If you set goals from confusion, from anxiety, from limiting beliefs — you limit yourself. The state you're in when you make a decision determines the quality of that decision. Always.

Think about it. When you sit down to plan your new year, what state are you usually in?

Exhausted. Scattered. Anxious about whether things will work out. Still carrying wounds from past failures.

And from that place — you try to design your dream life?

It doesn't work. It can't work. The math doesn't add up.

The AHA

You don't need better goals. You need a better state. When you upgrade your internal operating system, your goals, decisions, and actions automatically upgrade with it. This is the leverage point most people miss entirely.

The 7 Sacred States

After years of research, thousands of interviews with peak performers, and deep study of both ancient wisdom and modern neuroscience, I've identified seven specific internal states that, when cultivated, make you unfuckwithable.

These aren't abstract concepts. Each one is a trainable skill — a state you can practice and eventually embody as your default way of being.

Let me walk you through all seven:

State One

Knowingness

Understanding who you are. Getting clear on your values. Embodying absolute self-respect.

This is the foundation everything else builds upon. Knowingness means you understand who you are at the deepest level. You're clear on your values, your worth, your non-negotiables. You don't need external validation because you already know. The universe reflects who you ARE, not what you want. When you stand in your worth with unshakeable certainty, opportunities align with your true self — because you're no longer sending mixed signals to the world.

State Two

Faith

Trusting before proof. Releasing the need to control every outcome. Surrendering to something greater.

This isn't religious faith (though it can include that). This is the ability to trust before you have proof. To move forward confidently even when you can't see the full path ahead. Here's the reframe that changes everything: What's meant for you cannot miss you. What misses you was never yours. When you embody faith, you stop white-knuckling life. You stop trying to force outcomes. You take action from inspiration rather than desperation — and paradoxically, that's when things start flowing.

State Three

Love

Generating love within. Radiating it outward. Transforming relationships through presence.

Love is not weakness — it's power. And I'm not talking about romantic love. I'm talking about the ability to generate the feeling of love within yourself and beam it outward. HeartMath research shows this literally changes your biology — your heart rhythm, your brain function, even your immune system. It also changes the biology of people around you. When you operate from love, doors open that force could never unlock. Relationships transform. Conflicts dissolve. You become magnetic.

State Four

Stillness

Finding unshakeable calm within chaos. Accessing your deepest wisdom. Becoming the eye of the storm.

In a world that's constantly screaming for your attention, stillness is your competitive advantage. Your best ideas, clearest decisions, and deepest intuition all come from the quiet. The warrior's secret weapon isn't aggression — it's the ability to remain still when everyone else is panicking. This is the state where you access what some call intuition, others call inner knowing, and scientists call the default mode network. It's where your best thinking happens.

State Five

Non-Attachment

Wanting it. Working for it. But not needing it. Grip loosely and paradoxically achieve more.

This is perhaps the most misunderstood state. Non-attachment doesn't mean you don't care. It means you want something, you work for it with everything you have — but you don't need it. Steve Jobs got fired from Apple. He shrugged, started Pixar, and eventually returned to build the most valuable company on Earth. That's non-attachment in action. When rejection and setbacks lose their sting, you become antifragile. You take bigger swings. You recover faster. You play the long game without the emotional turbulence that derails most people.

Key Insight

Non-attachment is not apathy. It's the ability to care deeply about outcomes while simultaneously being okay if they don't happen. This paradox is where the magic lives. When you release the grip, you often get what you wanted — because you're no longer operating from desperate, grasping energy.

State Six

Devotion

Doing hard things because they matter. Discipline that creates legacy. Focus that spans generations.

Devotion is discipline elevated to a sacred practice. It's doing hard things not because you have to, but because they matter. It's commitment that doesn't waver when things get difficult. There's a story I tell about Easy Eddie and Butch O'Hare — a father and son whose devotion to what mattered echoed through generations and literally changed history. When your work becomes worship and your commitment becomes unbreakable, you tap into a reservoir of energy that willpower alone can never access.

State Seven

Receiving

Opening to abundance. Accepting that you are worthy. Letting life pour into you.

Most people are terrible at receiving. They deflect compliments. They feel guilty about success. They unconsciously block what life wants to give them. Receiving is the practice of opening your cup and letting it fill. It's accepting that you are worthy of abundance, ease, help, love, and magic. This isn't passive — it's an active practice of allowing. And it's often the missing piece for people who work hard but somehow never seem to get what they deserve.

The Compound Effect

Here's what makes this framework so powerful:

Each state amplifies the others.

When you have Knowingness, Faith becomes easier — because you trust yourself.

When you have Faith, Non-Attachment becomes natural — because you're not gripping outcomes.

When you have Non-Attachment, Receiving opens up — because you're not blocking flow.

Together, they create a state of being that is genuinely unfuckwithable. Not as a concept. As a lived experience.

The transformation: When you operate from these seven states, you don't just set different goals — you become a different person. One who naturally makes better decisions, builds deeper relationships, recovers faster from setbacks, and moves through life with a quality of presence that others can feel.
