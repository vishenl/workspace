"I brought in an advisor named Sabrina Stocker. She's 29, was on The Apprentice UK, and she completely rewired how I think about platforms."

"She taught me that each platform is a different room — with different rules."

The Four Rooms

  • 1 YouTube = The Classroom — Educate and inspire. Coffee conversation energy. Talk straight to camera like we're having a chat.
  • 2 LinkedIn = The Boardroom — Lead with vulnerability. Here's the weird thing: text beats video. People are browsing at work.
  • 3 Instagram = The Cafe — Entertainment first. Camera to the side — like you're being interviewed on a talk show.
  • 4 Newsletter = The Sanctuary — You OWN this list. No algorithm can throttle you.

"On YouTube, you talk straight to camera — like we're having coffee. You're the teacher, they're the student."

"On LinkedIn, something weird happens: text beats video. Why? People are browsing at work. They can't watch a video with their boss walking by. So you write."

"'Just hit $100 million in revenue! Here's what I learned!' Those got like 47 likes. From my mom. And employees who were afraid of getting fired."

"And newsletter — this is your sanctuary. Unlike every other platform, you OWN this list. Instagram can throttle you. YouTube can demonetize you. But your email list? That's yours forever."

"Here's a pro tip: Record with two cameras at once. Front camera for YouTube. Side camera for Instagram."

4. PERSONAL BRAND OPERATING SYSTEM

9:30 - 13:00

"Have you ever typed a prompt into ChatGPT asking it to write a LinkedIn post for you?"

😄 HUMOR BEAT

And it comes back sounding like...

"...a motivational poster had a baby with a corporate memo? And you close the tab in disgust?"

Make a disgusted face. Mime closing a laptop aggressively.

"The problem isn't AI. The problem is AI doesn't know you yet."

"You need to build your Personal Brand Operating System first."

The 5-Step Personal Brand OS

  • 1 Your Story — Go for a coffee walk. Record your life story with Otter AI or voice memo.
  • 2 Online Identity — Feed AI your existing content so it knows your voice.
  • 3 Role Models — Who do you want to be like online? Your top 5-10.
  • 4 Boundaries — What do you NOT want to share?
  • 5 Vision & Goals — What are you actually trying to achieve?
Story: The Coffee Walk

"Step one: Go for a coffee walk. Use Otter AI or just the voice memo on your phone. Tell your life story. Childhood. What made you angry. What excited you. How you ended up here."

"The first time I did this, I walked for 45 minutes around my neighborhood. I talked about growing up in Malaysia. I talked about my dad's philosophy. I talked about the exact moment I decided to leave my job at Microsoft."

"By the end, I had forgotten I was recording."

"That's when the gold comes out — when you forget you're performing."

"Step four is boundaries. What do you NOT want to share?"

"For me, it's pictures of my kids. I don't share them. And I made that very clear to my AI."

😄 HUMOR BEAT

Now when it suggests content ideas, it never suggests 'Post a cute photo of your kids!'

"Because it knows I'll throw my laptop across the room."

Start serious, then exaggerate "throw my laptop" with comedic physical gesture.

This is the tactical section. Slow down. Make sure each step is clear. This is the stuff people will screenshot.

People buy from people they like. And people like you because of:

  • 2Authenticity
  • 3Humor
The fake name story is powerful. Don't rush it. Let the vulnerability land.

6. THE $2.5M NEWSLETTER + CONTENT FLYWHEEL

16:30 - 19:00
Story: The Newsletter Experiment

"In December, I started writing a weekly newsletter. Real stories. A dinner conversation with Paul McKenna. A photo my son took. Super personal stuff."

$2.5M additional revenue from 45 newsletters

"The year before I started: email revenue down 38%. After I started: email revenue up 44%."

"I did the math. I wrote 45 newsletters. We made an extra $2.5 million. That's $56,000 per newsletter."

"And here's the crazy part:"

"The newsletter doesn't sell anything."

"There's no pitch. No 'click here to buy.' It just builds trust. And when I eventually do offer something... they're ready."

An email subscriber is worth more than a follower. You OWN that list.

"Now, how do you turn one piece of content into a whole system?"

The Content Flywheel

  • 1 Write your newsletter (core content)
  • 2 Extract LinkedIn posts (each lesson = one post)
  • 3 Turn it into a video script with AI
  • 4 Record from two angles (front for YouTube, side for Instagram)
  • 5 Create clips for TikTok/Instagram Reels
  • 6 Send reflection email to continue the conversation
😄 HUMOR BEAT

Some people call this "repurposing." I call it...

"...working smarter while everyone else is burning out creating 47 pieces of original content a week."

Pause like you're searching for a profound word. Then deliver the long punchline with mock seriousness.

"Start with email. It's the least sexy. But it's the most valuable. Then let the flywheel do the rest."

The $56K per newsletter stat is the money shot. Let it land. Pause after you say it.
