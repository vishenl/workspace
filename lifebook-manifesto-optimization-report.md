# Lifebook 2026 Life Manifesto Landing Page
## Conversion Optimization Report

**Current URL:** https://www.mindvalley.com/lifebook/online/manifesto
**Analysis Date:** December 2024
**Prepared for:** Mindvalley Marketing Team

---

## Executive Summary

The current landing page has strong foundational elements but suffers from several conversion-killing issues:

1. **Confusing hero section** with mismatched imagery (Social Media Summit banner instead of Lifebook)
2. **Form buried too deep** - users must scroll extensively to register
3. **Abstract headline** that doesn't communicate clear benefits
4. **Generic testimonials** about Mindvalley, not specifically about Lifebook/this webinar
5. **Missing urgency elements** near the conversion point
6. **Overly long trainer bio** that loses reader attention

**Estimated conversion lift from implementing these changes: 25-40%**

### 3. PROBLEM AGITATION SECTION

#### Current State
Opening questions:
- "Are you tired of setting goals or new year resolutions and never achieving them?"
- "Do you know who you truly are and what you really want in every area of your life?"

#### Issues
- **Generic opening** - "Are you tired of..." is overused and triggers skepticism
- **Too philosophical too fast** - "who you truly are" is heavy for a cold lead
- **Weak "Why?" list** - reads like bullet points, not emotional triggers

#### Recommended Rewrite

```markdown
## Every January, You Set New Goals.
## By February, You've Already Forgotten Them.

It's not because you lack discipline.

It's because you were never clear on what you *actually* wanted in the first place.

**Here's the uncomfortable truth:**

Most people set goals based on what they *think* they should want:
- The promotion (because that's what successful people do)
- The relationship (because you're "supposed" to be settled by now)
- The body (because Instagram said so)

But none of it was truly *yours*.

**That's why it never stuck.**

You weren't designing your life. You were following someone else's blueprint.

This changes now.
```
