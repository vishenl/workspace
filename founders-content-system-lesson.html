The Platform Selection Question

The number one question founders ask: "Which platform should I choose?"

Option 1: Which platform are you actually going to use consistently?
Option 2: Where is your ideal client hanging out?

Sabrina's recommendation: Option 1. Because most of us know what we should do - the question is whether we'll actually do it.

The Two-Camera Setup: Record from two angles simultaneously. Front-facing camera for YouTube (direct education), side camera for Instagram (observed conversation). One recording, two pieces of content.
PART 3

Vector Science & AI Engine Optimization

The game has changed. AI is now deciding who gets found. Here's how to position yourself.

What is Vector Science?

AI doesn't just search for keywords - it creates associations. Vector science is understanding how AI connects you to the words and concepts you want to be known for.

When someone asks AI "Who are the best speakers on personal growth?" - will your name appear? That depends on the vector associations you've built.

Exercise: Build Your Vector Map

  1. Column 1: What words do you want to be associated with? (Author, speaker, thought leader, podcast host, entrepreneur...)
  2. Column 2: What questions do you want to rank for? ("Best speakers on X", "How to Y", "Who invented Z")
  3. Column 3: Who currently ranks for these? These are your vector neighbors - you need to create content that places you in proximity to them.
.txt File

Add a TXT file to your website that AI algorithms can scrape. This is hidden code that helps AI understand who you are.

.bot File

This file speaks directly to the bots scraping your site. It's how you communicate your identity to AI systems.

The 6-12 Month Window: Whoever understands this process in the next 6-12 months will be exponentially above competitors who aren't doing it. It will be very hard to catch up.
PART 4

The Personal Brand Operating System

Before AI can help you create content, it needs to understand who you are. This 5-step system creates your Personal Brand OS.

1

Your Personal Story

Take yourself for a coffee walk. Use Otter AI or similar to record yourself telling your life story - childhood, teenage years, what made you angry, what excited you, how you started your company. Then ask AI: "What are the most interesting parts that create multiple story arcs?"

2

Your Online Identity

Gather the content you've already created - newsletters, YouTube videos, podcasts, LinkedIn posts. Feed this to AI so it understands your existing voice and style.

3

Your Role Models

Who are your top 5-10 role models online? Who has nailed it as a personal brand? This is unique to each person - maybe someone fast-forward and bold, or maybe a mom with a side hustle. Let AI learn from their patterns.

4

Your Boundaries

What do you NOT want to talk about? Family? Politics? Certain topics? Being clear about your "no" zones helps AI respect your boundaries. (Example: "I don't share pictures of my kids - I respect their privacy.")

5

Your Vision & Goals

What are your top 3 goals in the next 12 months? Maybe 50,000 email subscribers, posting 3x/week on LinkedIn, or specific business lead generation targets. This focuses AI on what matters.

Action Step: Build Your OS This Weekend

Complete all 5 steps and ask AI to create an Operating System document for your personal brand. Do the same for your company. Now every time you use AI for content, you feed it these two documents first - and your content will be 80% more consistent and on-brand.

PART 5

Platform-Specific Strategies

YouTube: The Classroom

The Daniel Priestly Model

Daniel has started $700 million in companies. His YouTube strategy? He simply gets in front of the camera and teaches. No fancy production. Just valuable education delivered like a coffee conversation.

Result: 84,000 views on videos with only 61K subscribers. That's exceptional engagement.

The Title is Everything: "The Rise of ChatGPT" got 4.2K views on YouTube. A better title would be: "The #1 Reason You'll Be Left Behind by AI in 2026." YouTube is about curiosity and hooks.

Turn Ads Into Education

The Iman Gadzhi Model: Create educational content that naturally leads to your offer. At the end of your teaching video, invite viewers to watch a 30-minute deep-dive video - which is actually your sales presentation.

Example: First video = "How to Sneak Into Premium Airport Lounges for Free" (content)
End CTA = "Want to know the best card for this? Watch this next video" (leads to offer)

LinkedIn: The Boardroom

Why Text Beats Video on LinkedIn

People browse LinkedIn while working. They can't always watch video. Text posts outperform video significantly on this platform.

The Vulnerability Formula: The biggest winners on LinkedIn right now aren't sharing their wins - they're sharing what went wrong and what they learned. People want your process, not your polish.

The Likability Principle (from Cialdini's "Influence")

People are influenced by you when they like you. And people like you because of:

  • Vulnerability - Share your struggles
  • Authenticity - Be genuinely you
  • Humor - Don't take yourself too seriously

LinkedIn Post Formula

  1. Share a real story from your experience (you provide the human element)
  2. Ask AI to help discover the lesson behind it
  3. Let AI suggest opening hooks and closing insights
  4. Keep it authentically messy - that's what resonates

Newsletter: The Sanctuary

Case Study: Vishen's Newsletter Results

In December, Vishen started a weekly newsletter with real stories - conversations with mentors, lessons learned, photos his son took. Super personal, super real.

The numbers:

  • Prior year: Email revenue down 38%
  • After starting newsletter: Email revenue up 44%
  • Additional revenue: $2.5 million
  • Revenue per newsletter: $56,000

The newsletter doesn't sell anything directly. It builds credibility and trust - so when offers come, people are ready to buy.

Newsletter vs. Quiz Funnel

Don't say "sign up for my newsletter" - what does the customer get from that?

Instead, say "Get your free mini report." Use a Typeform quiz. The first email is customized based on their answers - but now they're on your newsletter list.

Tools: Substack (open newsletter platform, helps with distribution) or Kit (more traditional, great recommendation features)

Why Email Matters Most: An email is way more valuable than a follower. Instagram and YouTube decide if your audience sees your posts through secret algorithms. Email is YOUR list. No one can throttle you.
PART 6

The Content Flywheel

One piece of content. Multiple platforms. Maximum leverage.

📧 Newsletter
↓
💼 LinkedIn Articles
↓
🎬 YouTube Video
↓
✂️ Clips
↓
📱 TikTok / Instagram
↓
💬 Reflection Email
↺

How It Works

  1. Write your newsletter - This is your core content
  2. Extract LinkedIn posts - Each lesson becomes a standalone post
  3. Turn it into a video script - Use AI to convert, then read from teleprompter
  4. Record from two angles - Front for YouTube, side for Instagram
  5. Create clips - Short segments for TikTok and Instagram Reels
  6. Send reflection email - Share comments, link to video, continue the conversation
Teleprompter App

Load your script, read naturally. You can also buy a physical teleprompter for more professional setup.

Higgs Field AI

Create a "Soul ID" with 20 photos of yourself. Generate any thumbnail image you need - you in a jellybean jacket, Star Trek uniform, anything.

Social Media Team GPT

Feed it your newsletter. Ask: "Turn this into an Instagram reel script, YouTube video script, and two LinkedIn articles."

Canva

Create your YouTube thumbnails. Test two different images - YouTube lets you A/B test covers.
