Design Recommendations

● Add Duration Info

Users need to see time commitment upfront. Add "12 min" badge to all content cards and intro screens.

High Impact

● Visible Play CTA

The "Begin meditation" button should be more prominent on the intro screen. Consider full-width styling.

High Impact

● Progress Persistence

Show "Continue from 3:42" for returning users. Visual progress bars on cards increase completion rates.

Medium Impact

● Social Proof

Add "1.2k listened" and listener avatars to build trust and encourage engagement.

Medium Impact

● Touch Targets

Increase icon buttons from 30px to 44px minimum for better accessibility (iOS HIG standard).

Accessibility

● Streak System

Add "Day 3 streak!" badges to encourage daily usage and habit formation.
