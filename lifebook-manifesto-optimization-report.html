Executive Summary

The current landing page has strong foundational elements but suffers from several conversion-killing issues:

  1. 1 Confusing hero section with mismatched imagery (Social Media Summit banner instead of Lifebook)
  2. 2 Form buried too deep — users must scroll extensively to register
  3. 3 Abstract headline that doesn't communicate clear benefits
  4. 4 Generic testimonials about Mindvalley, not specifically about Lifebook/this webinar
  5. 5 Missing urgency elements near the conversion point
  6. 6 Overly long trainer bio that loses reader attention
Estimated Conversion Lift: 25-40%

2. Problem Agitation Section

Current Issues

  • Generic opening — "Are you tired of..." is overused and triggers skepticism
  • Too philosophical too fast — "who you truly are" is heavy for a cold lead
  • Weak "Why?" list — reads like bullet points, not emotional triggers

Recommended Rewrite

Every January, You Set New Goals. By February, You've Already Forgotten Them.

It's not because you lack discipline. It's because you were never clear on what you actually wanted in the first place.

## Here's the uncomfortable truth: Most people set goals based on what they *think* they should want: - The promotion (because that's what successful people do) - The relationship (because you're "supposed" to be settled by now) - The body (because Instagram said so) But none of it was truly *yours*. **That's why it never stuck.** You weren't designing your life. You were following someone else's blueprint.

3. Trainer Bio Section

Problem: Current bio is ~300 words. Nobody reads this. It's an authority dump that loses attention.

Recommended Rewrite (Max 80 words)

Current (~300 words)

Vishen is the founder of Mindvalley: the world's largest personal growth company, with over 20 million students worldwide and growing fast. A bestselling international author and speaker, he is regarded as one of the foremost experts in the field of human development. Vishen and his team of 250 education innovators at Mindvalley have created many of the world's foremost brands... (continues for 4 more paragraphs)

Recommended (~80 words)

Vishen is the founder of Mindvalley, the world's largest personal growth platform with 20+ million students. A New York Times bestselling author, his mind-training techniques are used by Fortune 500 executives and professional athletes from the NBA, NFL, and US Open Tennis.

He's spent 25+ years studying what helps humans reach their fullest potential.

Keep These Credentials (as bullets):

  • Founder of Mindvalley (20M+ students)
  • NYT Bestselling Author: The Code of the Extraordinary Mind
  • Creator of the 6 Phase Meditation (used by pro athletes worldwide)
  • Featured in Forbes, CNN, BBC, The New York Times
