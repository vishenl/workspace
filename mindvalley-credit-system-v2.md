# PART 1: CREDIT SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

## 1.1 The Spark Currency

### Why "Sparks"
- Evokes transformation, energy, and the beginning of change
- Aligns with Mindvalley's mission of igniting human potential
- Short, memorable, works globally
- Action-oriented: "Spark your transformation"

### Base Conversion
```
100 Sparks = ~$10 USD value
1,000 Sparks = ~$100 USD value
```

This ratio creates "large numbers" that feel abundant while keeping mental math simple.

### Direct Spark Purchases

| Package | Sparks | Price | Per Spark | Savings |

| Starter | 500 | $55 | $0.110 | — |

**Psychology Applied:**
- **Anchoring:** Show largest pack first to anchor value perception
- **Bundle bias:** Larger packs offer progressively better value
- **Round numbers:** Spark amounts are round for easy mental accounting

### Subscription Credit Drips

Members receive monthly Spark allocations based on their tier (see Section 3).

**Monthly vs. Annual:**
- **Monthly subscribers:** Receive Sparks on billing date each month
- **Annual subscribers:** Receive 2 months' bonus Sparks upfront + monthly drip

Example (Seeker tier):
- Monthly: 1,200 Sparks/month × 12 = 14,400 Sparks/year
- Annual: 14,400 + 2,400 bonus = **16,800 Sparks/year** (17% more)

## 1.5 Breakage Strategy

**Breakage** = Sparks that expire without being redeemed. This is a profit center.

### Expiration Policy

| Spark Source | Expiration |
|--------------|------------|
| Subscription Sparks | 6 months from issue |
| Purchased Sparks | 12 months from purchase |
| Earned Sparks | 6 months from earning |
| Bonus/Promo Sparks | 90 days from issue |
| Referral Sparks | 6 months from issue |

### Rollover Policy

- Members can roll over up to **2 months' worth** of subscription Sparks
- Example: Seeker (1,200/mo) can accumulate max 3,600 Sparks before oldest expire
- Rollover only applies to **active subscribers**
- Cancellation forfeits all Sparks at end of billing period

### Target Breakage Rate: 18-22%

**Why this range:**
- Too low (<15%): Members extract too much value, poor unit economics
- Too high (>30%): Members feel cheated, damages trust
- Sweet spot: Enough urgency to drive engagement, not punitive

### Breakage Mitigation UX

To prevent frustration while maintaining breakage:

1. **30-day warning email:** "You have 1,500 Sparks expiring soon"
2. **14-day push notification:** "Don't lose your Sparks!"
3. **7-day in-app banner:** Persistent reminder with quick-use suggestions
4. **Expiration day:** Final notification with one-click options

# PART 2: CREDIT PRICING MODEL

## 2.1 Pricing Philosophy

### Core Principles

1. **Value Anchoring:** Show "retail value" vs. Spark cost to emphasize savings
2. **Psychological Decoupling:** Sparks feel like "play money" — easier to spend
3. **Accessibility Ladder:** Price premium products to be achievable through engagement
4. **Demand-Based Variation:** Popular items cost more Sparks (like ClassPass)

### Conversion Baseline

- **100 Sparks = $10 retail value**
- But members acquire Sparks at ~$8-9 effective cost through subscriptions
- This creates **10-20% perceived savings** on every redemption

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |

| **Competitor copy** | Low | Low | First-mover advantage; deep integration; switching costs |

**For current members:**
- Auto-convert to equivalent tier based on current subscription
- Award "Founding Member" badge (permanent status symbol)
- 3,000 bonus Sparks for transition
- 6-month price lock guarantee
- Opt-in migration (not forced)

# PART 6: INDUSTRY INSPIRATION

## 6.1 ClassPass Analysis

### What Works

| Element | How It Works | Mindvalley Application |
|---------|--------------|------------------------|
| **Dynamic pricing** | Popular classes cost more credits | Popular programs/events cost more Sparks |
| **Limited rollover** | Max 2 months accumulation | Same — prevents hoarding |
| **Tier efficiency** | Higher tiers = better $/credit | Higher tiers = better $/Spark + multipliers |
| **Usage limits** | Max visits per studio/month | Not needed (content is unlimited) |
| **Instant upgrade** | Upgrade takes effect immediately | Same — no waiting for next cycle |

### What Doesn't Work for Mindvalley

| Element | Why It Doesn't Apply |
|---------|---------------------|
| Studio payouts (50-70%) | Mindvalley owns content — near-zero marginal cost |
| Geographic pricing | Digital content is location-agnostic |
| Class capacity limits | Content scales infinitely |

---

## 6.2 Audible Analysis

### What Works

| Element | How It Works | Mindvalley Application |
|---------|--------------|------------------------|
| **1 credit = 1 book** | Flat pricing regardless of book cost | Consider "1 Spark Pack = 1 Program" bundles |
| **Cost depreciation** | Credits paid months ago feel "free" | Monthly drip creates same effect |
| **Annual bonus** | Annual plans get extra credits upfront | Same — 2 months bonus |
| **Pause option** | Pause up to 90 days, keep credits | Same — reduces hard cancellations |
| **Plus catalog** | Included content beyond credits | Meditation Library as included content |

### Key Insight: Flat-Rate Bias

Audible's genius: Variable-priced books ($10-50) all cost 1 credit.
Members feel they're getting deals on expensive books.

**Mindvalley Application:**
- Consider "Program Passes" — 1 pass = any standard program
- Sold in packs: 3 passes for 5,000 Sparks (vs. 6,000 individual)
- Creates perceived value on premium programs

---

## 6.3 Airline Miles Analysis

### What Works

| Element | How It Works | Mindvalley Application |
|---------|--------------|------------------------|
| **Status tiers** | Elite status creates identity | Tier badges, exclusive benefits |
| **Partner earning** | Earn from credit cards, hotels, shopping | Earn from referrals, completions, challenges |
| **Aspirational redemptions** | Dream of business class keeps members engaged | Dream of Mastery/A-Fest keeps members earning |
| **Status runs** | Members spend irrationally to maintain status | Tier perks worth more than cost difference |
| **Breakage profit** | ~15-20% of miles never redeemed | Target 18-22% breakage |

### What to Avoid

| Element | Why to Avoid |
|---------|--------------|
| Aggressive devaluation | Erodes trust, creates backlash |
| Complex rules | Confusion leads to disengagement |
| Dynamic award pricing (opaque) | Unpredictability frustrates members |

| **Daily login rewards** | Creates daily habit | Daily meditation = daily Sparks |

## 6.6 Psychological Principles Applied

### Token Decoupling

**Principle:** Credits create psychological distance from real money.

**Research:** People pay 113% more when using credit vs. cash (Prelec & Simester).

**Application:**
- Never show Spark-to-dollar conversion in redemption flow
- Show Spark costs prominently, cash alternative smaller
- "You have 12,450 Sparks" feels like abundance

### Anchoring

**Principle:** First number seen influences all subsequent judgments.

**Application:**
- Always show "Retail Value: $X" above Spark price
- Show largest Spark pack first in top-up screens
- Display savings percentage prominently

### Loyalty Tier Psychology

**Principle:** Status creates identity and switching costs.

**Application:**
- Tier names evoke progression (Explorer → Luminary)
- Visible badges on community profiles
- Exclusive experiences for higher tiers
- "You're a Transformer" becomes identity

### Expiry Urgency

**Principle:** Loss aversion (2x stronger than equivalent gain).

**Application:**
- Clear expiration warnings at 30/14/7 days
- "Don't lose your Sparks" messaging
- Easy one-click redemption options
- Pause option reduces hard cancellation

### Breakage Economics

**Principle:** Unredeemed credits = pure profit.

**Application:**
- 18-22% target breakage
- Rollover limits prevent infinite accumulation
- Expiration drives usage OR profit
- Balance: Too high = customer frustration; too low = poor economics

### Sunk Cost Engagement

**Principle:** Prior investment increases commitment.

**Application:**
- Monthly Spark deposits create fresh "investment"
- Progress toward Mastery shows accumulated value
- "You're 65% of the way" harder to abandon than "You have $2,000 in credits"
- Cancellation warning shows Spark loss

### Progression Loops

**Principle:** Clear progress toward goals increases engagement.

**Application:**
- Mastery Goal Tracker always visible
- Milestone celebrations with bonus Sparks
- Streak counters for meditation
- Level-up moments when reaching new tiers

1. **Spark ownership:** Sparks are not property, no cash value

5. **Partner liability:** Mindvalley not responsible for partner fulfillment

1. **Deferred revenue:** Recognize Spark revenue when redeemed

1. **California requirements:** Prepaid card disclosure rules
